Dose-finding, experimental medicine evaluation of sodium valproate for the prevention of post-cardiac surgery myocardial injury

Roman M; Beasley N; Ladak S; Solomon C; Laio W; Lai FY; Joel-David L; Aujla H; Condorelli G; Woźniak MJ; Codd V; Webb T; Brookes C; Mulla H; McCann GP; Murphy GJ
medRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-02 · 全文(europepmc) · 原文

该文为丙戊酸钠预防心脏手术后心肌损伤的剂量探索临床试验，涉及染色质可及性测序但未聚焦三维基因组/染色质组织建模，且无AI或分子模拟方法，与课题无关。

摘要

<h4>Background</h4> A dose-finding trial evaluated safety and adherence for pre-cardiac surgery administration of sodium valproate. Integrated multi-omics analyses of myocardium were used to characterise mechanisms underlying treatment effects. <h4>Methods</h4> Adults undergoing cardiac surgery were randomised 1:1:1:1 with concealed allocation to no treatment (Controls), sodium valproate 15mg/kg/day for 1-2 weeks, 15mg/kg/day for 4-6 weeks, or 25mg/kg/day for 4-6 weeks pre-surgery. The primary analysis evaluated adherence and toxicity. Myocardial injury was defined by high sensitivity serum troponin at 24 hours post-surgery. Single-nucleus Assay for Transposase-Accessible Chromatin with sequencing (snATACseq) and single nuclei RNA sequencing (snRNAseq) of myocardial biopsies collected at surgery assessed treatment effects on chromatin accessibility and gene expression. Candidate mechanisms were validated in in vitro . <h4>Results</h4> The analysis cohort included 42 participants enrolled between January 2020 and August 2024. Sodium valproate 15mg/kg/day for 1-2 weeks had the highest levels of complete treatment adherence (70%), with 20% experiencing moderate/severe drug related adverse effects. Non-compliance was increased with longer and higher dosing. An as-treated analyses demonstrated reductions in troponin release in participants receiving Valproate≤14 days where myocardial biopsies demonstrated activation of hormetic p53 and Akt-GSK-3β ferroptosis protection pathways versus controls. Silencing of these pathways attenuated the protective effect of valproate in vitro . Treatment effects were not attributable to chromatin accessibility. Treatment >14 days resulted in a heart failure phenotype, suppression of ferroptosis protection pathways, and increased myocardial injury. <h4>Conclusions</h4> Valproate 15mg/kg/day for ≤14 days pre-surgery is well tolerated in adults awaiting cardiac surgery and is associated with upregulation of ferroptosis protection pathways and reductions in myocardial injury.

摘要翻译

<h4>背景</h4> 一项剂量探索试验评估了心脏手术前给予丙戊酸钠的安全性与依从性。通过心肌组织的整合多组学分析，表征了治疗效应背后的机制。<h4>方法</h4> 接受心脏手术的成人受试者以1:1:1:1的比例随机分配至隐蔽分组：术前不接受治疗（对照组）、术前1-2周给予丙戊酸钠15mg/kg/天、术前4-6周给予15mg/kg/天、或术前4-6周给予25mg/kg/天。主要分析评估了依从性与毒性。心肌损伤定义为术后24小时高敏血清肌钙蛋白水平。通过手术时采集的心肌活检样本进行单核转座酶可及染色质测序（snATACseq）和单核RNA测序（snRNAseq），评估治疗对染色质可及性与基因表达的影响。候选机制在体外实验中得到验证。<h4>结果</h4> 分析队列包括2020年1月至2024年8月期间入组的42名受试者。丙戊酸钠15mg/kg/天治疗1-2周组具有最高的完全治疗依从性（70%），其中20%出现中度/重度药物相关不良反应。延长治疗时间或增加剂量均导致不依从性增加。按实际治疗分析显示，接受丙戊酸钠≤14天的受试者肌钙蛋白释放减少，其心肌活检相较于对照组表现出毒物兴奋效应p53与Akt-GSK-3β铁死亡保护通路的激活。在体外实验中，沉默这些通路减弱了丙戊酸钠的保护效应。治疗效应与染色质可及性无关。治疗>14天导致心力衰竭表型、铁死亡保护通路抑制及心肌损伤增加。<h4>结论</h4> 术前给予丙戊酸钠15mg/kg/天≤14天在等待心脏手术的成人中耐受性良好，并与铁死亡保护通路上调及心肌损伤减少相关。
