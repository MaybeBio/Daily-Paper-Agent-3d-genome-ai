Functional fine-mapping of the 9p21.3 cardiovascular disease locus implicates the lncRNA MIR31HG as an effector transcript in endothelial cells

Zhigulev A; Poujade F; Gryzunov N; Buyan A; Li H; Bergman O; Liu M; Pradhananga S; Franco-Cereceda A; Swoboda P; Kulakovskiy IV; Eriksson P; Sahlén P; Björck HM
medRxiv 预印本 · 2026-10-19 · 全文(europepmc) · 原文

该文用启动子捕获Hi-C和AlphaGenome等工具定位9p21.3心血管病位点的长程染色质互作与增强子调控，涉及染色质空间组织但以实验为主、AI/模拟方法仅作辅助，建模深度有限。

摘要

<h4>BACKGROUND</h4> The chromosome 9p21.3 locus is among the strongest genetic risk loci for cardiovascular disease (CVD), yet the regulatory mechanisms underlying this association remain poorly understood. Previous vascular disease studies focused predominantly on the role of this locus in smooth muscle cell (SMC) dysfunction, whereas its potential role in endothelial cells (ECs) remains less well characterized. Here, we sought to identify candidate regulatory variants at 9p21.3, their distal target genes, and effector transcripts that contribute to CVD-related phenotypes in ECs. <h4>METHODS</h4> Promoter capture Hi-C was used to map chromatin interactions at 9p21.3 in primary human ascending aortic SMCs (n=6), supplementing the previously obtained ECs (n=16) dataset. To support the regulatory relevance of these interactions, expression quantitative trait locus analysis was performed in 136 vascular biopsies. Variant effects were prioritized in silico using AlphaGenome. The regulatory activity of the prioritized region was validated using CRISPR activation. <h4>RESULTS</h4> Chromatin interaction mapping identified a complex regulatory architecture at the 9p21.3 locus involving 19 putative regulatory elements and 15 genes across ECs and SMCs. MIR31HG showed the most recurrent distal promoter connectivity with the 9p21.3 region. Consistent with the chromatin interaction map, expression of MIR31HG transcripts was associated with genetic variation at the 9p21.3 locus. In ECs, the lncRNA MIR31HG, but not miR-31, was responsive to hemodynamic flow. Depletion of MIR31HG in ECs was associated with endothelial activation. Epigenetic tracks and deep learning predictions highlighted an active enhancer element at 9p21.3, which was subsequently validated by CRISPR activation and increased MIR31HG expression, confirming its capacity to regulate MIR31HG over a large genomic distance of approximately 538 kb. <h4>CONCLUSIONS</h4> Altogether, these data implicate lncRNA MIR31HG as a likely effector of the 9p21.3 CVD locus, linking the cis-regulatory element and heritable genetic variation in the respective region to endothelial activation through long-range enhancer regulation. <h4>Clinical Perspective</h4> What Is New? Promoter capture Hi-C in primary human vascular cells identified recurrent long-range interactions between the 9p21.3 cardiovascular disease locus and the MIR31HG promoter, extending the regulatory landscape beyond the nearest genes. Expression of MIR31HG transcripts was associated with 9p21.3 genetic variation in vascular tissue; the lncRNA MIR31HG was responsive to hemodynamic flow in endothelial cells and influenced the endothelial activation transcriptional programs upon depletion. Integrated sequence-based, epigenomic, genetic, and CRISPR activation analyses nominated the 9p21.3 enhancer as a regulator of MIR31HG in endothelial cells. What Are the Clinical Implications? The findings suggest that the cardiovascular disease associations of 9p21.3 may involve stronger than previously anticipated endothelial regulatory mechanisms in addition to characterized effects in other vascular cell types. lncRNA MIR31HG provides a molecular link between noncoding genetic variation at 9p21.3 and endothelial cell-state regulation relevant to vascular disease.

摘要翻译

<h4>背景</h4> 染色体9p21.3位点是心血管疾病（CVD）最强的遗传风险位点之一，然而该关联背后的调控机制仍知之甚少。以往的血管疾病研究主要集中在该位点在平滑肌细胞（SMC）功能障碍中的作用，而其在内皮细胞（ECs）中的潜在作用仍未得到充分表征。在此，我们旨在识别9p21.3处的候选调控变异、其远端靶基因以及在内皮细胞中促成CVD相关表型的效应转录本。<h4>方法</h4> 使用启动子捕获Hi-C技术绘制原代人升主动脉SMCs（n=6）中9p21.3处的染色质相互作用，补充先前获得的ECs（n=16）数据集。为支持这些相互作用的调控相关性，在136例血管活检中进行了表达数量性状位点分析。使用AlphaGenome在计算机上优先排序变异效应。通过CRISPR激活验证了优先排序区域的调控活性。<h4>结果</h4> 染色质相互作用作图识别出9p21.3位点处涉及19个推定调控元件和15个基因（在ECs和SMCs中）的复杂调控架构。MIR31HG显示出与9p21.3区域最频繁的远端启动子连接。与染色质相互作用图一致，MIR31HG转录本的表达与9p21.3位点的遗传变异相关。在内皮细胞中，lncRNA MIR31HG（而非miR-31）对血流动力学流动有响应。内皮细胞中MIR31HG的耗竭与内皮激活相关。表观遗传学轨道和深度学习预测突出了9p21.3处的一个活性增强子元件，随后通过CRISPR激活验证并增加了MIR31HG表达，确认其在大约538 kb的大基因组距离上调控MIR31HG的能力。<h4>结论</h4> 总体而言，这些数据表明lncRNA MIR31HG可能是9p21.3 CVD位点的效应因子，通过长距离增强子调控将相应区域中的顺式调控元件和可遗传遗传变异与内皮激活联系起来。<h4>临床视角</h4> 有何新发现？在原代人血管细胞中的启动子捕获Hi-C识别出9p21.3心血管疾病位点与MIR31HG启动子之间的频繁长距离相互作用，将调控景观扩展到最近基因之外。MIR31HG转录本的表达与血管组织中9p21.3遗传变异相关；lncRNA MIR31HG对内皮细胞中的血流动力学流动有响应，并在耗竭时影响内皮激活转录程序。整合的基于序列、表观基因组、遗传和CRISPR激活分析提名9p21.3增强子为内皮细胞中MIR31HG的调控因子。临床意义是什么？研究结果表明，9p21.3的心血管疾病关联可能涉及比以前预期更强的内皮调控机制，此外还有其他血管细胞类型中已表征的效应。lncRNA MIR31HG提供了9p21.3非编码遗传变异与内皮细胞状态调控之间的分子联系，这与血管疾病相关。

本文通过 promoter capture Hi-C 在主动脉内皮细胞和平滑肌细胞中绘制 9p21.3 位点的启动子-增强子互作图，结合 eQTL、AlphaGenome 变异效应预测和 CRISPR 激活实验，证明 lncRNA MIR31HG 是 9p21.3 心血管疾病风险位点在内皮细胞中的远端效应转录本，受约 538 kb 外的增强子调控。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：9p21.3 是心血管疾病最强的遗传风险位点之一，但该位点内的非编码变异通过何种细胞类型、何种调控机制、作用于哪个远端效应基因来影响疾病风险？
  • 为什么重要：9p21.3 的关联信号位于非编码区，最近邻基因（ANRIL、CDKN2A/B）的机制研究数十年来未形成统一结论。确定真正的效应转录本和调控元件是理解该位点致病机制的关键，也是将 GWAS 关联转化为可干预靶点的前提。
  • 现有方法为何不足：既往研究主要聚焦于平滑肌细胞和最近邻基因 ANRIL，缺乏对远端染色质互作的全景分析；单细胞类型、单基因假设可能遗漏真正的效应细胞和效应转录本。
  • 精确研究问题：Can promoter capture Hi-C in primary human vascular cells identify distal effector transcripts of the 9p21.3 CVD locus, and can the regulatory mechanism be functionally validated?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法： - 对 6 例原代人升主动脉平滑肌细胞进行 promoter capture Hi-C（HiCap），与既往 16 例内皮细胞数据整合 - 绘制 9p21.3 位点启动子-增强子互作网络（19 个推定调控元件、15 个基因） - 在 136 例血管活检组织中进行 eQTL 分析 - 用 AlphaGenome 预测候选变异对染色质活性的影响 - 用 CRISPRa 验证候选增强子对 MIR31HG 的远程调控

2) 核心洞察： - 9p21.3 的调控网络远超最近邻基因：MIR31HG 是跨内皮细胞和平滑肌细胞最频繁互作的远端启动子 - 内皮细胞中 MIR31HG（而非 miR-31）对血流剪切力响应，且其敲低诱导内皮激活转录程序——提示该 lncRNA 是维持内皮静息态的关键因子 - 风险等位基因通过增强子活性变化上调 MIR31HG，而 MIR31HG 的功能丧失促进内皮激活——提示风险机制可能是 MIR31HG 表达失调而非简单缺失

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]： - 非编码风险位点的效应基因不能由「最近邻」原则推断，三维基因组互作数据应作为 GWAS 解读的常规工具 - 同一基因座可产生 lncRNA 和 miRNA 双重产物，功能研究必须区分各转录本而非笼统归因 - 多层级证据（染色质互作 + 遗传关联 + 序列预测 + 功能扰动）的收敛比任何单一证据更有说服力

输入： - 原代人升主动脉平滑肌细胞（n=6，新产生） - 原代人升主动脉内皮细胞（n=16，既往已发表数据） - 136 例血管活检组织（49 例主动脉内膜-中膜、43 例主动脉外膜、44 例胸廓内动脉内膜-中膜）用于 eQTL - EA.hy926 内皮细胞系（用于 flow 实验、siRNA 敲低、CRISPRa） - hTERT-RPE1 细胞（用于 CRISPRa）

09 关键公式符号

本文为实验生物学论文，核心分析为统计与生信流程，未提供形式化数学模型。以下列出隐含的统计方法：

1) Hi-C 互作显著性（HiCapTools 内部实现，具体公式未在文中给出）： - 基于负二项分布或泊松分布的互作计数模型 - 多重检验校正：Bonferroni 校正（文中明确提到 adjusted P < 0.05）

2) eQTL 线性回归： - 模型：Expression ~ Genotype dosage - 即：Y = β₀ + β₁·G + ε - 其中 Y 为基因表达量，G 为 SNP 等位基因剂量（0/1/2），β₁ 为等位基因效应 - 多重检验校正：Benjamini-Hochberg FDR

3) 差异表达分析（edgeR）： - 基于负二项分布模型的基因表达计数 - 阈值：|logFC| > 0.58（即 FC > 1.5），FDR < 0.05

4) AlphaChip/AlphaGenome 变异效应： - 文中未给出具体公式，为深度学习模型输出 - 变异效应 = 预测活性（alternative allele）− 预测活性（reference allele）

5) MARA 转录因子活性： - 基于 motif 富集与基因表达变化的贝叶斯推断 - 输出为 z-score（如 E2F z-score = −5.4）

11 结论正确解读

任务范围：本文仅针对 9p21.3 位点在主动脉内皮细胞和平滑肌细胞中的调控机制，不涉及该位点在其他细胞类型（如巨噬细胞、心肌细胞）或疾病阶段的作用。

oracle/真值输入：eQTL 分析使用了 136 例血管活检组织的基因型-表达配对数据；AlphaGenome 预测基于序列和表观特征，非实验测量。

端到端状态：从染色质互作 → 遗传关联 → 序列预测 → 功能验证的完整链条已闭合，但存在以下边界： - CRISPRa 验证的是增强子的「能力」（sufficiency），而非「必要性」（necessity）——未做增强子敲除实验 - 未验证 rs1537373 点突变对增强子活性的直接影响（仅预测） - MIR31HG 敲低实验在 EA.26 细胞系中进行，未在原代内皮细胞中重复 - 未进行动物模型或体内验证

算力/成本：未提供训练 AlphaGenome 的算力成本；Hi-C 实验需要约 10⁶ 细胞/样本，限制了在稀有细胞类型中的应用。

历史数据依赖：eQTL 分析依赖已发表的基因型-表达数据集；Hi-C 数据部分来自既往发表的内皮细胞数据集。

模型依赖：AlphaGenome 预测结果依赖训练数据的细胞类型覆盖度和模型架构；MARA 依赖 motif 数据库完整性。

最难情形：9p21.3 区域高连锁不平衡（LD）使得 fine-mapping 难以区分因果变异；Hi-C 互作在群体水平存在个体差异；MIR31HG 的核/质定位可能决定其功能差异。

群体/领域边界：研究对象为北欧裔（瑞典）人群，eQTL 结果可能不适用于其他种族；细胞均来自升主动脉扩张患者，可能不代表正常血管状态。

有边界的复述：在升主动脉扩张患者的原代血管细胞中，9p21.3 位点通过约 538 kb 的远程染色质互作调控 MIR31HG 表达；MIR31HG 敲低促进内皮激活转录程序；rs1537373 是候选因果变异，但需进一步功能验证。

1) 可迁移概念：多层级证据收敛策略 - 本文展示了从三维互作 → 遗传关联 → 序列预测 → 功能验证的完整证据链设计 - 迁移到三维基因组课题：任何单一数据类型的结论都需至少两个独立证据层级的支持

2) 可迁移方法：promoter capture Hi-C 的细胞类型比较设计 - 同一 locus 在 EC 和 SMC 中的互作差异可用于识别细胞类型特异的调控机制 - 迁移：在比较不同细胞类型/状态的 3D 基因组时，应同时考虑互作强度与互作频率的细胞类型特异性

3) 可迁移方法：eQTL 与 Hi-C 互作的整合分析 - 将互作数据作为「候选靶基因」的筛选器，再用 eQTL 提供遗传学证据 - 迁移：3D 基因组数据可作为 GWAS 位点功能注释的常规工具

4) 可迁移概念：lncRNA 与 miRNA 共享启动子的功能分化 - MIR31HG 和 miR-31 共享启动子但功能独立——同一基因座可产生多个功能不同的转录本 - 迁移：在 3D 基因组研究中，启动子-增强子互作可能同时调控多个转录本，功能验证需区分

5) 可迁移方法：CRISPRa 验证远程增强子 - 用 dCas9-VP192 激活候选增强子，检测远端基因表达变化——直接验证增强子-启动子功能连接 - 迁移：3D 基因组预测的增强子-启动子对可用 CRISPRa/i 进行功能验证

6) 可迁移概念：flow 响应作为内皮功能筛选 - 血流剪切力响应是内皮细胞功能的重要指标，可用于筛选疾病相关转录本 - 迁移：在 3D 基因组研究中，可将环境刺激（flow、炎症因子）作为功能筛选条件

7) 可迁移方法：AlphaChip/AlphaGenome 变异效应预测 - 深度学习模型预测非编码变异对染色质活性的影响，与 eQTL 和实验数据整合 - 迁移：3D 基因组课题中可用此类模型优先化候选因果变异

8) 可迁移概念：亚细胞定位决定 lncRNA 功能 - MIR31HG 的核/质定位可能决定其功能机制（染色质调控 vs 细胞质信号） - 迁移：lncRNA 功能研究必须包含亚细胞定位分析

15 与已有知识连接

1) 9p21.3 位点与 ANRIL 研究 - 本文直接挑战既往以 ANRIL 为中心的 9p21.3 研究范式（引文 3-11） - 与 McPherson et al. (2007)、Samani et al. (2007) 等 GWAS 原始发现连接 - 与 Holdt et al. 的 ANRIL 功能研究形成对比——本文提出 ANRIL 互作有限，MIR31HG 是更可能的效应转录本

5) lncRNA 功能研究 - MIR31HG 在癌症中的研究（如 miR-31 宿主基因）与本文血管功能形成跨领域连接 - 与 lncRNA 亚细胞定位决定功能机制的一般性认识一致

Reviewer

Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness / Statistical power
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that promoter capture Hi-C identified MIR31HG as the most recurrent distal target of the 9p21.3 locus, based on data from 6 SMC and 16 EC samples.
  • Evidence pointer Results section "Chromatin interaction mapping identifies MIR31HG as a target gene of the 9p21.3 locus", Figure 1A-F, Table 1.
  • Concern The sample sizes for the Hi-C experiments are very small (n=6 for SMCs, n=16 for ECs). The authors do not report on the reproducibility of the interaction calls across biological replicates, nor do they provide any statistical measure of confidence for the interaction frequencies. The claim that MIR31HG is the "most recurrent" target is based on a qualitative assessment of interaction frequency, which may be highly variable given the small n. Furthermore, the authors do not validate the key long-range interaction (9p21.3-MIR31HG) by an independent method (e.g., 3C-qPCR or FISH) in the relevant cell types.
  • Why it matters The entire premise of the paper rests on the identification of MIR31HG as a target of the 9p21.3 locus. If the interaction calls are not robust or are not reproducible, the subsequent eQTL, functional, and genetic analyses lose their foundation. The lack of validation and statistical rigor undermines the confidence in this central claim.

  • Resolution test The authors should provide a statistical framework for identifying significant interactions (e.g., using a background model or comparing to a negative control set). They should also validate the 9p21.3-MIR31HG interaction in at least one of the primary cell types using an independent technique. Ideally, they would also show that the interaction is specific to ECs or SMCs and not a generic feature of all cell types.

  • Concern ID R1-M2

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Statistical power / Genetic evidence
  • Claim pointer The authors state that eQTL analysis in 136 vascular biopsies showed the strongest association between MIR31HG expression and genetic variation at 9p21.3, specifically in the internal thoracic artery intima-media (MMed).
  • Evidence pointer Results section "Chromatin interaction mapping identifies MIR31HG as a target gene of the 9p21.3 locus", Figure 1E, Table S1.
  • Concern The eQTL analysis is based on a small number of samples (n=49 for AMed, n=43 for AAdv, n=44 for MMed). The authors report that only the MMed association passed multiple testing correction (FDR < 0.05), while other associations were suggestive (P < 0.05, FDR > 0.05). This is a weak genetic signal. The effect size (beta = 0.76) is reported, but the confidence intervals are not provided. The authors do not discuss the possibility of a false positive, nor do they attempt to replicate this finding in an independent cohort. The lack of a robust, replicated eQTL signal significantly weakens the claim that MIR31HG is genetically regulated by the 9p21.3 locus.
  • Why it matters The eQTL analysis is the primary genetic evidence linking the 9p21.3 locus to MIR31HG expression. A weak, unreplicated signal is insufficient to establish this link, especially given the small sample size. Without a solid genetic association, the relevance of the subsequent functional experiments to human disease is uncertain.

  • Resolution test The authors should provide effect sizes with confidence intervals and report the number of independent SNPs tested. They should attempt to replicate the eQTL association in an independent dataset (e.g., GTEx or STARNET). If replication is not possible, they should temper their conclusions and clearly state that the genetic association is suggestive and requires further validation.

  • Concern ID R1-M3

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Functional validation / Physiological relevance
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that CRISPRa of a candidate enhancer located ~538 kb from the MIR31HG promoter significantly increased MIR31HG expression, confirming its capacity to regulate MIR31HG over a large genomic distance.
  • Evidence pointer Results section "9p21.3 locus harbors a distal enhancer and putative regulatory SNPs of MIR31HG", Figure 3A.
  • Concern The CRISPRa experiment was performed in hTERT-RPE1 cells, a non-vascular, non-endothelial cell line. The enhancer activity and the long-range interaction may be cell-type specific. The authors do not demonstrate that the same enhancer is active in primary ECs or that the CRISPRa effect is specific to the targeted region (e.g., no off-target controls or a negative control gRNA targeting a different region). The physiological relevance of an effect in an immortalized retinal pigment epithelial cell line for a cardiovascular phenotype is questionable.
  • Why it matters The CRISPRa experiment is the only direct functional evidence that the identified enhancer can regulate MIR31HG. If this effect is not reproducible in a relevant cell type (e.g., primary ECs) or is not specific, the claim that this enhancer is a functional regulator of MIR31HG in the cardiovascular context is not supported.

  • Resolution test The authors should repeat the CRISPRa experiment in primary human aortic endothelial cells. They should include a negative control (e.g., a non-targeting gRNA or a gRNA targeting a known inactive region) and demonstrate that the effect is specific to the MIR31HG locus. They should also consider using a more physiologically relevant readout, such as allelic expression analysis in cells heterozygous for the risk variant.

  • Concern ID R1-M4

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Causal variant identification
  • Claim pointer The authors nominate rs1537373 as a candidate causal variant based on AlphaGenome predictions and concordance with eQTL data.
  • Evidence pointer Results section "9p21.3 locus harbors a distal enhancer and putative regulatory SNPs of MIR31HG", Figure 3C, Table S8.
  • Concern The evidence for rs1537373 as a causal variant is entirely computational. AlphaGenome is a predictive model, and its predictions are not validated by any experimental assay. The concordance with eQTL data is based on a single, unreplicated eQTL analysis. The authors do not perform any fine-mapping analysis (e.g., using a Bayesian approach like SuSiE or FINEMAP) to statistically prioritize causal variants. The claim that this is a "high-confidence" prioritization is overstated given the lack of experimental validation.
  • Why it matters Identifying the causal variant is crucial for understanding the mechanism of the 9p21.3 association. If the nominated variant is not causal, the entire mechanistic model proposed in the paper may be incorrect. The current evidence is too weak to support a definitive claim.
  • Resolution test The authors should perform a formal statistical fine-mapping analysis of the 9p21.3 locus using the eQTL data. They should validate the effect of rs1537373 on enhancer activity using a reporter assay (e.g., luciferase or MPRA) in ECs. They should also test whether the variant affects transcription factor binding (e.g., using an electrophoretic mobility shift assay or ChIP-qPCR).

Risk / unsupported claims

  • The claim that MIR31HG is the "most recurrent" distal target of the 9p21.3 locus is unsupported without statistical analysis of the Hi-C data.
  • The claim that the eQTL association in MIR31HG is "stable" is unsupported given the small sample size and lack of replication.
  • The claim that the prioritized enhancer is "sufficient to upregulate MIR31HG" is based on a single CRISPRa experiment in a non-vascular cell line and is therefore not fully supported.
  • The nomination of rs1537373 as a "candidate causal variant" is based on computational predictions and a single, unreplicated eQTL analysis; this is a hypothesis, not a validated finding.
  • The statement that "several layers of evidence suggest the high confidence of the prioritization strategy" is an overstatement of the evidence presented.
  • The claim that MIR31HG depletion promotes an "endothelial activation transcriptional program" is based on RNA-seq data, but the functional consequences of this program (e.g., effects on endothelial barrier function, leukocyte adhesion) are not directly demonstrated.
