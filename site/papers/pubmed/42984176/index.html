Polyphosphate and Disordered Hfq Regions Drive DNA Compaction through Conformation-Dependent Interactions.

Navon, Yoav; Levy, Yaakov
Biophysical journal · 2026-11-05 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用粗粒化和全原子分子动力学模拟研究Hfq蛋白与多聚磷酸盐驱动DNA压缩的构象依赖机制，虽对象为细菌DNA而非真核染色质，但其环境响应性DNA空间架构建模思路对染色质组织计算建模有可迁移启发。

摘要

The RNA-binding protein Hfq and inorganic polyphosphate (polyP) were previously suggested to promote DNA compaction of AT-rich regions by forming heterochromatin-like structures, yet the underlying molecular mechanism remains unclear. Here, we combine coarse-grained and all-atom molecular dynamics simulations to investigate the interplay between Hfq, polyP, and DNA. Under physiological conditions, polyP electrostatically interacts with the distal faces of multiple Hfq hexamers, clustering them near DNA and leaving the proximal faces available to interact with the DNA minor groove via the disordered N-termini, thereby promoting compaction. While this mechanism shows limited dependence on DNA sequence, it is highly sensitive to environmental conditions. Under high-salt conditions, electrostatic interactions are screened, weakening polyP-Hfq association and limiting Hfq clustering, while stabilization of non-canonical DNA conformations exposes hydrophobic surfaces. These changes shift the mode of Hfq interaction with DNA toward its distal face and disordered C-termini, favoring hydrophobic interactions and reducing compaction. Together, our findings reveal a conformation-dependent switch in Hfq-DNA interaction modes governed by cellular conditions, providing a molecular framework for environment-responsive genome organization in bacteria.

摘要翻译

RNA结合蛋白Hfq和无机多聚磷酸盐（polyP）此前被认为通过形成类异染色质结构促进富含AT区域的DNA压缩，但其潜在分子机制仍不清楚。在此，我们结合粗粒化和全原子分子动力学模拟，研究Hfq、polyP与DNA之间的相互作用。在生理条件下，polyP通过静电作用与多个Hfq六聚体的远端面相互作用，使其在DNA附近聚集，同时保留近端面通过无序N端与DNA小沟相互作用，从而促进压缩。尽管该机制对DNA序列的依赖性有限，但对环境条件高度敏感。在高盐条件下，静电相互作用被屏蔽，削弱polyP-Hfq结合并限制Hfq聚集，同时非经典DNA构象的稳定化暴露疏水表面。这些变化将Hfq与DNA的相互作用模式转向其远端面和无序C端，有利于疏水相互作用并减少压缩。总之，我们的发现揭示了由细胞条件调控的Hfq-DNA相互作用模式中的构象依赖性开关，为细菌中环境响应的基因组组织提供了分子框架。

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法：使用粗粒化（coarse-grained）和全原子（all-atom）分子动力学模拟，构建 Hfq 六聚体、polyP 与 DNA 的三元体系，在生理条件和高盐条件下分别模拟，观察互作模式与 DNA 压实程度。

2) 核心洞察：polyP 通过静电作用结合 Hfq 六聚体的远端面（distal face），使多个 Hfq 六聚体在 DNA 附近聚集，同时保留近端面（proximal face）通过无序 N-端与 DNA 小沟互作，从而驱动压实。高盐条件下静电屏蔽削弱 polyP-Hfq 结合，同时非经典 DNA 构象（non-canonical conformations）暴露疏水表面，使 Hfq 转向通过远端面和 C-端与 DNA 疏水互作，压实减弱。这是一个「conformation-dependent interaction switch」——环境条件通过改变 DNA 构象和静电环境，切换 Hfq 的 DNA 结合模式。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：蛋白-DNA 互作模式并非固定，而是由环境条件（盐、pH、配体）通过改变 DNA 构象和蛋白表面可用性来动态切换。这一「条件依赖的互作模式切换」概念可迁移到真核染色质组织研究——例如，组蛋白修饰或染色质重塑因子可能通过类似机制在不同环境或修饰状态下切换与 DNA 的结合模式，从而调控染色质状态。

Agent 提炼的知识候选

  1. 条件依赖的互作模式切换框架：本文的核心概念——环境条件（盐浓度）通过改变 DNA 构象和静电环境，切换蛋白-DNA 结合模式（近端面/N-端 vs 远端面/C-端）——可直接迁移到真核三维基因组研究。例如，在研究染色质重塑因子或组蛋白变体如何响应代谢状态或离子环境时，可采用类似的双条件模拟设计（改变环境参数 + 观察结合模式切换）。

  2. polyP 作为多价阴离子介导蛋白聚集的机制：polyP 通过静电结合蛋白远端面促进多聚体聚集，这一「多价配体介导的蛋白簇集」概念可类比于真核中 RNA 或组蛋白修饰介导的相分离/簇集现象。在三维基因组 AI 模型中，可将 polyP 类似物（多价阴离子）作为特征纳入蛋白-DNA 互作预测。

The authors conclude that a conformation-dependent switch in Hfq-DNA interaction modes provides a molecular framework for environment-responsive genome organization in bacteria.

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a plausible and potentially interesting mechanistic hypothesis for Hfq-polyP-mediated DNA compaction, supported by a dual-resolution simulation approach. However, the evidence base available for review is limited to the abstract, which precludes verification of the central claims. The mechanistic narrative is coherent, but several assertions regarding molecular interactions and conformational switching require quantitative support that is not visible in the abstract. The work may be of interest to the bacterial chromatin and nucleic acid biophysics communities, but the case is not fully established from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers studying bacterial nucleoid organization, Hfq biology, polyphosphate physiology, and protein-nucleic acid interactions would find these results relevant. The proposed environment-responsive mechanism could inform studies on bacterial stress responses and genome architecture. Computational biophysicists interested in coarse-grained and all-atom simulation approaches for protein-DNA systems may also find the methodological combination of interest.
  • Major strengths The abstract addresses a significant open question regarding the molecular mechanism of Hfq-polyP-mediated DNA compaction. The combination of coarse-grained and all-atom simulations is appropriate for capturing both large-scale assembly and detailed interaction modes. The proposed dual-face model of Hfq engagement with DNA, with distinct roles for N-terminal and C-terminal disordered regions, is mechanistically appealing and testable. The emphasis on environmental sensitivity provides a biologically relevant dimension that extends beyond static structural descriptions.

  • Claim pointer The authors claim that polyP electrostatically interacts with the distal faces of multiple Hfq hexamers, clustering them near DNA and leaving proximal faces available to interact with the DNA minor groove via disordered N-termini, thereby promoting compaction.

Without quantitative evidence for this arrangement, the model remains speculative. The distinction between distal-face polyP binding and proximal-face DNA binding is critical, as it determines whether the proposed mechanism is physically plausible or merely one of many possible configurations.
