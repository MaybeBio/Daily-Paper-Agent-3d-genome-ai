Green Extraction and Molecular Docking-Guided Discovery of Asiaticoside as a Multi-Target Modulator of the SIRT1/HMGB1/p53-p21 Axis in H(2)O(2)-Induced Senescent Endothelial Cells.

Li, Wenkai; Huang, Jianhua; Zeng, Fei; Wang, Yuping; Liu, Xiong; Wang, Junjie; Liu, Siyu
Molecules (Basel, Switzerland) · 2026-10-16 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究积雪草苷对氧化应激诱导内皮细胞衰老的调控作用，涉及分子对接但对象为细胞衰老通路，与三维基因组/染色质组织建模无关。

摘要

Endothelial cell senescence is a key contributor to vascular ageing and cardiovascular diseases. Asiaticoside, a pentacyclic triterpenoid saponin from Centella asiatica, exhibits anti-inflammatory and antioxidant activities, yet its potential to modulate endothelial senescence remains unexplored. Herein, we report the molecular docking-guided discovery of asiaticoside as a multi-target modulator of the SIRT1/HMGB1/p53-p21 senescence regulatory axis. Asiaticoside was isolated via a green acid-hydrolysis strategy using dilute HCl as the extraction solvent, followed by polyamide enrichment and C(18) reversed-phase purification (purity > 98%). Molecular docking revealed that asiaticoside binds favourably to SIRT1 (-8.1 kcal mol(-1)), the HMGB1-DNA complex (-10.7 kcal mol(-1)), and several senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP)-related inflammatory cytokines. In H(2)O(2)-induced senescent human umbilical vein endothelial cells (HUVECs), asiaticoside at 100 muM significantly reduced senescence-associated beta-galactosidase activity and alleviated G0/G1 cell cycle arrest without cytotoxicity. Mechanistically, asiaticoside restored SIRT1 expression, suppressed the secretion of IL-1beta, IL-6, IL-8, TNF-alpha, and HMGB1, downregulated p21 at both mRNA and protein levels, reduced p53 protein expression, and suppressed p16 mRNA expression. These findings demonstrate that asiaticoside attenuates endothelial senescence and SASP-driven inflammation, an effect associated with restoration of SIRT1 expression and suppression of the HMGB1/p53-p21 pathway.

摘要翻译

内皮细胞衰老是血管老化与心血管疾病的关键诱因。积雪草苷（Asiaticoside）是从积雪草（Centella asiatica）中提取的一种五环三萜皂苷，具有抗炎和抗氧化活性，但其调节内皮细胞衰老的潜力尚未被探索。在此，我们报道了基于分子对接（molecular docking）引导的积雪草苷作为SIRT1/HMGB1/p53-p21衰老调控轴多靶点调节剂的发现。积雪草苷通过使用稀盐酸作为提取溶剂的绿色酸水解策略分离获得，随后经聚酰胺富集和C(18)反相纯化（纯度>98%）。分子对接显示，积雪草苷与SIRT1（-8.1 kcal mol(-1)）、HMGB1-DNA复合物（-10.7 kcal mol(-1)）以及多种衰老相关分泌表型（senescence-associated secretory phenotype, SASP）相关炎症细胞因子具有良好结合。在H(2)O(2)诱导衰老的人脐静脉内皮细胞（HUVECs）中，100 μM积雪草苷显著降低衰老相关β-半乳糖苷酶活性，并缓解G0/G1细胞周期阻滞，且无细胞毒性。机制上，积雪草苷恢复SIRT1表达，抑制IL-1β、IL-6、IL-8、TNF-α和HMGB1的分泌，在mRNA和蛋白水平下调p21，降低p53蛋白表达，并抑制p16 mRNA表达。这些发现表明，积雪草苷可减轻内皮细胞衰老及SASP驱动的炎症，其效应与恢复SIRT1表达和抑制HMGB1/p53-p21通路相关。
