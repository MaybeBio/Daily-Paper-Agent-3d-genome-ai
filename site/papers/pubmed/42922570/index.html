HiC-LEGO: Biologically Guided High-Resolution 3D Genome Reconstruction Preserves Chromatin Organization at Kilobase Resolution.

Pandeya, Abhishek; Chowdhury, Md Fahimul Kabir; Oluwadare, Oluwatosin
bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology · 2026-10-25 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

HiC-LEGO是一个基于图学习与层次化组装的高分辨率三维基因组重构框架，能从Hi-C/Micro-C数据重建染色质空间结构并保留TAD、增强子-启动子互作等组织特征，直接契合三维基因组计算建模课题。

摘要

Three-dimensional (3D) chromosome reconstruction from Hi-C contact maps remains challenging because genome organization is hierarchical and fine-resolution models must reconcile local structure with chromosome-scale constraints. Here we present HiC-LEGO, a domain-aware hierarchical framework integrating ensemble chromatin domains with graph-based structural learning and progressive chromosome assembly. By combining ensemble domain selection with hierarchical reconstruction, HiC-LEGO reduces dependence on individual domain definitions while maintaining local organization during chromosome-scale assembly. Across five human cell lines at 5-kb resolution, HiC-LEGO achieves higher reconstruction concordance than evaluated state-of-the-art methods while better preserving domain organization. At 1-kb resolution, HiC-LEGO reconstructs complete GM12878 chromosome 8 and recovers close spatial proximity between an epigenomically supported distal MYC enhancer and its promoter. Reconstructions from 5-kb Micro-C data show that 249 experimentally defined RCMC microcompartment interactions at the Ppm1g locus occupy compact 3D configurations. In the breast cancer dataset, HiC-LEGO reconstructs structures that maintain stable TAD organization across healthy breast, primary tumors, and liver metastases, while revealing greater inter-patient structural heterogeneity in malignant pleural effusion samples. Pore-C validation shows that experimentally observed multi-way contacts spanning 1-5 Mb are enriched in compact reconstructed configurations across all 23 chromosomes. Thus, HiC-LEGO preserves regulatory interactions, disease-associated chromatin organization and higher-order spatial relationships beyond pairwise contact-map concordance.

摘要翻译

从Hi-C接触图谱进行三维（3D）染色体重建仍然具有挑战性，因为基因组组织是分层的，且精细分辨率模型必须协调局部结构与染色体尺度约束。在此，我们提出HiC-LEGO，一种域感知的分层框架，整合了集成染色质域、基于图的结构学习和渐进式染色体组装。通过将集成域选择与分层重建相结合，HiC-LEGO减少了对单个域定义的依赖，同时在染色体尺度组装过程中维持局部组织。在五种人类细胞系中，以5-kb分辨率，HiC-LEGO实现了比所评估的最先进方法更高的重建一致性，同时更好地保留了域组织。在1-kb分辨率下，HiC-LEGO重建了完整的GM12878染色体8，并恢复了表观基因组学支持的远端MYC增强子与其启动子之间的紧密空间邻近。从5-kb Micro-C数据进行的重建显示，Ppm1g位点上249个实验定义的RCMC微区室相互作用占据紧凑的3D构型。在乳腺癌数据集中，HiC-LEGO重建的结构在健康乳腺、原发肿瘤和肝转移中维持稳定的TAD组织，同时在恶性胸腔积液样本中揭示了更大的患者间结构异质性。Pore-C验证表明，实验观察到的跨越1-5 Mb的多向接触在所有23条染色体的紧凑重建构型中富集。因此，HiC-LEGO在成对接触图谱一致性之外，保留了调控相互作用、疾病相关染色质组织和更高阶空间关系。

02 一句话总结

针对高分辨率（<25 kb）染色体级 3D 重建中「域定义依赖单一 TAD caller、局部到全局装配易破坏局部组织」的问题，HiC-LEGO 用多 TAD caller 的 ensemble 域选择 + LINE 嵌入/GNN 域级结构推断 + 1-Mb backbone 引导的分层装配，在 5 kb 下于 5 个人类细胞系取得高于 FLAMINGO 与 Hierarchical3DGenome 的 dSCC 并更好保留 TAD 边界与域内组织，在 1 kb 下重建完整 GM12878 8 号染色体并复现 MYC enhancer–promoter 空间邻近，但结论边界是：重建坐标以 pairwise 接触约束为主，未标定物理单位，且部分样本/指标未超过 null。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何在 <25 kb 分辨率下重建染色体级 3D 结构，同时（a）不依赖单一 TAD caller 的域划分，（b）在从局部域到染色体尺度的装配过程中不破坏局部染色质组织。
  • 为什么重要：高分辨率重建把接触图转成可直接解读的空间坐标，可用于检查 enhancer–promoter 邻近、loop anchor 空间关系、疾病相关 TAD/compartment 变化等接触图本身难以直接判读的问题。
  • 现有方法为何不足：作者指出两类高分辨率分层方法各有缺陷——FLAMINGO 类方法用固定基因组窗口或先建粗 backbone 再局部精修，可能切分或合并生物学定义的 TAD；Hierarchical3DGenome 依赖单一 TAD caller，使 caller 特异的边界误差传播进最终结构。而 TAD caller 之间假设差异大（insulation score、统计建模、聚类、图模块度），同一 Hi-C 图可给出不同边界。
  • 精确研究问题（[Analysis] 由本文动机重构）：Can a hierarchical 3D reconstruction framework that uses an ensemble-consensus TAD set, rather than a single caller's domains or fixed windows, preserve both chromosome-scale contact concordance and local domain/regulatory organization at kilobase resolution?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法：把染色体先切成「共识 TAD」，每个域作为一个图节点，用 LINE 嵌入 + mean-neighbor 聚合 GNN 推断域级 3D 坐标；再把域内细 bin 用局部 LINE 嵌入 + 正交 Procrustes 对齐到域表示后微调；随后按 1-Mb 索引把域组装成 LEGO 区块，用刚体变换（尺度/旋转/平移）与坐标下降最大化与 Hi-C 目标距离的 Spearman 相关；最后用独立重建的 1-Mb backbone 锚定并做染色体级精修。 2) 核心洞察：高分辨率重建的瓶颈不只是优化器，而是「结构单元怎么定义」；把域定义从单点决策变成 ensemble 共识，可把 caller 不确定性从结构误差中解耦；同时把「域内几何」与「域间装配」分成两级自由度（域内固定相对几何、域间只允许刚体变换），使局部组织在全局装配中不被破坏。 3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在分层建模中，把「不确定的中间表示」（此处为 TAD 边界）显式做成 ensemble/共识层，并把下游优化限制在保结构的变换族（刚体 + 各向同性尺度）内，是一种可迁移的「不确定性隔离 + 结构保持」设计模式；该模式可迁移到其他分层生物结构建模（如染色质 loop 域、核小体阵列、多聚体模拟的粗粒化层级）。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：某染色体 fine-resolution（5 kb 或 1 kb）KR 归一化 Hi-C 矩阵；该染色体的 1-Mb KR 归一化 Hi-C 矩阵；由多 TAD caller 得到的域集合（半开区间 [sk, ek)）。
  • 输出：每个有效 fine-resolution bin 一个笛卡尔坐标（arbitrary model units），可导出 PDB。
  • 模块：① ensemble TAD 选择（MoC + 三假设）；② 域图构建与 GNN 域级重建（LINE 512 维嵌入、mean-neighbor 聚合、512→256→128→64→3）；③ 域内细分辨率重建（局部 LINE 嵌入 + Procrustes 对齐 + 微调）；④ LEGO 区块组装（1-Mb 索引、刚体变换、坐标下降、gap 用 Lorentzian 优化、bead-level 平滑）；⑤ 1-Mb backbone 重建与染色体级精修（Huber 加权、L-BFGS-B、Gauss–Seidel 顺序更新、spline 插值补 gap）。
  • 训练：每个染色体单独训练 GNN；α（接触→距离幂指数）用网格搜索（域级 {0.1,…,1.9}，域内 {0.1,…,2.0}，染色体级 {0.1,…,2.0}），以 Spearman 距离相关选最优；Adam，域级 lr 1e-3、最多 1000 epochs、收敛阈值 |Δloss|<1e-8；域内微调收敛阈值 |Δloss|<1e-4；LINE 训练 100 epochs、Adam lr 0.01、batch 4096、负采样比 5。
  • 工具：Python + PyTorch + SciPy + Numba；GPU 可用；Juicer Tools 做 KR 归一化；cooltools 算 PC1；TADCompare 评边界；Chimera/PyMOL 可视化。
  • 反馈回路：α 网格搜索 → 训练 → 以 Spearman 选 α；染色体级精修后重算 contact-to-distance 关系并做第二轮粗对齐。
  • 假设：Hi-C 交互频率越高对应空间距离越小（逆幂律，Eq. 6/13）；域内相对几何在装配中应保持（仅允许各向同性尺度 + 旋转 + 平移）；1-Mb backbone 可代表染色体尺度构型。

文字流程：多 caller 域 → MoC 选共识域 → 域间聚合交互建图 → LINE 嵌入 + GNN 得域坐标 → 域内细 bin 局部嵌入 + Procrustes 对齐 + 微调 → 按 1-Mb 分组为 LEGO → 刚体变换 + 坐标下降装配 → gap 插值 + 平滑 → 1-Mb backbone 锚定 → 染色体级 Huber 精修 → 输出坐标/PDB。

09 关键公式符号

  • Eq. 1 MoC(A,B) = Σ(|A∩B|² / (|A||B|)) / (N−1)：|A∩B| 为两域重叠长度，|A|、|B| 为各自长度，N 为比较次数；用途：量化两个域集的结构一致性；直觉：归一化区间重叠越大分越高。来源：Methods 4.1。
  • Eq. 2 AvgMoC(X) = (1/|𝒟∖{X}|) Σ_{Y∈𝒟∖{X}} MoC(X,Y)：𝒟 为全部 TAD-call 集；用途：Hypothesis 3 中评估单个域集与其余方法的平均一致性。来源：Methods 4.1。
  • Eq. 3 D* = argmax_X AvgMoC(X)：用途：Hypothesis 3 选最优域集。来源：Methods 4.1。
  • Eq. 4 I_ab = Σ_{i∈ℬa} Σ_{j∈ℬb} C_ij：C_ij 为 KR 归一化交互频率，ℬa/ℬb 为域内 fine-res bin 集；用途：域间聚合交互。来源：Methods 4.3。
  • Eq. 5 h'i = W_nbr (1/|𝒩(i)| Σ{j∈𝒩(i)} h_j) + W_root h_i：h_i 为节点表示，𝒩(i) 为邻居集，W_nbr/W_root 为可学习线性变换；用途：mean-neighbor 聚合 + 自身变换的图卷积层。来源：Methods 4.3。
  • Eq. 6 d_ij = (I_ij + 10⁻⁸)^(−α)：I_ij 为域间聚合交互（图构建前），d_ij 为目标距离，α>0 为转换因子，10⁻⁸ 仅数值稳定、无物理含义；用途：交互→距离。来源：Methods 4.3。
  • Eq. 7 ℒ_domain = (1/N²) Σ_i Σ_j (d̂_ij − d_ij)²：N 为保留域数，d̂_ij = ‖x_i − x_j‖₂；用途：域级训练损失。来源：Methods 4.3。
  • Eq. 8 R_k* = argmin_{RᵀR=I} ‖E_k^loc R − (1/n_k) e_kᵀ‖_F²：E_k^loc 为域内局部 LINE 嵌入，e_k 为染色体级域嵌入，n_k 为域内 bin 数；用途：正交 Procrustes 对齐局部与域级表示。来源：Methods 4.3。
  • Eq. 9 ℒ_k = (1/|Ω_k|) Σ_{(i,j)∈Ω_k} (‖x_i − x_j‖₂ − d_ij)²，Ω_k = {(i,j): i<j}：用途：域内微调损失（上三角对）。来源：Methods 4.3。
  • Eq. 10 X_k* = q_k R_k X_k + t_k：q_k 各向同性尺度，R_k 旋转，t_k 平移；用途：域刚体装配。来源：Methods 4.4。
  • Eq. 11 max_{θ_k} ρ_S({‖x_i−x_j‖₂}, {d_ij})：ρ_S 为 Spearman 相关，θ_k 为域变换参数；用途：LEGO 内坐标下降目标。来源：Methods 4.4。
  • Eq. 12 max_{X_gap} Σ_{(i,j)∈Ω_gap} c²/(c² + (‖x_i−x_j‖₂ − d_ij)²)，c=1：用途：gap bin 的有界 Lorentzian 优化。来源：Methods 4.4。
  • Eq. 13 q_uv(α) = (C_uv + 10⁻⁶)^(−α)，w_uv = C_uv：C_uv 为 fine-res KR 归一化交互，10⁻⁶ 仅数值稳定；用途：染色体级未缩放目标距离与亚线性权重。来源：Methods 4.4。
  • Eq. 14 s* = [Σ w_uv d_uv^pred q_uv(α)] / [Σ w_uv q_uv(α)²]：Ω_inter 为选定域间接触集；用途：全局距离尺度的闭式加权最小二乘估计。来源：Methods 4.4。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：方法针对单一染色体内的高分辨率 3D 重建；跨染色体/全核构型不在范围内。
  • oracle/真值输入：TAD 域集来自多 caller ensemble，不是真值；MYC enhancer 由 ChromHMM + H3K27ac 独立支持；RCMC 与 Pore-C 为实验注释，但 Pore-C 验证依赖重建坐标与 null 模型选择。
  • 端到端状态：从 KR 归一化 Hi-C 矩阵到 PDB 坐标是端到端流程；但 α 网格搜索与多级精修引入多个超参，未报告完整敏感性分析。
  • 算力成本：仅相对 runtime 比较，无绝对时间/硬件；1 kb 全染色体因成本只做 chr8。
  • 历史数据依赖：乳腺癌结论依赖 van den Brand et al. [35] 的既有发现作为参照，属「复现既有观察」而非独立新发现。
  • 模型依赖：结论依赖 GNN 架构、LINE 嵌入、Procrustes 对齐、刚体装配等设计选择；未做逐模块消融。
  • 最难情形：PE 样本异质性最强，部分重建未超 null（5 kb 下 5/20，含 3 个 PE）；anchor-shuffle 严格 null 下 Pore-C 平均距离无全基因组优势。
  • 群体/领域边界：5 个人类细胞系 + mESC + 乳腺癌样本；未覆盖其他物种或非哺乳动物系统。
  • 不确定性：坐标为 arbitrary model units，未标定物理尺度；dSCC 在 1 kb 降至 0.34；部分指标（如 APA = 1.04）效应量小。
  • 有边界复述：在 5 kb 下，HiC-LEGO 在 5 个人类细胞系的 115 个染色体级重建中取得高于 FLAMINGO 与 H3DG 的 dSCC 并更好保留 TAD 边界与域内组织；在 1 kb 下可重建完整 GM12878 chr8 并复现 MYC enhancer–promoter 邻近；在 Micro-C、RCMC、乳腺癌与 Pore-C 数据上保留若干实验观察到的空间关系，但部分样本/严格 null 下未达一致优势，且坐标未物理标定。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选

  • 可迁移概念：把「不确定的中间结构表示」（TAD 边界）做成 ensemble 共识层，是隔离上游方法不确定性的通用手段；可迁移到 loop 域定义、compartment 边界、核小体阵列边界等。
  • 可迁移方法：分层自由度设计——域内保持相对几何、域间仅允许各向同性尺度 + 旋转 + 平移（Eq. 10），使局部组织在全局装配中不被破坏；可迁移到多聚体粗粒化模拟的层级装配。
  • 可迁移方法：用正交 Procrustes（Eq. 8）把局部嵌入对齐到全局嵌入，是一种轻量的表示迁移手段，可迁移到跨分辨率表示对齐（如 5 kb 表示迁移到 1 kb）。
  • 可迁移公式：逆幂律接触→距离（Eq. 6/13）+ 网格搜索 α + Spearman 选优，是可复用的接触图→距离转换模板；注意 10⁻⁸/10⁻⁶ 仅为数值稳定项。
  • 可迁移实验设计：用正交实验数据（FISH、CTCF/RAD21、ChromHMM/H3K27ac、RCMC、Pore-C）做接触图一致性之外的验证；用距离匹配 null 与 anchor-shuffle null 双重 null 检验高阶组织。
  • 可迁移评测：TADCompare 的边界分类（non-differential / shift / merge / split / complex）可作为重建质量的结构性指标，补充 dSCC。
  • 面向本课题的迁移点：若做「AI + 物理模拟」混合，可把 HiC-LEGO 的坐标作为物理模拟的初始构型，再用 polymer 势能精修；或把 ensemble 域选择作为物理模拟中约束分区的输入。

15 与已有知识连接

  • 相似：Hierarchical3DGenome [18]（TAD 级重建 + inter-TAD 装配）、FLAMINGO [19]（模块化 + 优化式 embedding）——本文直接对标的两条高分辨率路线。
  • 组合：GEM-FISH [16]（Hi-C + FISH + polymer 约束）、Hi-BDiSCO [17]（Hi-C/Micro-C 约束 + Brownian dynamics）——可与 HiC-LEGO 的坐标输出组合，形成「AI 重建 + 物理精修」流程。
  • 冲突/张力：本文以 pairwise 接触一致性为主目标，而物理模拟方法强调能量与物理尺度；本文坐标未标定物理单位（Methods 4.5），与物理模拟整合时需先做尺度标定。
  • 可迁移领域：图神经网络在生物结构建模中的应用（LINE 嵌入 [43]、mean-neighbor 聚合）；正交 Procrustes [45] 在表示对齐中的通用性；Huber 损失 [46] 与 L-BFGS-B [47] 在带噪结构优化中的稳健性。
  • 外部可核验文献（本文引用）：Rao et al. [27]（FISH 探针）、Bonev et al. [33]（mESC Hi-C）、van den Brand et al. [35]（乳腺癌 Hi-C）、RCMC [34]、Pore-C [36,37]、TADCompare [29]、cooltools [26]、Juicer Tools [41]、MoC [23]。

16 研究想法

Agent 生成的研究候选

  1. 名称：Ensemble-Domain Ablation Suite for HiC-LEGO - 来源局限/观察：Results 第 2 节仅比较 Hypothesis 2 vs 3，未做逐模块消融（13 节观察 1）。 - 核心假设：dSCC 提升主要来自 ensemble 域选择，而非 GNN 架构或 α 搜索。 - 相对本文的增量：把「生物学引导」的贡献从其他模块中分离出来。 - 初步方法：固定 GNN/装配/α 流程，分别替换域选择为单一 caller（如 insulation score、HiCExplorer、TopDom）与固定窗口，比较 dSCC、TADCompare 边界指标、域内 dSCC。 - 验证方式：在 5 细胞系 × 23 染色体上做配对检验，报告效应量与置信区间。 - 可能的失败模式：若单一 caller 与 ensemble 差异不显著，则核心主张被削弱。 - 创新状态：unverified。

  2. 名称：Physical-Scale Calibration of HiC-LEGO Coordinates via FISH - 来源局限/观察：坐标为 arbitrary model units（Methods 4.5），无法与物理模拟或 FISH 物理距离直接比较。 - 核心假设：存在稳定的线性/幂律映射把模型单位转为 nm 尺度。 - 相对本文的增量：使重建坐标可用于物理模拟初始化与跨方法比较。 - 初步方法：用 Rao et al. [27] 的 FISH 探针距离拟合尺度映射，检验跨细胞系/跨染色体稳定性。 - 验证方式：留出 FISH 位点做预测误差评估；与 Hi-BDiSCO 的物理尺度结构比较。 - 可能的失败模式：映射不稳定或依赖细胞系，说明模型单位不可标定。 - 创新状态：unverified。

  3. 名称：Multi-Null Pore-C Benchmark for Higher-Order Reconstruction - 来源局限/观察：Pore-C 在 anchor-shuffle null 下平均最大距离无全基因组优势（Results 第 8 节）。 - 核心假设：紧凑性信号部分来自 null 构造，需多 null 联合判定。 - 相对本文的增量：给出高阶组织保留的稳健判定标准。 - 初步方法：预注册距离匹配、anchor-shuffle、序列保守 shuffle 三类 null，报告全部结果与效应量。 - 验证方式：在多个重建方法（HiC-LEGO、FLAMINGO、H3DG）上做同一 benchmark。 - 可能的失败模式：所有 null 下信号均不显著，则高阶组织保留主张不成立。 - 创新状态：unverified。

  4. 名称：Cross-Resolution Representation Transfer with Procrustes - 来源局限/观察：1 kb 仅 chr8 且 dSCC 降至 0.34（Results 第 4 节）。 - 核心假设：5 kb 学到的域表示可通过 Procrustes 迁移到 1 kb，降低 1 kb 训练成本。 - 相对本文的增量：把 1 kb 重建扩展到多染色体/多细胞系。 - 初步方法：用 5 kb 域嵌入初始化 1 kb 域内微调，比较 dSCC 与训练时间。 - 验证方式：在 GM12878 多条染色体上做 1 kb 重建，报告 dSCC 分布与 MYC 类位点验证。 - 可能的失败模式：跨分辨率表示不对齐，迁移后 dSCC 不升反降。 - 创新状态：unverified。

  5. 名称：Hybrid AI–Physics Refinement of HiC-LEGO Structures - 来源局限/观察：本文结构主要受 pairwise 接触约束（Discussion），未引入物理势能。 - 核心假设：以 HiC-LEGO 坐标为初始构型，用 polymer/Brownian dynamics 精修可提升与 FISH/Pore-C 的一致性。 - 相对本文的增量：把 AI 重建与物理模拟串联，形成可标定物理尺度的流程。 - 初步方法：用 Hi-BDiSCO 类流程对 HiC-LEGO 输出做约束模拟，比较精修前后与 FISH/Pore-C 的一致性。 - 验证方式：在 GM12878 chr8 与 mESC Ppm1g 位点上做前后对比。 - 可能的失败模式：物理精修破坏局部域组织，或计算成本不可接受。 - 创新状态：unverified。

  6. 名称：Ensemble Domain Selection for Single-Cell / Low-Coverage Hi-C - 来源局限/观察：本文仅在 bulk Hi-C/Micro-C 上验证，未涉及低覆盖或单细胞数据。 - 核心假设：ensemble 共识域在低覆盖下比单一 caller 更稳健。 - 相对本文的增量：把方法推广到数据稀疏场景。 - 初步方法：对单细胞 Hi-C 或下采样 bulk Hi-C 做 ensemble 域选择与重建，比较 dSCC 与边界指标。 - 验证方式：与单细胞专用方法比较；用已知 TAD 边界做 ground truth。 - 可能的失败模式：低覆盖下所有 caller 都不稳定，ensemble 无法提供共识。 - 创新状态：unverified。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The manuscript describes a coherent and reasonably well-motivated method for high-resolution 3D genome reconstruction and reports a broad set of evaluations spanning contact-map concordance, domain preservation, regulatory proximity, Micro-C/RCMC features, disease-associated organization, and multi-way Pore-C contacts. The conceptual framing, namely that domain definition is a critical and under-addressed step in hierarchical reconstruction, is sensible and the ensemble-selection idea is a reasonable response to TAD-caller variability. However, the supplied material does not permit independent verification of several central quantitative claims because key statistics, null models, and per-chromosome results reside in supplementary items that were not provided. Several reported effects are modest in magnitude, and the manuscript itself acknowledges that a stricter null does not reproduce the Pore-C compaction advantage in mean distance. The evidence as presented is suggestive but not yet sufficient to establish the stronger claims in the title and abstract.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in computational genomics and 3D genome biology interested in converting Hi-C/Micro-C/Pore-C contact maps into explicit spatial coordinates; groups studying TAD-caller disagreement and ensemble domain definitions; cancer genomics researchers interested in structural correlates of disease progression; and method developers benchmarking high-resolution reconstruction against FLAMINGO and Hierarchical3DGenome. The work would also interest scientists seeking structural frameworks for interpreting regulatory interactions such as enhancer–promoter proximity.
  • Major strengths The problem framing is clear and identifies a genuine limitation of existing hierarchical methods, namely sensitivity to domain definition. The pipeline is described in sufficient methodological detail to be reimplemented in principle, with explicit equations for domain interaction aggregation, embedding, distance conversion, and assembly. The evaluation spans multiple cell lines, multiple resolutions, multiple data modalities (Hi-C, Micro-C, RCMC, Pore-C), and a disease progression dataset, which is broader than typical for a single reconstruction method. The authors report a negative or non-confirmatory result for the stricter Pore-C anchor-shuffle null, which is a point of scientific honesty. Runtime and GPU compatibility are reported, which is relevant for practical adoption.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1 Severity Major Blocking Yes Axis Benchmarking validity and baseline fairness Claim pointer HiC-LEGO achieves higher reconstruction concordance than evaluated state-of-the-art methods at 5-kb resolution across five cell lines, with the highest mean chromosome-level dSCC in all 23 chromosomes per cell line. Evidence pointer Results section "Benchmarking HiC-LEGO Against State-of-the-Art..."; Fig. 2a, 2b, 2i Concern The comparison rests on dSCC as the primary metric, but the manuscript does not establish that dSCC is comparably optimized or comparably defined across the three methods. FLAMINGO and H3DG may use different internal objectives, distance conversions, or output conventions, and dSCC computed on reconstructed coordinates can favor methods whose training objective is itself a distance-correlation-like criterion. The reported margins (for example 0.52 versus 0.44–0.45 and 0.40–0.41 in K562 and HMEC) are not accompanied by dispersion, confidence intervals, or paired tests across chromosomes, so it is not possible to judge whether the differences are statistically separable from chromosome-to-chromosome variability. Why it matters The central comparative claim of the paper depends on this benchmark. If the metric is not neutral across methods, or if the differences are within noise, the headline claim of superior concordance is not established. Resolution test Provide per-chromosome dSCC values with dispersion, paired statistical tests across the 115 chromosome-level reconstructions, and a demonstration that dSCC is computed identically and neutrally for all three methods. Ideally include at least one orthogonal accuracy metric that is not a distance-correlation measure.
  • Concern ID R1-M2 Severity Major Blocking Yes Axis Statistical support for null-model comparisons Claim pointer The MYC enhancer–promoter pair is 4.01-fold closer than the control median with empirical p = 0.0050; the 249 RCMC interactions are 1.24-fold enriched with p = 4.87 × 10−10; Pore-C native contacts show a genome-wide 7.4% median reduction and 17.4% falling in the most compact 10% of controls. Evidence pointer Results sections on 1-kb MYC locus, Ppm1g RCMC, and Pore-C; Fig. 3c,d, Fig. 4d, Fig. 7b,c; Supplementary Methods referenced but not provided Concern The construction of genomic-distance-matched controls, the number of control draws, the treatment of multiple comparisons across loci and chromosomes, and the definition of the empirical p-value are all deferred to Supplementary Methods that were not supplied. The Pore-C analysis in particular reports a genome-wide median reduction of 7.4% and an enrichment of 17.4% versus a 10% null, which are modest effects whose interpretation depends entirely on how the null preserves genomic distance, anchor identity, and interaction complexity. The manuscript also reports that under the stricter anchor-shuffle null the mean maximum distance advantage disappears genome-wide, which weakens the robustness of the compaction claim. Why it matters These null-model comparisons carry several of the paper's biological validation claims. Without the null construction, the reported p-values and fold-enrichments cannot be assessed for correctness or for sensitivity to null design. Resolution test Supply the Supplementary Methods with the exact control-generation procedure, the number of matched controls per test, the multiple-testing correction, and a sensitivity analysis showing that the reported enrichments survive reasonable alternative null constructions, including the anchor-shuffle null for the RCMC and MYC analyses.
  • Concern ID R1-M3 Severity Major Blocking No Axis Internal consistency of ensemble domain selection Claim pointer Hypothesis 2 consistently outperforms Hypothesis 3 across all autosomes and chromosome X, with mean chromosome-wide dSCC increasing from 0.399 to 0.477, supporting the use of Hypothesis 2 for subsequent reconstruction. Evidence pointer Results section "Ensemble-based TAD optimization..."; Supplementary Figure S1 (not provided) Concern The selection of Hypothesis 2 is based on downstream dSCC, which is the same metric used to benchmark against competing methods. This creates a risk that the ensemble strategy is tuned to the evaluation metric rather than to biological fidelity. In addition, Hypothesis 1 was excluded because it retained too few domains, which means the ensemble framework's most stringent consensus option was never quantitatively evaluated. The claim that Hypothesis 2 reduces dependence on individual domain definitions is therefore supported only relative to Hypothesis 3, not relative to the full space of domain-selection strategies. Why it matters The methodological novelty of the paper is the ensemble domain selection. If its selection is driven by the same metric used to claim superiority, the independence of the validation is reduced. Resolution test Report the per-chromosome comparison underlying Supplementary Figure S1 in the main text, and evaluate the selected domain set against at least one biological criterion that is not dSCC, for example boundary overlap with an independent annotation or recovery of CTCF-anchored loops.
  • Concern ID R1-M4 Severity Major Blocking No Axis Generalizability of the 1-kb claim Claim pointer HiC-LEGO reconstructs complete GM12878 chromosome 8 at 1-kb resolution and recovers close spatial proximity between an epigenomically supported distal MYC enhancer and its promoter. Evidence pointer Results section "HiC-LEGO resolves regulatory chromatin proximity at 1 kb resolution"; Fig. 3a–e Concern The 1-kb evaluation is limited to a single chromosome in a single cell line, and the chromosome-wide dSCC of 0.34 is substantially lower than the 5-kb values. The enhancer–promoter result rests on one selected HiCCUPS loop with a single empirical p-value, and the manuscript does not report how many other candidate enhancer–promoter pairs were tested or whether the selected pair is representative. The authors state that the analysis was limited by computational cost, which is understandable, but the abstract and title foreground kilobase resolution as a general capability. Why it matters The kilobase-resolution claim is a headline element. A single-locus, single-chromosome demonstration does not establish that the method generally preserves regulatory proximity at this resolution. Resolution test Either extend the 1-kb analysis to additional chromosomes or cell lines, or report a systematic survey of multiple enhancer–promoter pairs with an explicit multiple-testing framework, and temper the generality of the kilobase claim accordingly.
  • Concern ID R1-M5 Severity Major Blocking No Axis Interpretation of disease-associated findings Claim pointer HiC-LEGO reconstructs structures that maintain stable TAD organization across healthy breast, primary tumors, and liver metastases, while revealing greater inter-patient structural heterogeneity in malignant pleural effusion samples. Evidence pointer Results sections on breast cancer at 5-kb and 1-kb; Figs. 5 and 6; Supplementary Figs. S1 and S2 (not provided) Concern The breast cancer analyses are explicitly described as partially recovering reported patterns, with several metrics following the expected direction but not all, and with five of twenty chromosome 13 reconstructions (including three PE samples) classified as lower confidence. The leave-one-out centroid analysis and boundary-similarity comparisons depend on Supplementary Figures that were not supplied. The claim of "stable TAD organization" across HB, PB, and LM is therefore a partial reproduction of a prior study's finding rather than an independent discovery, and the manuscript does not clearly separate what is reproduced from what is newly contributed. Why it matters The disease-associated claims are presented as validation of biological relevance. If they largely restate prior findings and are only partially recovered, their weight as independent evidence is limited. Resolution test Clearly delineate reproduced versus novel observations, report the lower-confidence samples explicitly in the main text with their effect on the conclusions, and provide the sensitivity analyses currently in Supplementary Figs. S1 and S2.

  • Assessment against Nature-style criteria Originality is moderate. Ensemble TAD selection and hierarchical assembly are individually established ideas, and the contribution is their combination with graph-based structural learning; the novelty is incremental rather than conceptual. Scientific importance is potentially meaningful for the 3D genome reconstruction community, but the demonstrated gains over existing methods are modest and the biological validations are largely reproductions of prior findings. Interdisciplinary readership is limited; the work is primarily of interest to computational genomics and 3D genome biology, with only indirect relevance to broader biology or medicine. Technical soundness is the weakest axis in the supplied material, because several central quantitative claims depend on supplementary null models and per-chromosome statistics that were not provided, and the primary benchmark metric is not demonstrated to be neutral across methods. Readability for nonspecialists is adequate in the Introduction and Discussion, but the Results sections assume substantial familiarity with dSCC, TAD callers, and contact-map conventions, and the abstract overstates generality relative to the single-chromosome 1-kb demonstration.

The work is potentially supportive if the technical concerns, in particular the benchmark neutrality, null-model construction, and generality of the kilobase claim, are resolved with the missing supplementary material and additional statistical reporting.

Risk / unsupported claims

  • The claim of higher reconstruction concordance than state-of-the-art methods is supported only by dSCC, whose neutrality across methods is not demonstrated, and without dispersion or paired tests (weakly supported).
  • The claim that Hypothesis 2 reduces dependence on individual domain definitions is supported only relative to Hypothesis 3, with Hypothesis 1 excluded and Supplementary Figure S1 not provided (weakly supported).
  • The MYC enhancer–promoter proximity claim rests on a single selected loop with an empirical p-value whose null construction is not provided (unverifiable from supplied material).
  • The RCMC microcompartment compaction claim (1.24-fold, p = 4.87 × 10−10) depends on a matched-control procedure described only in Supplementary Methods (unverifiable from supplied material).
  • The Pore-C multi-way compaction claim is weakened by the manuscript's own report that the stricter anchor-shuffle null does not show a genome-wide mean-distance advantage, and the genome-wide 7.4% median reduction and 17.4% versus 10% enrichment are modest (weakly supported).
  • The breast cancer claims of stable early-stage TAD organization and increased pleural-effusion heterogeneity are partial reproductions of van den Brand et al., with five of twenty chromosome 13 reconstructions classified as lower confidence and sensitivity analyses deferred to Supplementary Figures (weakly supported).
  • The generalizability of kilobase-resolution reconstruction is asserted in the title and abstract but demonstrated only for GM12878 chromosome 8 (unsupported as a general claim).
  • The statement that HiC-LEGO "preserves regulatory interactions, disease-associated chromatin organization and higher-order spatial relationships beyond pairwise contact-map concordance" is broader than the supplied evidence, which is largely concordance-based with limited independent biological validation (overstated relative to evidence).
