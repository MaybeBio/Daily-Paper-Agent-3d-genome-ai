Multi-task adversarial autoencoder for functional genomic element generation with preserved biophysical properties.

Adamu, Shamsuddeen; Alhussian, Hitham; Abdulkadir, Said Jadid; Eltahir, Majdy Mohamed Eltayeb; Khairy, Sallam O F; Hassan, Ibrahim Hayatu; Kurah, Ibrahim Muhammad; Mukhtar, Abubakar; Ganesalingam, Vijayakanthan; Ravi, Vaishali; Saidu, Yahaya
BioData mining · 2026-11-02 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文提出多任务对抗自编码器生成功能基因组序列并保留顺式调控语法与生物物理特性，虽涉及基因组调控元件生成，但未建模染色质三维空间组织或互作，方法上AI生成模型对序列生成有启发，但与本课题三维基因组架构建模关联较弱。

摘要

Generative modeling of genomic sequences presents a stringent test for deep learning, requiring the capture of long-range dependencies and functional constraints beyond local nucleotide statistics. Existing architectures frequently collapse to limited modes or reproduce shallow nucleotide distributions without encoding functional semantics. We introduce the Multi-Task Adversarial Autoencoder (MT-AAE), a hybrid generative framework that integrates adversarial regularization with auxiliary functional and biophysical objectives to enforce structured latent representations. Evaluated on an empirical human gene corpus, MT-AAE achieved a Train-on-Synthetic-Test-on-Real (TRTS) accuracy of 74.7%, compared with 41.0% for a standard GAN baseline. Stratified analysis further showed that functional discriminability increased to 89.3% when sequence lengths aligned with the model's architectural window. Importantly, the learned representations exhibited emergent biological structure: synthetic sequences spontaneously preserved cis-regulatory syntax, including canonical TATA-box motifs recovered across 100% of generated promoter sequences without explicit rule encoding, though positional placement relative to the TSS was not statistically significant (KS [Formula: see text]), and the high occurrence rate is partly attributable to the AT-rich composition of the generated sequences. Representation-level validation using frozen DNABERT-2 and DNABERT-S embeddings confirmed that the generated sequences retained functional information beyond shallow k-mer statistics. Cross-species evaluation on Mus musculus sequences further demonstrated species-specific learning consistent with known human-mouse regulatory divergence. The framework also mitigated mode collapse, maintaining near-uniform generation across functional classes ([Formula: see text]), including rare categories such as tRNAs ([Formula: see text] of the dataset). These findings position MT-AAE as an effective framework for biologically constrained genomic sequence generation.

摘要翻译

基因组序列的生成建模对深度学习提出了严格考验，要求捕捉超出局部核苷酸统计的远程依赖关系和功能约束。现有架构常常坍缩至有限模式，或仅复现浅层核苷酸分布，而未编码功能语义。我们引入了多任务对抗自编码器（Multi-Task Adversarial Autoencoder, MT-AAE），这是一种混合生成框架，将对抗正则化与辅助功能和生物物理目标相结合，以强制实现结构化潜在表示。在经验性人类基因语料库上的评估中，MT-AAE 实现了合成训练-真实测试（Train-on-Synthetic-Test-on-Real, TRTS）准确率74.7%，而标准GAN基线仅为41.0%。分层分析进一步表明，当序列长度与模型架构窗口对齐时，功能可区分性提升至89.3%。重要的是，学习到的表示展现出涌现性生物结构：合成序列自发保留了顺式调控语法，包括在100%的生成启动子序列中恢复典型TATA盒基序，而无需显式规则编码，尽管相对于转录起始位点（TSS）的位置分布未达到统计显著性（KS [公式见原文]），且高出现率部分归因于生成序列的AT富集组成。使用冻结的DNABERT-2和DNABERT-S嵌入进行的表示层面验证确认，生成序列保留了超出浅层k-mer统计的功能信息。对小鼠（Mus musculus）序列的跨物种评估进一步展示了与已知人类-小鼠调控差异一致的物种特异性学习。该框架还缓解了模式坍缩，在功能类别间保持近均匀生成（[公式见原文]），包括稀有类别如tRNA（占数据集的[公式见原文]）。这些发现将MT-AAE定位为生物约束基因组序列生成的有效框架。

02 一句话总结

本文提出 MT-AAE（Multi-Task Adversarial Autoencoder），通过将对抗训练与多任务学习（序列分类、GC 含量回归、类别平衡采样）结合，生成在功能可判别性和生物物理性质（GC 含量、k-mer 频率）上与天然人类基因组序列高度一致的变长功能基因组元件（启动子、增强子等），并在 Train-on-Synthetic/Test-on-Real（TRTS）协议下达到 74.7% 的准确率，显著优于 VAE（44.0%）和 GAN（41.0%）基线。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何生成功能基因组元件（启动子、增强子、内含子等），使其同时满足：(1) 功能可判别性（能被下游分类器识别为正确的功能类别）；(2) 生物物理性质保真（GC 含量、k-mer 频率等与天然序列一致）；(3) 类别平衡（稀有类别如 snoRNA、tRNA 不被模式崩溃吞没）？
  • 为什么重要：合成生物学、基因治疗和 mRNA 疫苗设计需要大量功能明确的调控元件，但湿实验筛选成本高、周期长。计算生成高质量合成序列可显著加速设计-验证循环，同时为数据增强提供解决方案，缓解稀有功能类别数据不足的问题。
  • 现有方法为何不足：(1) VAE 存在后验坍塌（posterior collapse），生成序列特征模糊；(2) GAN 在稀有类别上存在模式崩溃（mode collapse）；(3) 多数模型固定输出长度，不适用于长度异质的调控元件；(4) 现有方法通常只优化单一目标（如 motif 保真），缺乏对生物物理性质（GC 含量、热力学稳定性）的显式约束；(5) 缺乏可解释的隐空间结构，难以进行生物学验证。
  • 精确的「Can ... ?」研究问题：Can a multi-task adversarial autoencoder with biophysical auxiliary losses generate variable-length functional genomic elements that are both functionally discriminable (TRTS accuracy) and biophysically faithful (GC content, k-mer distribution) to natural human sequences, while maintaining class balance across rare functional categories?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法：构建一个对抗自编码器（AAE），编码器为双向 LSTM + 多头注意力，解码器为双向 LSTM，隐空间由判别器（discriminator）通过对抗训练正则化为各向同性高斯先验。在重建目标之外，叠加三个辅助任务头：(a) 功能类别分类头（10 类）；(b) GC 含量回归头；(c) 类别平衡采样策略（Algorithm 1）。总损失为重建损失、分类损失、GC 回归损失与对抗损失的加权和（λ_rec=0.3, λ_class=0.6, λ_GC=0.1, λ_adv=0.001）。

2) 核心洞察： - 对抗正则化替代 KL 散度：AAE 用判别器迫使聚合后验 q(z) 匹配先验 p(z)，避免了 VAE 中 KL 项导致的后验坍塌，同时保留了隐空间的连续性和可采样性。 - 多任务作为隐空间结构约束：分类头和 GC 回归头不仅提升下游可用性，更关键的是它们将生物学语义（功能类别、热力学性质）直接编码进隐空间，使隐变量携带可解释的生物学信息，而非仅服务于重建。 - 损失权重的不对称设计：分类损失权重（0.6）远高于重建（0.3）和 GC（0.1），反映「功能可判别性优先、生物物理保真为辅」的优化哲学——先保证序列"有用"，再保证序列"像天然序列"。 - 类别平衡作为结构干预：Algorithm 1 的平衡采样不是简单的统计修正，而是对隐空间流形的结构性干预，防止稀有类别在对抗训练中被高频类别"挤出"。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]： - 在生成模型中，「约束的显式化」比「约束的隐式化」更有效：与其依赖模型隐式学习 GC 含量等全局统计量，不如将其作为显式回归目标，让模型"知道"要优化什么。 - 多任务学习不仅是正则化手段，更是隐空间语义化的途径：当生成模型的隐空间被多个生物学相关任务同时约束时，隐变量自然获得可解释性，这比事后分析（如 probing）更直接。 - 对抗训练与任务损失的组合需要精心调权：本文的权重选择（0.3/0.6/0.1/0.001）表明，对抗损失只需极小权重即可发挥正则化作用，过大的对抗权重反而可能破坏任务学习。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：变长 DNA 序列（≤500 bp），one-hot 编码为 {0,1}^(L×4)，L=500（含 padding）；类别标签 y ∈ {1,...,10}；GC 含量标签（连续值）
  • 输出：生成的 DNA 序列（离散核苷酸序列，通过 softmax 采样/argmax）；隐空间向量 z ∈ R^128
  • 模块： 1. 编码器 E：双向 LSTM（2 层，每层 128 隐藏单元）+ 多头注意力（8 头，d_k=32）→ 全局池化 → 稠密层 → 隐向量 z 2. 解码器 G：双向 LSTM（2 层），以 z 和类别条件向量 y 为输入，逐位置输出 4 类核苷酸概率分布 3. 隐空间判别器 D_z：MLP，区分真实先验样本（从 N(0,I) 采样）与编码器输出 q(z) 4. 分类头：从 z 映射到 10 类功能类别 5. GC 回归头：从 z 映射到 GC 含量（连续值）
  • 训练：交替优化（1）生成器（E+G+任务头）最小化重建+分类+GC+对抗损失；（2）判别器最大化区分真实/伪造隐变量。优化器 Adam（lr=0.001），梯度裁剪（max norm=1.0），学习率衰减（factor=0.5, patience=15 epochs）
  • 工具：TensorFlow 2.8，scikit-learn（评估）；CD-HIT-EST（同源性聚类）；MEME Suite 和 FIMO（motif 发现与扫描，JASPAR 2026）
  • 硬件：Dell Precision 7540，Intel Xeon E-2276M，128 GB RAM
  • 流程： 1. Phase I：在合成数据集（1,850 条，6 类）上训练并验证机制有效性 2. Phase II：在 NCBI 人类基因数据集（1,910 条，10 类）上训练并评估 3. 评估：TRTS（Train-on-Synthetic/Test-on-Real）和 RTSR（反向）协议；GC 含量 MAE；k-mer JSD；类别平衡比率 R_g 4. 消融：五阶段增量消融（Base AAE → +Class → +GC → +Balance → Full） 5. 泛化测试：人类训练的模型直接应用于小鼠（Mus musculus）序列

09 关键公式符号

1) 总损失函数（Eq. 3）： L_total = λ_rec · L_rec + λ_class · L_class + λ_GC · L_GC + λ_adv · L_adv

  • L_rec：重建损失（逐位置交叉熵）
  • L_class：分类损失（加权交叉熵，权重 w_yi = N / (C · |{j: y_j = y_i}|)）
  • L_GC：GC 回归损失（均方误差）
  • L_adv：对抗损失（判别器二分类交叉熵）
  • λ_rec = 0.3, λ_class = 0.6, λ_GC = 0.1, λ_adv = 0.001

2) 编码器输出（Eq. 1）： z = W_p · (1/L · Σ_{t=1}^{L} h_t) + b_p

  • h_t：位置 t 的 BiLSTM 隐藏状态拼接（前向+后向，各 128 维 → 256 维）
  • W_p ∈ R^{128×512}：投影矩阵（含注意力加权后的全局池化）
  • b_p：偏置

3) 解码器输出（Eq. 2）： û_t = softmax(W_d · h_t^d + b_d) ∈ R^4

  • h_t^d：解码器位置 t 的隐藏状态
  • W_d ∈ R^{4×256}，b_d ∈ R^4
  • 约束：Σ_{j=1}^{4} û_{t,j} = 1（保证概率分布合法）

4) 对抗目标（Eq. 7）： min_E max_Dz E_{z~p(z)}[log D_z(z)] + E_{X~p_data}[log(1 - D_z(E(X)))]

  • p(z) = N(0, I)：各向同性高斯先验
  • D_z：隐空间判别器

5) 泛化差距（Eq. 9）： Δ_Gen = |TRTS - RTSR| = |0.747 - 0.701| = 0.046 < 0.10

  • 阈值 0.10 被作者定义为模式崩溃的指示线

6) 生成比率： R_g = N_syn / N_real，最优范围 [0.8, 1.2]

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：本文解决的是「功能基因组元件序列生成」，即给定功能类别标签，生成该类别的新序列。不是序列-功能预测，也不是调控机制推断。
  • "功能"的操作定义：功能可判别性 = 下游分类器（Random Forest + TF-IDF）能区分合成序列的类别。这是一个代理指标，不等于体外/体内实验验证的生物学功能。
  • "生物物理性质"的操作定义：GC 含量 MAE 和 k-mer JSD。这些是序列组成层面的统计量，不涉及 DNA 弯曲性、核小体定位、染色质可及性等更复杂的生物物理特征。
  • 性能边界：74.7% TRTS 准确率是在 10 类分类任务上，且分类器仅在合成序列上训练。真实生物学场景中，功能验证的成功率可能远低于此。
  • 跨物种失败：人类训练的模型在小鼠上接近随机（5.1% vs 10% 随机基线），说明模型学到的是人类特异性调控语法，不能外推到其他物种。这对三维基因组领域是一个重要警示：染色质组织特征同样具有物种特异性。
  • 长度限制：模型固定输出 500 bp（实际生成 363 bp），无法处理更长的调控元件或基因座。对三维基因组研究而言，TAD、compartment 等结构域远超过这一尺度。
  • 未验证的维度：没有 Hi-C、ATAC-seq、ChIP-seq 等实验验证合成序列的染色质状态或 3D 相互作用。作者在 Limitations 中承认未纳入染色质组织特征。
  • 端到端状态：这是一个生成模型，不是端到端的"序列→功能"管线。下游分类器是评估工具，不是模型的一部分。
  • 计算成本：训练 13.5 小时（单机工作站），生成 100,000 条序列约 16 分钟。对大规模全基因组生成仍有限制。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选

  1. 对抗正则化替代 KL 散度的有效性：在序列生成任务中，AAE 的对抗正则化比 VAE 的 KL 散度更能保持隐空间的表达力，避免后验坍塌。这一机制可直接迁移到三维基因组领域的序列-结构联合嵌入学习——例如，将 Hi-C 接触矩阵编码为隐空间时，对抗正则化可能比 ELBO 目标更稳健。

  2. 多任务损失作为隐空间语义化的工具：本文的核心方法论贡献在于，通过叠加分类和回归任务头，将生物学语义直接"写入"隐空间。对三维基因组研究，这意味着可以在生成模型中同时预测 TAD 边界、compartment 类型、CTCF 结合位点等多个结构特征，使隐空间携带多尺度结构信息。

  3. 损失权重的不对称设计原则：作者将分类损失权重设为 0.6、重建 0.3、GC 回归 0.1，体现了「功能优先、物理约束辅助」的优化哲学。在三维基因组建模中，类似地可以设计「结构保真优先、序列组成辅助」的权重分配。

  4. 类别平衡采样的结构干预作用：Algorithm 1 表明，对稀有类别的显式采样干预不仅是统计修正，更是对隐空间流形结构的保护。在三维基因组中，稀有结构类型（如特定细胞类型的独特 TAD 结构）同样需要类似的保护机制。

  5. 跨物种泛化失败的警示：人类训练的模型在小鼠上接近随机，说明调控语法具有物种特异性。这对三维基因组领域尤为重要——不同物种的染色质组织原则（如 TAD 的保守性）不能想当然地跨物种迁移，需要显式的物种条件化设计。

  6. 评估协议的循环性风险：TRTS 协议中分类器在合成数据上训练，可能高估功能保真。三维基因组领域的生成模型评估应避免类似的循环性——例如，用生成序列训练的模型去预测真实 Hi-C 数据，可能因共享浅层统计特征而虚高。

  7. frozen foundation model 作为验证工具：作者用 DNABERT-2/S、HyenaDNA 作为 frozen encoder 验证合成序列的高阶结构信息。这一思路可迁移到三维基因组领域——用训练好的 Enformer、Basenji 等模型作为 frozen evaluator，评估生成序列的调控活性预测。

  8. 消融实验中的任务干扰现象：GC 回归和平衡采样单独加入时性能下降、协同加入时超加性提升，说明多任务学习中的任务间关系是非单调的。在三维基因组的多目标建模中，需要系统搜索任务组合而非简单叠加。

15 与已有知识连接

  • BindVAE [28]：本文直接对比的 VAE 基线。BindVAE 用 Dirichlet 先验增强 motif 结构，但受限于后验坍塌。MT-AAE 用对抗正则化替代 KL 散度，是对 BindVAE 思路的改进。在三维基因组领域，类似地可以用对抗自编码器替代 VAE 进行 Hi-C 嵌入学习。

  • FBGAN [31]：本文对比的 GAN 基线。FBGAN 引入反馈机制引导生成，但模式崩溃问题严重。MT-AAE 通过重建损失 + 多任务头缓解了这一问题。三维基因组领域的 GAN 应用（如生成 Hi-C 矩阵）可借鉴这一混合架构。

  • DNABERT [21]、DNABERT-2 [66]、DNABERT-S [67]、HyenaDNA [68]：本文用这些 frozen foundation model 做表征验证。这些模型在三维基因组领域已被用于预测 CTCF 结合、启动子-增强子相互作用等任务，本文展示了它们作为生成模型评估工具的新用途。

  • Sefer [39, 40]：作者明确引用的三维基因组相关工作。Sefer [39] 证明组蛋白修饰对 Hi-C 相互作用具有预测力，Sefer [40] 提出 ProbC 概率框架联合建模表观基因组与三维基因组。本文在 Limitations 中承认未纳入这些染色质组织约束，为未来工作留下接口。

  • TRTS/TRTS 评估协议 [48]：Train-on-Synthetic/Test-on-Real 协议源自合成数据评估文献。本文将其系统化应用于基因组序列生成，并增加了 RTSR 反向验证和 Δ_Gen 阈值（0.10），这一评估框架可迁移到三维基因组生成模型的验证。

  • CD-HIT-EST [59]：序列同源性聚类工具，本文用于排除训练/测试集同源泄漏。三维基因组领域处理 Hi-C 数据时，类似的同源性控制（如排除同源 TAD 结构）值得借鉴。

  • MEME Suite / FIMO / JASPAR [53]：motif 发现与扫描工具。本文用它们验证生成序列中的转录因子结合位点。在三维基因组领域，这些工具可用于验证生成序列中 CTCF、 cohesin 相关 motif 的保真度。

  • 候选连接方向 [Analysis]：本文的「多任务 + 对抗」架构与三维基因组领域的 ChromoGen（扩散模型生成 Hi-C）、C.Origami（序列→3D 结构预测）等近期工作存在潜在互补——这些工作多聚焦于结构预测而非序列生成，本文的生成框架可作为反向任务（给定结构特征生成序列）的起点。

16 研究想法

Agent 生成的研究候选

候选 1：Hi-C 约束的调控元件生成（HiC-AAE） - 来源局限/观察：本文明确承认未纳入染色质组织约束（Limitations 节），而 Sefer [39, 40] 已证明组蛋白修饰与 Hi-C 相互作用高度关联。 - 核心假设：将 Hi-C 衍生的染色质状态特征（如 TAD 边界距离、compartment 标签、CTCF 环锚点）作为条件输入，可生成在三维基因组层面更"可及"的调控元件。 - 增量：在 MT-AAE 的隐空间条件向量中拼接 Hi-C 特征（如某 locus 的 insulation score、边界强度），并增加一个 Hi-C 接触模式预测头作为第四任务。 - 初步方法：使用 ENCODE/GEO 的匹配 Hi-C 与 DNA 序列数据；编码器输入序列 + 对应 locus 的 Hi-C 特征；解码器生成序列；新增任务头预测生成序列的预期接触模式。 - 验证方式：TRTS 协议 + 生成序列的 predicted Hi-C 模式与真实 locus 的余弦相似度；与本文 MT-AAE 对比 GC/k-mer 保真度。 - 创新状态：unverified

候选 2：跨物种染色质语法迁移（xSpecies-3DGen） - 来源局限/观察：本文跨物种泛化失败（小鼠 TRTS 5.1%），作者归因于物种特异性调控语法。 - 核心假设：通过多物种联合训练 + 物种嵌入（species embedding），模型可学习共享的染色质组织原则，同时保留物种特异特征。 - 增量：在 MT-AAE 中加入物种条件向量；训练数据混合人类、小鼠、斑马鱼等多物种序列与对应 Hi-C 数据。 - 初步方法：在编码器输入中拼接 one-hot 物种向量；训练后测试零样本迁移（如训练人类+小鼠，测试大鼠）。 - 验证方式：跨物种 TRTS 准确率；生成序列的 motif 保守性分析（如 CTCF motif 在不同物种生成序列中的富集）。 - 创新状态：unverified

候选 3：三维基因组数据增强管线（3D-Augment） - 来源局限/观察：本文的核心卖点是数据增强（"mitigate privacy concerns in large-scale biobanks"），但仅针对序列层面。 - 核心假设：MT-AAE 类生成模型可为三维基因组下游任务（如 TAD 边界预测、启动子-增强子相互作用预测）提供合成训练数据，缓解稀有细胞类型 Hi-C 数据不足的问题。 - 增量：将生成序列输入到 Hi-C 预测模型（如 C.Origami），生成合成 Hi-C 矩阵，用于增强下游分类器训练。 - 初步方法：在稀有细胞类型（如特定免疫亚群）的 Hi-C 数据上训练 MT-AAE；生成合成序列 → 预测 Hi-C → 混合真实/合成数据训练下游模型。 - 验证方式：下游任务在真实测试集上的性能提升；与仅用真实数据的基线对比。 - 创新状态：unverified

候选 4：可解释隐空间中的染色质状态解耦（Latent3D-Disentangle） - 来源局限/观察：本文声称隐空间"可解释"但仅提供 t-SNE 可视化，缺乏定量验证。 - 核心假设：通过引入解耦正则化（如 β-VAE 风格或总相关性惩罚），隐空间的不同维度可分别编码序列组成、功能类别、生物物理性质，从而允许对生成序列进行"语义编辑"。 - 增量：在 MT-AAE 的对抗正则化基础上加入解耦损失；通过干预隐空间单维度观察生成序列的生物学特征变化。 - 初步方法：训练后对隐空间各维度做干预实验（如固定某维度为高/低值，观察生成序列的 GC 含量、motif 组成变化）；计算各维度与生物学特征的互信息。 - 验证方式：解耦分数（如 DCI、β-VAE 指标）；干预实验的生物学合理性。 - 创新状态：unverified

候选 5：长程染色质相互作用的序列生成（LoopGen） - 来源局限/观察：本文处理的是单序列（≤500 bp），而三维基因组的功能元件（如增强子-启动子对）往往需要协同考虑两个远端位点。 - 核心假设：将一对互作位点的序列联合编码，可生成在三维空间中可能发生互作的调控元件对。 - 增量：修改 MT-AAE 为双序列编码器（两个 BiLSTM 共享权重），隐空间拼接后解码生成两个序列；增加互作预测头。 - 初步方法：使用 Hi-C 衍生的显著互作对（如启动子-增强子接触）作为训练数据；生成序列对后验证其 motif 互补性（如 CTCF 位点配对）。 - 验证方式：生成序列对的 predicted 互作强度（用独立模型评估）；与随机配对的对比。 - 创新状态：unverified

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The manuscript addresses a relevant and timely problem in generative genomics: producing synthetic regulatory elements that are not only sequence-valid but also biophysically plausible and functionally classifiable. The proposed MT-AAE architecture is a reasonable synthesis of adversarial training and multi-task learning, and the evaluation framework (TRTS/RTSR, k-mer JSD, GC MAE) is thoughtful. However, the evidence presented is insufficient to fully support the central claims. Key weaknesses include: a small and synthetic Phase I dataset (1,850 sequences) with no clear biological validation; a Phase II dataset that is drastically reduced from 27,346 to 1,910 sequences without adequate justification; a lack of statistical significance testing; and an ablation study that shows severe performance collapse at intermediate stages, raising questions about the stability and robustness of the final model. The interpretability claim is not substantiated with any quantitative or qualitative analysis of the latent space. The cross-species generalization failure, while honestly reported, is framed as expected but undermines the broader utility claim. The manuscript is well-written and structured, but the technical case is not yet established.

  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in computational genomics, synthetic biology, and deep generative modeling would find this work of interest. The problem of generating functional DNA sequences with controlled biophysical properties is directly relevant to designing promoters, enhancers, and other regulatory elements for therapeutic and biotechnological applications. The TRTS/RTSR evaluation protocol and the multi-task regularization approach could be adopted by others working on sequence generation. The work may also appeal to those studying latent space interpretability in biological sequence models.

  • Major strengths 1. The problem is well-motivated and addresses a genuine gap: most generative models for DNA focus on sequence statistics or motif recovery but rarely jointly enforce biophysical constraints and functional class separability. 2. The evaluation protocol is more rigorous than typical in this field, incorporating TRTS/RTSR bidirectional testing, k-mer JSD at multiple orders, GC-content MAE, and class-wise generation ratios to assess mode collapse. 3. The ablation study is a valuable addition, as it systematically isolates the contribution of each component (classification, GC regression, balanced sampling) and reveals non-trivial interactions. 4. The honest reporting of the cross-species generalization failure, including a plausible mechanistic explanation (GC distribution mismatch), is commendable and adds credibility. 5. The writing is clear, and the mathematical formulations (loss functions, metrics) are presented in a reproducible manner.

  • Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness / Validation
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that the MT-AAE "generates variable-length genomic elements with high functional and biophysical fidelity" and that the model "alleviates mode collapse and posterior divergence."
  • Evidence pointer Section "Phase I: Synthetic benchmark for mechanistic validation", Table 2, Table 7, Table 14
  • Concern The Phase I dataset is entirely synthetic (1,850 sequences) with deterministic motif placement. This is a reasonable starting point, but the claim of "high functional fidelity" is only demonstrated on this artificial corpus. The Phase II dataset, which is the only real-world test, is reduced from 27,346 to 1,910 sequences after filtering, and the filtering criteria are not fully described. More critically, the reported TRTS accuracy of 74.7% is not accompanied by any confidence intervals or statistical significance tests against the baselines (VAE: 44.0%, GAN: 41.0%). The shuffled control (50.7%) is also not tested for significance. Without such tests, it is impossible to determine whether the observed improvements are robust or within noise, especially given the small per-class sample sizes (e.g., snoRNA n=3).
  • Why it matters The central claim of the paper is that MT-AAE outperforms existing methods. Without statistical validation, this claim is not established. The small sample sizes for minority classes make the reported recall values (e.g., snoRNA 0.33 at S5) highly unstable and potentially misleading.

  • Resolution test Provide confidence intervals or p-values for the TRTS/RTSR accuracy differences between MT-AAE and each baseline. Report per-class results with error bars. Justify the filtering criteria for the Phase II dataset and describe the final class distribution in detail.

  • Concern ID R1-M2

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness / Ablation interpretation
  • Claim pointer The authors state that "the full MT-AAE training reconciles these objectives, achieving synergistic performance" and that the ablation demonstrates "task interference" that is resolved in the final model.
  • Evidence pointer Section "Ablation study: architectural component contributions", Figure 8, Table 16
  • Concern The ablation results show a dramatic performance collapse at intermediate stages: adding GC regression drops TRTS accuracy from 0.450 to 0.350, and adding balanced sampling further drops it to 0.170. The final model recovers to 0.747. This pattern suggests that the loss landscape is highly unstable and that the final performance may be sensitive to initialization, hyperparameter choices, or training order. The authors provide a post-hoc hypothesis for the GC regression interference but do not test it. Moreover, the per-class recall at S5 shows severe degradation for snoRNA (1.00 to 0.33) and moderate degradation for ncRNA (0.875 to 0.48), which is not fully explained by the "small sample size" argument alone. The claim of "synergistic performance" is not supported by a mechanistic understanding of why the full model succeeds where intermediate stages fail.
  • Why it matters If the final model's performance is contingent on a fragile balancing act that is not well understood, the method is not reproducible or generalizable. The ablation study, as presented, raises more questions than it answers about the robustness of the architecture.

  • Resolution test Perform multiple training runs with different random seeds to assess variance. Provide a more detailed analysis of the loss landscape, including gradient norms and latent space visualizations at each ablation stage. Test the hypothesis that GC regression over-regularizes the latent space by, for example, removing the GC head and instead adding a fixed penalty.

  • Concern ID R1-M3

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Interpretability claim
  • Claim pointer The authors claim "an interpretable latent space representation designed to enhance trust in synthetic sequences for therapeutic use."
  • Evidence pointer Section "Latent space design and regularization", Figure 9
  • Concern The only evidence for interpretability is a t-SNE projection showing clustering by functional class. This is a weak form of interpretability. The authors do not demonstrate that specific latent dimensions correspond to biologically meaningful features (e.g., GC-content, motif presence, or class identity). There is no analysis of what the latent space encodes beyond class separability, nor any comparison to latent spaces of baseline models. The claim that this "enhances trust" is not substantiated.
  • Why it matters Interpretability is a stated contribution and a key differentiator from prior work. Without a concrete demonstration of what the latent dimensions represent and how they can be used for biological insight or quality control, this claim is overstated.

  • Resolution test Provide a systematic analysis of the latent space, such as: (a) correlation of individual latent dimensions with biophysical properties (GC, k-mer frequencies); (b) interpolation experiments showing smooth transitions between functional classes; (c) a comparison of latent space structure with a standard VAE or AAE baseline.

  • Concern ID R1-M4

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Technical soundness / Baseline fairness
  • Claim pointer The authors claim MT-AAE outperforms VAE and GAN baselines, and that the baselines were "implemented using their original published architectures and default hyperparameters without validation-set tuning."
  • Evidence pointer Section "Baseline models for comparison", Table 14
  • Concern The baseline comparison is potentially unfair. The MT-AAE's hyperparameters were tuned via grid search on the validation set (Table 6), while the baselines were not tuned at all. This is a classic "tuned model vs. untuned baseline" comparison, which can inflate the apparent improvement. Additionally, the WGAN-GP baseline is reported with a TRTS accuracy of 0.21, which is below the shuffled control (0.507). This is suspicious and suggests the WGAN-GP may have been poorly configured or undertrained. The authors do not provide training curves or convergence diagnostics for any of the baselines.
  • Why it matters The claim of superiority over baselines is a core contribution. An unfair comparison undermines the validity of this claim and the practical value of the proposed method.

  • Resolution test Either tune the baselines using the same validation-based grid search procedure, or clearly state that the comparison is against default-configuration baselines and discuss the implications. Provide training curves and final loss values for all models to demonstrate convergence.

  • Concern ID R1-M5

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Scientific importance / Generalizability
  • Claim pointer The authors state that the model "does not generalise to Mus musculus sequences" and frame this as expected due to species-specific regulatory grammar.
  • Evidence pointer Section "Cross-species generalisation assessment", Figure 10
  • Concern The cross-species failure is reported as a limitation, but it also reveals a fundamental constraint of the approach: the model learns species-specific and potentially dataset-specific biases. The authors suggest future work on multi-species training, but this is not explored. The claim that the model is useful for "data augmentation for underrepresented functional classes" is questionable if the model cannot generalize across species or tissue types. The GC distribution mismatch explanation is plausible but not tested (e.g., by retraining on a GC-matched subset of mouse data).
  • Why it matters The practical utility of the model depends on its ability to generate useful sequences beyond the exact training distribution. The reported failure mode is significant and deserves deeper investigation, not just a forward-looking statement.

  • Resolution test Conduct a controlled experiment to test the GC-mismatch hypothesis, such as training on a GC-matched subset of mouse sequences or applying a domain adaptation technique. Discuss the implications for the claimed utility in data augmentation.

Risk / unsupported claims

  • The claim that the MT-AAE "alleviates mode collapse and posterior divergence" is not directly tested. The generation ratio analysis is indirect evidence and does not measure mode collapse in the latent space.
  • The claim of "interpretable latent space" is unsupported. No analysis of latent dimensions or their biological meaning is provided.
  • The claim that the model "generates variable-length genomic elements" is misleading, as the architecture operates on fixed-length (363 bp) sequences with a normalization strategy, not native variable-length generation.
  • The reported TRTS accuracy of 74.7% is presented as a single point estimate without variance or significance testing, making it impossible to assess reliability.
  • The cross-species generalization failure is reported, but the claim that this is "expected" is not supported by any comparative analysis with other models or species.
  • The statement that the model "preserves biophysical properties" is based on GC-content and k-mer JSD, which are necessary but not sufficient indicators of biophysical plausibility. No thermodynamic or structural validation is provided.
