Identification of ageing-related genes in prostate cancer with type 2 Diabetes using machine learning: a multi-omics study focusing on SOX4, PRNP, and FOXO4.

Zhang, Wei; Zhang, Xueqian; Zhang, Chenghao; Shi, Xinyang; Deng, Yiheng; Gao, Yan; Bi, Kaipeng; Wang, Zheng; Chen, Guanqiu; Ye, Zhoujie; An, Ziyan; Fu, Weijun
Artificial cells, nanomedicine, and biotechnology · 2026-10-29 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究用机器学习筛选前列腺癌与2型糖尿病及衰老相关的诊断基因标志物（SOX4、PRNP、FOXO4），属临床生物标志物与转录组分析，不涉及染色质三维构架或分子模拟建模，与本课题无关。

摘要

Prostate cancer (PCa) demonstrates complex associations with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) and ageing, though their interconnected molecular mechanisms remain poorly defined. This study aimed to identify and validate diagnostic biomarkers at the intersection of these pathways to enable early detection and risk stratification in high-risk populations. Bioinformatic analysis was performed on PCa and T2DM transcriptomic data downloaded from the GEO database, followed by differential expression analysis, weighted gene co-expression network analysis (WGCNA), and functional enrichment. Eight machine learning algorithms were applied to identify hub ageing-related genes (ARGs), with SHAP analysis interpreting feature importance. A nomogram model was constructed and validated across multiple cohorts. Immune infiltration, drug sensitivity, single-cell RNA sequencing, and spatial transcriptomics were used for mechanistic exploration. Three key ARGs (SOX4, PRNP, and FOXO4) were identified and used to develop a diagnostic model with high accuracy (AUCs: 0.726-0.905). SOX4 promoted immune evasion, PRNP exhibited context-dependent duality, and FOXO4 functioned as a tumor suppressor. Spatial and single-cell analyses revealed cell-type-specific expression and fibroblast-epithelial communication via MIF, LRP1, and FZD1. Drug sensitivity profiling identified abiraterone and docetaxel as potential therapeutics for high- and low-risk patients, respectively. In conclusion, SOX4, PRNP, and FOXO4 are key biomarkers linking PCa, T2DM, and ageing, and the diagnostic model shows robust performance and clinical utility, providing insights into microenvironment-driven mechanisms and personalized treatment strategies for PCa patients with T2DM.

摘要翻译

前列腺癌（PCa）与2型糖尿病（T2DM）及衰老之间表现出复杂的关联，尽管其相互关联的分子机制仍不明确。本研究旨在识别并验证这些通路交叉点处的诊断生物标志物，以实现高危人群的早期检测和风险分层。对从GEO数据库下载的PCa和T2DM转录组数据进行了生物信息学分析，随后进行了差异表达分析、加权基因共表达网络分析（WGCNA）和功能富集分析。应用八种机器学习算法识别核心衰老相关基因（ARGs），并通过SHAP分析解释特征重要性。构建了一个列线图模型，并在多个队列中进行了验证。使用免疫浸润、药物敏感性、单细胞RNA测序和空间转录组学进行机制探索。识别出三个关键ARGs（SOX4、PRNP和FOXO4），并用于开发具有高准确性的诊断模型（AUCs：0.726-0.905）。SOX4促进免疫逃逸，PRNP表现出情境依赖性双重作用，而FOXO4作为肿瘤抑制因子发挥作用。空间和单细胞分析揭示了细胞类型特异性表达以及通过MIF、LRP1和FZD1介导的成纤维细胞-上皮细胞通讯。药物敏感性分析确定阿比特龙和多西他赛分别作为高风险和低风险患者的潜在治疗药物。总之，SOX4、PRNP和FOXO4是连接PCa、T2DM和衰老的关键生物标志物，该诊断模型显示出稳健的性能和临床实用性，为伴有T2DM的PCa患者提供了微环境驱动机制和个性化治疗策略的见解。
