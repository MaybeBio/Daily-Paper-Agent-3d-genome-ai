Deep Learning-Guided Identification and In Vivo Validation of Compact Cis-Regulatory Elements for the Zebrafish Habenula.

Li, Zeran; Liu, Shanshan; Zhang, Quan; Zhang, Cuizhen; Peng, Gang
Genes · 2026-09-26 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用深度学习模型（Enformer变体）跨物种预测并验证斑马鱼缰核的紧凑顺式调控元件，虽涉及染色质可及性建模，但核心是调控元件鉴定而非三维基因组/染色质空间架构建模，方法可迁移性有限。

摘要

BACKGROUND/OBJECTIVES: Precise genetic access to the zebrafish habenula remains limited by a scarcity of compact, sequence-defined cis-regulatory elements (CREs). Here, we integrated developmental expression mapping, deep-learning predictions on long-range sequences, and in vivo reporter assays to identify compact regulatory sequences driving habenular expression. METHODS: Using a transgenic zebrafish line enriched for habenular reporter expression, we isolated GFP-positive cells from larval brains and profiled their transcriptomes via microarray. A subset of candidate genes enriched in this dataset was validated using whole-mount in situ hybridization across two developmental stages. This analysis identified genes with highly reproducible habenular expression, leading to the selection of the gng8 and ano2 loci for subsequent CRE characterization. We developed ZEN-former (Zebrafish EN-former), an Enformer-based sequence-to-function model trained on neuronal subclass chromatin accessibility profiles from the adult mouse brain. RESULTS: The model demonstrated strong correlation between predicted and experimentally measured signals across held-out genomic regions. To prioritize regulatory candidates, we integrated ZEN-former predictions with available zebrafish ATAC-seq data, RepeatMasker annotations, and gene models, identifying two ~600 bp intervals at each gene locus. These selected intervals were combined to generate ~1.2 kb reporter constructs for gng8 and ano2 loci, which were then evaluated using Tol2 transposon mediated transgenesis assays in zebrafish. In transiently injected larvae, both constructs successfully drove reporter expression in the habenular region. Furthermore, the resulting stable transgenic lines displayed highly specific and reproducible habenular expression. Quantitative confocal analysis showed mean habenular labeling completeness values of 84.5% and 96.2% for the gng8- and ano2-derived lines, respectively. CONCLUSIONS: Together, these findings provide a proof of concept that sequence features learned from mammalian chromatin accessibility datasets can effectively guide the prioritization of functional regulatory elements across species in zebrafish. The compact regulatory constructs and stable transgenic lines generated here offer robust genetic tools for investigating habenular circuitry.

摘要翻译

背景/目标：对斑马鱼缰核的精准遗传操作仍受限于缺乏紧凑、序列定义的顺式调控元件（cis-regulatory elements, CREs）。在此，我们整合了发育表达图谱、长程序列的深度学习预测以及体内报告基因检测，以识别驱动缰核表达的紧凑调控序列。方法：利用富含缰核报告基因表达的转基因斑马鱼品系，我们从幼鱼脑中分离出GFP阳性细胞，并通过微阵列分析其转录组。该数据集中富集的一部分候选基因通过两个发育阶段的整封原位杂交进行了验证。此分析识别出具有高度可重复缰核表达的基因，从而选择gng8和ano2位点进行后续CRE表征。我们开发了ZEN-former（Zebrafish EN-former），一种基于Enformer的序列-功能模型，该模型在成年小鼠大脑的神经元亚类染色质可及性谱上进行了训练。结果：该模型在保留的基因组区域中，预测信号与实验测量信号之间表现出强相关性。为优先筛选调控候选元件，我们将ZEN-former预测与可用的斑马鱼ATAC-seq数据、RepeatMasker注释和基因模型相结合，在每个基因位点识别出两个约600 bp的区间。这些选定的区间被组合生成约1.2 kb的报告基因构建体，用于gng8和ano2位点，随后通过Tol2转座子介导的转基因检测在斑马鱼中进行了评估。在瞬时注射的幼鱼中，两种构建体均成功驱动了缰核区域的报告基因表达。此外，所得稳定转基因品系表现出高度特异且可重复的缰核表达。定量共聚焦分析显示，gng8和ano2衍生品系的平均缰核标记完整度分别为84.5%和96.2%。结论：综合来看，这些发现提供了概念验证，表明从哺乳动物染色质可及性数据集中学习到的序列特征可有效指导跨物种斑马鱼中功能性调控元件的优先筛选。此处生成的紧凑调控构建体和稳定转基因品系为研究缰核回路提供了稳健的遗传工具。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：斑马鱼缰核缺乏紧凑、序列定义的cis-regulatory elements（CREs），限制了对该脑区的遗传学操作。
  • 为什么重要：缰核是保守的进化古老脑区，参与奖赏、厌恶、成瘾等行为调控；精确的遗传工具是解析其环路功能的前提。
  • 现有方法为何不足：传统增强子发现依赖大规模随机转基因筛选或保守性分析，效率低、周期长；且斑马鱼中已知的缰核CREs数量稀少，缺乏系统性的序列特征注释。
  • 精确研究问题：Can sequence features learned from mammalian chromatin accessibility data (via Enformer) generalize across species to prioritize functional cis-regulatory elements in zebrafish?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：训练一个Enformer架构的模型（ZEN-former）在成年小鼠脑神经亚类染色质可及性数据上，然后直接应用于斑马鱼基因组序列，预测候选CREs；结合斑马鱼ATAC-seq、RepeatMasker和基因模型进行候选区间筛选；最后用Tol2转基因报告实验验证。

  2. 核心洞察：染色质可及性的序列决定因素在哺乳动物与鱼类之间可能高度保守——即「序列语法」（sequence grammar）的进化保守性足以支撑跨物种预测。作者通过实验证明这一假设在缰核CREs上成立。

  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：深度学习模型学到的序列特征可能比我们预期的更跨物种保守；「训练于一个物种、应用于另一个物种」的迁移策略，在调控元件发现中可能比「同物种从头训练」更高效——因为高质量染色质数据在模式生物（小鼠）中远丰富于非模式生物（斑马鱼）。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：斑马鱼基因组序列（候选基因座区域）；小鼠脑神经亚类染色质可及性数据（训练用）
  • 输出：预测的染色质可及性信号谱；优先化的候选CRE区间（~600 bp）
  • 模块： 1. 表达谱筛选：转基因缰核富集报告系 → FACS分离GFP+细胞 → microarray转录组 2. 原位杂交验证：两个发育阶段，确认候选基因的缰核表达 3. ZEN-former训练：Enformer架构，小鼠脑神经亚类ATAC数据 4. 候选区间整合：ZEN-former预测 + 斑马鱼ATAC-seq + RepeatMasker + 基因模型 5. 报告构建体：~1.2 kb（两个~600 bp区间合并）→ Tol2转基因 6. 验证：瞬时注射 + 稳定系；共聚焦定量标记完整性
  • 训练：ZEN-former在成年小鼠脑神经亚类染色质可及性谱上训练（具体训练细节未提供）
  • 假设：跨物种序列-功能关系保守；斑马鱼ATAC-seq峰与ZEN-former预测的叠加可富集功能性CREs
  • 文字流程：缰核富集转基因系 → FACS+microarray → 候选基因列表 → 原位杂交确认 → 选择gng8和ano2 → ZEN-former预测+ATAC-seq整合 → 各选两个~600 bp区间 → 合并为~1.2 kb构建体 → Tol2转基因 → 瞬时+稳定系验证 → 共聚焦定量

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：本文仅针对斑马鱼缰核的两个基因座（gng8、ano2），不声称覆盖全基因组或所有脑区。
  • oracle/真值输入：无oracle；真值为转基因报告表达。
  • 端到端状态：从序列预测到体内验证的完整流程已跑通，但仅验证了2个位点，样本量极小。
  • 算力成本：未提供训练ZEN-former的计算资源信息。
  • 历史数据依赖：ZEN-former依赖小鼠脑神经亚类ATAC数据；斑马鱼ATAC-seq为辅助筛选。
  • 模型依赖：依赖Enformer架构的跨物种泛化能力；若序列语法在更远物种间不保守，此策略可能失效。
  • 最难情形：未测试非缰核脑区或非神经组织；未测试更紧凑（<600 bp）的区间。
  • 群体/领域边界：结论限于斑马鱼缰核；跨物种外推需谨慎。
  • 不确定性：标记完整性数值来自稳定系，但未报告生物学重复数、变异范围或统计检验。
  • 有边界的复述：在斑马鱼gng8和ano2位点，ZEN-former预测结合ATAC-seq筛选出的~1.2 kb区间能够驱动缰核特异报告表达；此结果支持「哺乳动物训练的序列模型可跨物种优先化鱼类CREs」这一假设，但证据强度受限于位点数量和缺乏阴性对照。

14 Agent 提炼的知识候选

  1. 跨物种迁移的序列-功能建模：ZEN-former展示了「在数据丰富的物种（小鼠）上训练、在数据稀缺物种（斑马鱼）上应用」的策略。可迁移至三维基因组：在人类/小鼠Hi-C数据上训练的模型，可能预测斑马鱼或其他物种的TAD边界/CTCF loop锚点。
  2. 多源数据整合的候选筛选流程：深度学习预测 + 同物种ATAC-seq + 重复序列注释 + 基因模型的叠加策略，可迁移至三维基因组调控元件注释——例如将预测的增强子与Hi-C loop锚点、eQTL共定位。
  3. 紧凑构建体的设计原则：~600 bp区间合并为~1.2 kb构建体，提示「最小调控单元」可能比传统大片段报告构建更精准。对三维基因组课题，可探索「紧凑调控单元」与「三维互作域」的对应关系。
  4. 瞬时+稳定系的两级验证协议：先瞬时注射快速筛选，再稳定系精细验证，可迁移至三维基因组编辑验证（如CRISPR删除候选loop锚点后的表型验证）。
  5. 缰核作为跨物种保守脑区的调控逻辑：缰核在进化中保守，其CREs可能也保守——这提示三维基因组中「保守拓扑关联域（TAD）」与「保守调控元件」可能共进化。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a conceptually interesting cross-species transfer of deep learning predictions from mammalian chromatin accessibility to zebrafish regulatory element discovery. The reported outcomes, particularly the high labeling completeness values, are potentially valuable for the habenula research community. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient detail to evaluate the robustness of the computational pipeline, the rigor of the experimental validations, or the statistical support for the quantitative claims. Several key methodological steps are described only superficially, and the evidence base is too thin to establish the case as presented.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers studying zebrafish neurogenetics, habenular circuitry, and cis-regulatory element biology would find these tools and transgenic lines useful. Additionally, computational biologists working on cross-species transfer of deep learning models for genomics may be interested in the ZEN-former approach as a proof of concept.
  • Major strengths The study addresses a real gap in genetic tools for the zebrafish habenula. The integration of multiple data types (transcriptomics, chromatin accessibility, repeat annotations, gene models) for candidate prioritization is methodologically sensible. The cross-species transfer of deep learning features is an innovative angle with potential broader applicability. The reported stable transgenic lines with high labeling completeness suggest practical utility.

Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer "The model demonstrated strong correlation between predicted and experimentally measured signals across held-out genomic regions."
  • Evidence pointer Abstract, Results section (location not provided)
  • Concern The abstract claims strong correlation but provides no correlation coefficients, no definition of "strong," no description of the held-out regions, and no comparison to baseline or alternative models. The training data are from adult mouse brain neuronal subclasses, yet the validation appears to be on genomic regions that are not clearly specified as mouse or zebrafish.
  • Why it matters Without quantitative metrics and clear validation design, the predictive performance of ZEN-former cannot be evaluated. The cross-species transfer claim depends entirely on this validation being robust and appropriately designed.

  • Resolution test Provide specific correlation values (e.g., Pearson or Spearman rho) with confidence intervals, describe the held-out region selection process, and clarify whether validation was performed on mouse or zebrafish data. Include comparison to a null model or existing tools.

  • Concern ID R1-M2

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Reproducibility and rigor
  • Claim pointer "To prioritize regulatory candidates, we integrated ZEN-former predictions with available zebrafish ATAC-seq data, RepeatMasker annotations, and gene models, identifying two ~600 bp intervals at each gene locus."
  • Evidence pointer Abstract, Methods section (location not provided)
  • Concern The integration process is described in a single sentence. No criteria are given for how ZEN-former scores were weighted against ATAC-seq peaks, how repeat masking influenced interval selection, or why ~600 bp was chosen as the interval size. The selection of gng8 and ano2 from the in situ hybridization screen is also not justified beyond "highly reproducible expression."
  • Why it matters The reproducibility of the candidate selection pipeline is central to the study's utility. If the integration rules are not specified, other researchers cannot apply or adapt the method, and the specificity of the chosen intervals cannot be assessed.

  • Resolution test Provide a detailed step-by-step description of the prioritization algorithm, including thresholds, scoring functions, and decision rules. Justify the interval size and the choice of gng8 and ano2 relative to other candidates.

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer "In transiently injected larvae, both constructs successfully drove reporter expression in the habenular region. Furthermore, the resulting stable transgenic lines displayed highly specific and reproducible habenular expression."
  • Evidence pointer Abstract, Results section (location not provided)
  • Concern The abstract reports transient and stable transgenic outcomes but provides no images, no quantification of specificity (e.g., ectopic expression rates), and no reproducibility metrics across independent injection batches or generations. Reproducibility across animals and generations is essential for a genetic tool.

  • Resolution test Include representative images of reporter expression in transient and stable lines, quantify the proportion of larvae or fish showing the expected pattern, report ectopic expression rates, and describe the number of independent transgenic lines and generations examined.

  • Severity Major

  • Evidence pointer Abstract, Results section (location not provided)
