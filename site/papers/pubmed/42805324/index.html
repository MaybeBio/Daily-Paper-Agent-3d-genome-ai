TAD boundary disruption in Xq27-q28 deletions rewires 3D chromatin architecture and may predispose to primary skewed X chromosome inactivation.

Katoh, Kimiko; Nishide, Hiroyo; Hamaguchi, Yu; Soga, Minami; Aiba, Kaori; Kato, Kagayaki; Tsutsumi, Motosuke; Sakamoto, Joe; Solovieva, Elena; Nemoto, Tomomi; Era, Takumi; Hamada, Michiaki; Uchiyama, Ikuo; Wakamatsu, Nobuaki; Asai, Masato
Journal of molecular biology · 2026-09-28 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究利用深度学习模型Orca预测Xq27-q28缺失导致的TAD边界破坏与neo-TAD形成，并结合Hi-C和3D DNA-FISH验证其对X染色体失活偏斜的影响，直接涉及染色质三维架构建模与调控功能关联。

摘要

In placental females, X chromosome inactivation (XCI) establishes random monoallelic expression as XIST RNA spreads along a pre-existing three-dimensional (3D) chromatin contact network. How structural variants reshape this 3D landscape to influence XCI choice remains unclear. We previously reported two female individuals with overlapping Xq27-q28 deletions but discordant XCI, skewed in one and random in the other. By re-evaluating 17 reported cases, we found that skewing in a subset was not readily explained by classical routes of primary bias from the canonical XCI machinery or by secondary cell selection. Using the deep-learning model Orca, we predicted that the deletion in the skewed case would fuse disparate topologically associating domains (TADs) into a neo-TAD. Analysis of human preimplantation Hi-C data showed that the relevant TAD boundaries were already apparent by the 8-cell stage, before stable XCI. 3D DNA-FISH revealed reduced spatial distances between selected loci, consistent with the Orca-predicted neo-TAD architecture, in patient-derived naive iPSCs and lymphoblastoid cells. Orca-predicted neo-TAD formation was also identified in three of six random-XCI cases, indicating that it is insufficient on its own to bias XCI. We identified 86 candidate Polycomb-associated loci with chromatin features consistent with Polycomb seed sites on the human X chromosome. The skewed case showed the most extensive disruption of these candidate loci, although candidate-locus disruption was also observed in random-XCI cases. We therefore propose a working seed convergence model in which the convergence of predicted neo-TAD formation and extensive disruption of candidate Polycomb-associated loci may predispose to skewed XCI.

摘要翻译

在胎盘雌性个体中，X染色体失活（XCI）通过XIST RNA沿预先存在的三维（3D）染色质接触网络传播，建立随机的单等位基因表达。结构变异如何重塑这一3D景观以影响XCI选择仍不清楚。我们此前报道了两名携带重叠Xq27-q28缺失但XCI不一致的女性个体，其中一例表现为偏斜，另一例为随机。通过重新评估17例已报道病例，我们发现部分病例中的偏斜现象无法轻易通过经典XCI机制的初级偏斜途径或继发性细胞选择来解释。使用深度学习模型Orca，我们预测偏斜病例中的缺失会将不相连的拓扑关联结构域（TADs）融合成一个新TAD（neo-TAD）。对人类植入前Hi-C数据的分析显示，相关TAD边界在8细胞阶段已明显存在，早于稳定XCI的发生。3D DNA-FISH显示，在患者来源的初始态iPSCs和淋巴母细胞中，选定基因座之间的空间距离减小，与Orca预测的新TAD结构一致。在六例随机XCI病例中，有三例也检测到Orca预测的新TAD形成，表明其本身不足以偏倚XCI。我们在人类X染色体上鉴定了86个候选Polycomb相关基因座，其染色质特征与Polycomb种子位点一致。偏斜病例显示出这些候选基因座最广泛的破坏，尽管在随机XCI病例中也观察到候选基因座破坏。因此，我们提出一个工作性的种子汇聚模型，其中预测的新TAD形成与候选Polycomb相关基因座的广泛破坏的汇聚可能易导致XCI偏斜。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：Xq27-q28 缺失如何通过重塑 3D 染色质结构影响 XCI 的选择（偏斜 vs 随机）？结构变异导致的 TAD 边界破坏是否以及如何参与 XCI 偏斜的建立？
  • 为什么重要：XCI 是雌性哺乳动物剂量补偿的核心机制，其选择偏差（skewed XCI）与多种 X-linked 疾病相关。理解结构变异如何通过 3D 基因组层面影响 XCI 选择，有助于解释表型异质性和疾病易感性。
  • 现有方法不足：经典解释（XIST 位点缺失、CTCF 位点破坏、secondary cell selection）无法解释部分病例的偏斜；缺乏将 3D 结构预测与 XCI 机制直接关联的分析框架。
  • 精确研究问题：Can deep-learning-predicted neo-TAD formation, in combination with disruption of candidate Polycomb-associated loci, explain primary skewed XCI in Xq27-q28 deletion carriers?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法： - 对 17 例 Xq27-q28 缺失病例进行表型再分类（skewed vs random XCI） - 使用深度学习模型 Orca 预测缺失导致的 TAD 结构变化 - 用人类着床前胚胎 Hi-C 数据验证 TAD 边界在 XCI 建立前已存在 - 用 3D DNA-FISH 在患者来源细胞中验证空间距离变化 - 系统鉴定 X 染色体上候选 Polycomb 相关位点

2) 核心洞察： - TAD 边界在 8-cell 阶段已建立，早于稳定 XCI——说明 3D 结构是 XCI 的「pre-existing scaffold」而非结果 - 结构变异可通过「TAD 融合」产生 neo-TAD，改变 XIST 扩散的 3D 底物 - 单一结构变化（neo-TAD）不足以决定 XCI 偏斜，需要与 Polycomb 种子位点的广泛破坏「汇聚」 - 提出「seed convergence model」：neo-TAD 形成 + 候选 Polycomb 位点破坏 → 偏斜易感性

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]： - 深度学习 3D 基因组预测可作为「结构变异 → 表型」关联的筛选工具，但需实验验证 - 表型决定因素可能是「多重结构变化的汇聚」而非单一事件 - 早期胚胎 3D 结构的「预建立」特性为理解发育决策提供了时间窗口

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：本文结论限于 Xq27-q28 缺失这一特定结构变异类型，不推广至所有 X 染色体结构变异。
  • oracle/真值输入：Orca 预测基于缺失断点序列，预测结果经 DNA-FISH 部分验证，但未进行全基因组 Hi-C 验证。
  • 端到端状态：非端到端——预测→实验验证→表型关联是分步完成的，且表型关联基于病例再分析（回顾性）。
  • 算力成本：未提供。
  • 历史数据依赖：17 例病例来自文献报道，存在报告偏倚可能。
  • 模型依赖：Orca 预测的准确性依赖模型训练数据分布，对罕见变异可能不准确。
  • 最难情形：随机 XCI 病例中也存在 neo-TAD 形成（3/6），说明预测的结构变化不能单独预测表型。
  • 群体/领域边界：仅限女性 Xq27-q28 缺失携带者；不涉及体细胞变异或男性。
  • 不确定性：样本量小（2 例先证者细胞系）；「predispose」而非「determine」——模型为易感性模型，非决定性机制。
  • 有边界的复述：在 Xq27-q28 缺失的女性携带者中，深度学习预测的 neo-TAD 形成与候选 Polycomb 位点的广泛破坏可能共同增加原发性 XCI 偏斜的风险，但任一因素单独存在（如随机 XCI 病例中的 neo-TAD）不足以决定偏斜表型。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting hypothesis linking structural variant-induced 3D genome reorganization to XCI skewing, but the evidence as summarized is insufficient to establish causality or even a robust association. The working model is speculative and the key claims are supported only by qualitative statements without quantitative support. The manuscript may have merit, but the current abstract does not provide enough detail to assess technical soundness or the strength of the conclusions.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in X chromosome inactivation, 3D chromatin architecture, topologically associating domain biology, and clinical genetics of X-linked structural variants would find this relevant. The potential link between TAD disruption and XCI skewing could inform understanding of variable penetrance in X-linked disorders, which is of broad interest to human geneticists.
  • Major strengths The study addresses an important and under-explored question about how structural variants influence XCI choice through 3D genome organization. The use of multiple complementary approaches (clinical case re-evaluation, deep-learning prediction, preimplantation Hi-C, and DNA-FISH) is commendable. The authors acknowledge that neo-TAD formation alone is insufficient, which shows scientific caution.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer The claim that the deletion in the skewed case would fuse disparate TADs into a neo-TAD, and that this is supported by DNA-FISH showing reduced spatial distances.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract states that 3D DNA-FISH revealed reduced spatial distances between selected loci in patient-derived cells, but provides no quantitative data, no number of cells examined, no statistical comparison to controls, and no information on which loci were selected or how they relate to the predicted neo-TAD.
  • Why it matters Without quantitative evidence and appropriate controls, the DNA-FISH result cannot be distinguished from technical variation or cell-type-specific differences unrelated to the deletion. This is a central piece of evidence for the neo-TAD model.
  • Resolution test Provide quantitative distance measurements with effect sizes, statistical tests, number of nuclei, and appropriate control cell lines. Show that the reduced distances are specific to the predicted neo-TAD region and not a global chromatin compaction effect.
  • Concern ID R1-M2
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Causal inference
  • Claim pointer The proposal that convergence of predicted neo-TAD formation and extensive disruption of candidate Polycomb-associated loci may predispose to skewed XCI.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract reports that three of six random-XCI cases also showed predicted neo-TAD formation, and that candidate-locus disruption was observed in random-XCI cases. This means neither feature is specific to the skewed case. The claim of a "working seed convergence model" is presented without any statistical or probabilistic framework to support the idea that the combination is more than coincidental.
  • Why it matters The core hypothesis of the paper rests on the combination of two features being predictive of skewing, but with only one skewed case and six random cases, the power to establish this is extremely limited. The abstract does not report whether the random-XCI cases with neo-TAD formation also had candidate-locus disruption, which is essential to evaluate the convergence model.
