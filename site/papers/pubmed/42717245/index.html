The periphery of nuclear speckles defines a spatially and temporally regulated compartment of long-lived intron-retained RNAs that resolves during mitosis.

Biayna, Josep; Baranovskii, Artem; Chaudhuri, Anusha; Paladin, Marta; Erdem, Berkay; Keller, Luise-Elektra; Barutcu, A Rasim; Dimmeler, Stefanie; Marsico, Annalisa; Dumbovic, Gabrijela
Nature cell biology · 2026-09-10 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究核斑周边RNA定位与内含子保留的时序调控，虽涉及深度学习预测RNA特征，但对象非染色质三维组织或空间架构，与课题核心方向关联较弱。

摘要

RNA localization adds a fundamental layer to gene expression by determining when and where translation-ready mRNAs become available, yet how this timing is coordinated with nuclear architecture and cell-cycle progression remains unclear. Here we identify a subnuclear RNA niche at the nuclear speckle periphery that couples intron retention to cell-cycle-timed RNA release. Using compartment-resolved transcriptional inhibition, sequence-based deep learning and single-molecule and super-resolution RNA imaging in human pluripotent stem cells, we define a class of nuclear RNAs with long-lived retained introns that persist for hours and are enriched in transcripts encoding regulators of genome maintenance and mitosis, including centromere and kinetochore assembly, DNA repair and telomere maintenance. Long-lived retained introns exhibit elevated GC content, predicted structural stability and enrichment for nuclear speckle-associated RNA-binding proteins. In interphase, these RNAs localize to a distinct nuclear speckle-peripheral RNA niche in a spatial arrangement conserved across cell types. During mitotic remodelling, they undergo coordinated, kinase-dependent splicing and are released into the cytoplasm of early G1 daughter cells. Together, these findings link cis-encoded intronic features, subnuclear organization and mitotic remodelling to temporal control of RNA fate.

摘要翻译

RNA定位通过决定翻译就绪的mRNA何时何地可用，为基因表达增加了基础性层面，然而这一时机如何与核架构和细胞周期进程相协调仍不清楚。在此，我们识别出核斑周边的一个亚核RNA生态位，该生态位将内含子保留与细胞周期定时的RNA释放相耦合。利用区室分辨的转录抑制、基于序列的深度学习以及人类多能干细胞中的单分子和超分辨率RNA成像，我们定义了一类具有长寿命保留内含子的核RNA，这些内含子持续数小时，并富集于编码基因组维持和有丝分裂调控因子的转录本中，包括着丝粒和动粒组装、DNA修复和端粒维持。长寿命保留内含子表现出升高的GC含量、预测的结构稳定性以及核斑相关RNA结合蛋白的富集。在间期，这些RNA定位于一个独特的核斑周边RNA生态位，其空间排列在不同细胞类型中保守。在有丝分裂重塑过程中，它们经历协调的激酶依赖性剪接，并在早期G1子细胞中释放到细胞质。总之，这些发现将顺式编码的内含子特征、亚核组织及有丝分裂重塑与RNA命运的时序控制联系起来。
