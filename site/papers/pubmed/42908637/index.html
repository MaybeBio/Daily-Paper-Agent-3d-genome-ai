Sequence-activity mapping and model-guided engineering of the phenol-responsive DmpR-Po sigma(54) promoter.

Xu, Yuxi; Song, Mingxuan; Xing, Wenglong; Han, Jianying; Wu, Jiahao; Cheng, Yi; Liu, Jiayi; Wang, Linfeng; Zeng, Ji; Zhao, Yu; Guo, Jinquan; Cai, Shuting
Frontiers in microbiology · 2026-10-23 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文对细菌sigma54依赖型启动子进行序列-活性图谱构建与深度学习预测，虽涉及DNA架构蛋白和长程调控，但对象是原核启动子而非染色质/三维基因组，且无三维空间构象建模，仅对课题有间接方法启发。

摘要

Promoters are core cis-regulatory elements that determine gene expression levels and dynamic responses, and their quantitative engineering directly dictates the performance of synthetic biological systems such as whole-cell biosensors. However, the transcriptional output of complex inducible systems, exemplified by sigma(54)-dependent promoters, relies on long-range coordination among distal bacterial enhancer-binding proteins, DNA architectural proteins, and RNA polymerase. The combined effects of multiple regulatory regions on promoter output remain poorly understood, which limits the cross-background transfer of local sequence rules and hinders the accurate prediction and rational design of promoter activity. The phenol-responsive DmpR-Po promoter serves as both a classical model for investigating sigma(54)-dependent regulation and a high-value sensing element for environmental pollutant detection and high-throughput enzyme activity screening. Resolving sequence-activity relationships across its complete regulatory region therefore holds both mechanistic and practical significance. To overcome the barriers to rational engineering of complex promoters, we established a modular design-measure-model-guide-validate framework targeting the full DmpR-Po regulatory region. A library of 17,500 designed promoter sequences was constructed to systematically perturb the upstream activating sequence (UAS), the IHF2 region, combined UAS-IHF2 mutational backgrounds, and downstream regulatory architecture. Fluorescence-activated cell sorting coupled with sequencing yielded a high-quality sequence-activity map covering 11,940 variants. At the regional level, identical UAS and IHF2 haplotypes generally preserved their relative activity rankings across single-region and joint-variant backgrounds, and the main effects estimated independently for UAS and IHF2 could approximately predict the activity of combined variants. At the nucleotide level, however, the effects of individual substitutions were strongly constrained by the overall DNA architecture and full-sequence context. A deep learning model trained on the complete regulatory sequence captured these context-dependent relationships, achieved robust predictive performance on held-out sequences and a separately transformed and processed Batch 2 derived from the same preconstructed plasmid-library pool, and enabled model-guided prioritization of targeted local variants. Single-clone validation further showed that model-guided edits produced variable experimental outcomes, supporting candidate prioritization while indicating that individual engineering predictions require experimental validation. Collectively, this study characterizes the transferability and context-dependent constraints of regional sequence effects and establishes a data-driven framework for quantitative prediction and model-guided engineering of complex sigma(54)-dependent promoters.

摘要翻译

启动子是决定基因表达水平和动态响应的核心顺式调控元件，其定量工程直接决定全细胞生物传感器等合成生物系统的性能。然而，复杂诱导型系统（以sigma(54)依赖性启动子为例）的转录输出依赖于远端细菌增强子结合蛋白、DNA结构蛋白和RNA聚合酶之间的长程协调。多个调控区域对启动子输出的联合效应仍知之甚少，这限制了局部序列规则在不同背景间的转移，并阻碍了启动子活性的准确预测和合理设计。苯酚响应型DmpR-Po启动子既是研究sigma(54)依赖性调控的经典模型，也是环境污染物检测和高通量酶活性筛选的高价值传感元件。解析其完整调控区域内的序列-活性关系因此具有机制和实践双重意义。为克服复杂启动子合理工程的障碍，我们建立了一个针对完整DmpR-Po调控区域的模块化设计-测量-建模-指导-验证框架。构建了一个包含17,500个设计启动子序列的文库，系统性地扰动上游激活序列（UAS）、IHF2区域、UAS-IHF2联合突变背景以及下游调控架构。荧光激活细胞分选结合测序获得了覆盖11,940个变体的高质量序列-活性图谱。在区域水平上，相同的UAS和IHF2单倍型在单区域和联合变体背景中通常保持其相对活性排名，且独立估计的UAS和IHF2主效应可近似预测联合变体的活性。然而，在核苷酸水平上，单个替换的影响受到整体DNA架构和全序列背景的强烈约束。基于完整调控序列训练的深度学习模型捕捉了这些背景依赖性关系，在留出序列以及来自同一预构建质粒文库池的独立转化和处理的第二批样本上实现了稳健的预测性能，并实现了模型指导的靶向局部变体优先级排序。单克隆验证进一步表明，模型指导的编辑产生了可变的实验结果，支持候选优先级排序，同时表明个体工程预测需要实验验证。总体而言，本研究表征了区域序列效应的可转移性和背景依赖性约束，并建立了一个数据驱动的框架，用于复杂sigma(54)依赖性启动子的定量预测和模型指导工程。
