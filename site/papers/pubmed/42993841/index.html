Explainable AI identifies recombination and chromatin environment as key predictors of subgenome evolution in maize and Brassica.

Schuster, Layla A; Liu, Beibei; Silva, Jose Cleydson F; Cheng, Xi; Dias, Raquel; Zhao, Meixia
Science advances · 2026-11-06 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用可解释AI预测多倍体亚基因组分化，涉及染色质环境特征但非三维空间架构建模，方法可迁移性有限。

摘要

Polyploidy, or whole genome duplication, reshapes genomes through biased gene loss and regulatory rewiring, yet the drivers of biased fractionation among subgenomes remain unclear. Using maize and Brassica rapa as model allopolyploids, we compiled 60 genomic and epigenomic features in maize and 45 in B. rapa and constructed supervised machine learning models to classify genes by subgenome identity. In both species, eXplainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) approaches identified recombination rate as the most influential and highly interconnected feature despite nonsignificant mean differences between subgenome groups. Chromosome location, transposon density, and chromatin-associated features, including the proximity of accessible chromatin regions to genes and active histone marks such as H3K9ac and H3K27ac, consistently ranked among the top contributors to subgenome classification. XAI-derived covariation and interaction networks further revealed recombination rate as the central node connecting significant features. Together, these results highlight recombination and chromatin environment as major predictors of subgenome divergence in polyploid genomes.

摘要翻译

多倍化，即全基因组复制，通过偏向性基因丢失和调控重连重塑基因组，然而亚基因组间偏向性分化的驱动因素仍不清楚。以玉米和白菜作为异源多倍体模型，我们汇编了玉米中的60个基因组和表观基因组特征以及白菜中的45个特征，并构建了监督式机器学习模型，以根据亚基因组身份对基因进行分类。在这两个物种中，可解释人工智能（eXplainable Artificial Intelligence, XAI）方法识别出重组率是最具影响力且高度互联的特征，尽管亚基因组组间均值差异不显著。染色体位置、转座子密度以及染色质相关特征，包括可及染色质区域与基因的邻近性以及活性组蛋白标记如H3K9ac和H3K27ac，始终位列亚基因组分类的主要贡献因素之中。XAI衍生的协变和交互网络进一步揭示重组率是连接显著特征的中心节点。综合来看，这些结果强调重组和染色质环境是多倍体基因组中亚基因组分歧的主要预测因子。
