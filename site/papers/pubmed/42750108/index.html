Molecular Bases and Genetic Design of Rice Disease Resistance for Optimized Yield and Sustainable Agriculture.

Hou, Xinyue; Cui, Yuanjiang; Ding, Chaoqing; Qian, Qian; Ren, Deyong
Advanced science (Weinheim, Baden-Wurttemberg, Germany) · 2026-09-17 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文综述水稻抗病性的分子基础与遗传设计育种策略，与三维基因组/染色质组织及AI/分子模拟建模完全无关。

摘要

Rice diseases continue to undermine yield stability and threaten the sustainability of rice production. The central challenge is therefore not simply to maximize immune activation, but to identify genetic interventions that remain effective across diverse pathogen races and environmental conditions without imposing excessive penalties on growth or yield. Here, we synthesize the molecular basis of rice immunity from a design-oriented perspective. We first examine cell-surface pattern-recognition receptors and intracellular nucleotide-binding leucine-rich repeat receptors, and then assess the shared signaling hubs and defence outputs that connect pathogen perception to antimicrobial responses. Rather than treating these components as equivalent breeding targets, we compare their translational potential according to resistance spectrum, anticipated durability, tunability, pleiotropic risk, and the strength of field evidence. We further discuss breeding strategies based on receptor engineering, editing of susceptibility genes and cis-regulatory elements, post-translational motif engineering, pathogen-inducible and upstream open reading frame-mediated regulation, resistance-gene stacking and artificial intelligence-assisted prediction. We argue that rational resistance design in rice should move beyond constitutive immune activation toward allele-specific, quantitative, spatially restricted and infection-responsive regulation. Integrating mechanistic insights with precision genome editing, accelerated breeding and responsible deployment offers a practical route to durable, yield-compatible disease resistance while reducing dependence on chemical control.

摘要翻译

水稻病害持续削弱产量稳定性，并威胁水稻生产的可持续性。因此，核心挑战不仅在于最大化免疫激活，更在于识别那些在不同病原菌小种和环境条件下仍保持有效、且不对生长或产量造成过度代价的遗传干预措施。在此，我们从设计导向的视角综合阐述水稻免疫的分子基础。我们首先考察细胞表面模式识别受体和细胞内核苷酸结合亮氨酸富集重复受体，然后评估连接病原感知与抗菌反应的共享信号枢纽和防御输出。我们并未将这些组分视为等同的育种靶标，而是根据抗性谱、预期持久性、可调性、多效性风险及田间证据强度，比较其转化潜力。我们进一步讨论基于受体工程、感病基因和顺式调控元件编辑、翻译后基序工程、病原诱导及上游开放阅读框介导的调控、抗性基因聚合和人工智能辅助预测的育种策略。我们认为，水稻的理性抗性设计应超越组成型免疫激活，转向等位基因特异性、定量、空间受限及感染响应性的调控。将机制洞见与精准基因组编辑、加速育种和负责任部署相结合，为持久且产量兼容的病害抗性提供了实用路径，同时减少对化学防治的依赖。
