Decoding Conformational Dynamics and Hyperactivity of Histone H3K36 N-Methyltransferase in Oncogenic Mutations via tICA and Markov State Modeling.

Shah, Tejas; Heidari, Sahar; Rydzewski, Jakub; Torabifard, Hedieh
Journal of chemical information and modeling · 2026-10-26 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究通过全原子分子动力学模拟、tICA和马尔可夫状态模型，揭示NSD2组蛋白甲基转移酶致癌突变通过构象动力学重排增强催化活性的机制，涉及染色质修饰酶与核小体相互作用，但未直接建模染色质三维空间架构，方法可迁移至染色质相关蛋白的构象动力学研究。

摘要

NSD2 is a histone methyltransferase that modifies lysine 36 in histone H3 (H3K36), playing a central role in chromatin organization and transcriptional regulation. Oncogenic mutations, such as E1099K and T1150A in NSD2, have been associated with hyperactive methylation, but the molecular mechanisms underlying this gain of function remain poorly understood. In this study, we performed all-atom molecular dynamics simulations of NSD2 bound to the nucleosome in wild type (WT), E1099K, T1150A, and the E1099K/T1150A double mutant. Analysis of MD simulations reveals that the global dynamics of the enzymes remain unaltered upon mutations. The time-lagged independent component analysis and Markov state modeling uncovered fundamental differences in free-energy landscapes among the variants. The WT NSD2 exhibited energetically and kinetically unfavorable transitions between the macrostates along with extended enzyme-substrate distances. On the other hand, the mutant systems demonstrate reduced SAM-H3K36 distances with modified energy landscapes that facilitate transitions or favor prolonged occupancy of catalytically competent states. Importantly, the mutations reorganize the network of intramolecular contacts around the catalytic site, SAM-binding pocket, and histone-binding interface, optimizing substance engagement geometry. These findings are consistent with experimental observations of enhanced catalytic activity and suggest that oncogenic mutations promote hyperactivity through strategic reorganization of conformational dynamics rather than simple destabilization, balancing local flexibility with global stability to enhance catalytic efficiency. Our results provide mechanistic insights into NSD2 dysregulation in cancer and establish a framework to develop novel therapeutics that target the enzyme's dynamic landscape.

摘要翻译

NSD2是一种组蛋白甲基转移酶，修饰组蛋白H3中的第36位赖氨酸（H3K36），在染色质组织和转录调控中发挥核心作用。致癌突变，如NSD2中的E1099K和T1150A，与过度活跃的甲基化相关，但这一功能获得背后的分子机制仍知之甚少。在本研究中，我们对野生型（WT）、E1099K、T1150A以及E1099K/T1150A双突变体中与核小体结合的NSD2进行了全原子分子动力学模拟。MD模拟分析显示，突变后酶的全局动力学保持不变。时间滞后独立成分分析和马尔可夫状态建模揭示了各变体之间自由能景观的根本差异。WT NSD2在大状态之间的转变在能量和动力学上均不利，且酶-底物距离延长。另一方面，突变系统表现出减小的SAM-H3K36距离，并具有修饰的能量景观，促进转变或有利于催化活性状态的长时间占据。重要的是，突变重组了催化位点、SAM结合口袋和组蛋白结合界面周围分子内接触的网络，优化了底物结合几何结构。这些发现与增强催化活性的实验观察一致，并表明致癌突变通过构象动力学的战略性重组而非简单去稳定化来促进过度活跃，平衡局部灵活性与全局稳定性以增强催化效率。我们的结果为癌症中NSD2失调提供了机制性见解，并建立了开发靶向该酶动态景观的新型治疗策略的框架。

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法

对NSD2-核小体复合物进行全原子MD模拟（WT、E1099K、T1150A、双突变），使用tICA降维提取慢变量，构建MSM分析自由能景观、宏观态分布与转换动力学。

2) 核心洞察

致癌突变并非通过改变酶的全局动态或静态结构来增强活性，而是通过局部接触网络重组（催化位点、SAM结合口袋、组蛋白结合界面）来重塑自由能景观，使得系统更易进入或更久停留于催化活性构象，同时保持整体结构的稳定性。这是一种「局部灵活性-全局稳定性」的平衡策略。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]

  • 对于酶功能获得性突变，构象动态的重组可能比静态结构变化更关键；研究突变效应时，应关注自由能景观与动力学而非仅比较静态结构。
  • tICA + MSM 是解析「动态表型」的通用框架，可迁移至其他染色质相关蛋白（如修饰酶、重塑复合物、结构蛋白）的突变机制研究。
  • 「局部接触网络」作为突变效应的传播媒介，可能是一个普遍机制：突变通过改变远端残基间的协同运动来影响活性位点。

  3. 底物距离作为活性指标：SAM-H3K36距离作为催化活性的动态指标，类似地，在染色质研究中可用「DNA结合位点距离」「核小体结合界面距离」作为功能指标。
  4. 多突变体系统比较设计：WT + 单突变 + 双突变的体系设计可迁移至染色质蛋白突变研究，用于解析突变协同效应。
  5. 自由能景观差异作为功能预测器：通过比较WT与突变体的自由能景观，可预测突变的功能影响（增强/减弱），这一思路可用于染色质蛋白突变的功能注释。

15 与已有知识连接

  • NSD2/MMSET 与癌症：NSD2（WHSC1/MMSET）突变在多发性骨髓瘤t(4;14)易位中高表达，E1099K为已知激活突变（外部知识：Kuo et al., Nature 2011 等），本文的计算结果与实验活性增强一致。
  • H3K36甲基化与染色质：H3K36me2/3与转录延伸、DNA修复、染色质重塑相关（外部知识：Wagner & Carpenter, Nat Rev Mol Cell Biol 2012），本文为理解H3K36甲基化失调如何影响染色质状态提供分子基础。
  • MD + MSM 在表观遗传酶中的应用：类似方法已用于其他组蛋白修饰酶（如PRC2、DOT1L）的构象动态研究（外部知识：Zhang et al., JACS 2021等），本文是该方法在NSD2上的应用。
  • 与三维基因组的连接：H3K36me2/3与染色质区室化、TAD边界维持相关（外部知识：Leung et al., Nat Struct Mol Biol 2021），NSD2活性变化可能通过改变H3K36修饰状态影响三维基因组组织——本文未直接研究此连接，但为后续研究提供起点。

  • Assessment boundary Claims and evidence as presented in the abstract; no full text, figures, tables, or supplementary materials were provided
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors report all-atom molecular dynamics simulations of NSD2 bound to the nucleosome for wild type, E1099K, T1150A, and the double mutant. They claim that global enzyme dynamics are unaltered by mutations, but time-lagged independent component analysis and Markov state modeling reveal differences in free-energy landscapes, reduced SAM-H3K36 distances, and reorganized intramolecular contact networks in mutants, consistent with experimentally observed hyperactive methylation.

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a plausible mechanistic hypothesis linking oncogenic NSD2 mutations to hyperactive methylation via altered conformational dynamics. However, the evidence base is limited to a summary, and several claims are either under-specified or not verifiable from the supplied material. The central conclusion that mutations reorganize conformational dynamics to enhance catalytic efficiency is interesting but requires substantial technical validation before the case is established.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in epigenetic regulation, cancer biology, and computational biophysics would be interested. The work addresses a clinically relevant question about how specific oncogenic mutations alter enzyme function, and the proposed dynamic-based mechanism could inform drug discovery targeting NSD2. The methodological approach using tICA and Markov state modeling may also appeal to computational chemists studying conformational ensembles of large protein-nucleosome complexes.
  • Major strengths The study addresses a biologically and clinically important question with a sophisticated computational approach. The use of multiple mutants including a double mutant is a strength. The claim that mutations act through reorganization of conformational dynamics rather than simple destabilization is a nuanced and potentially valuable insight.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that tICA and Markov state modeling uncovered fundamental differences in free-energy landscapes among the variants.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
  • Concern No details are provided on the tICA implementation, number of features, lag times, or the construction and validation of the Markov state models. Without these, the reliability of the free-energy landscapes and transition analyses cannot be assessed.
  • Why it matters Markov state models are highly sensitive to feature selection, lag time, and discretization. Poor choices can produce artifactual macrostates and misleading free-energy differences. The central mechanistic claim depends entirely on the validity of these models.
  • Resolution test Provide tICA eigenvalue plots, implied timescale tests, Chapman-Kolmogorov validation, and a clear description of the discretization scheme and model selection criteria.
  • Concern ID R1-M2
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that global dynamics of the enzymes remain unaltered upon mutations.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract states that global dynamics are unaltered, but no metric is defined for "global dynamics." It is unclear whether this refers to root-mean-square deviation, root-mean-square fluctuation, principal component analysis, or another measure. The claim is also in tension with the later statement that mutations reorganize contact networks and alter free-energy landscapes.
  • Why it matters If global dynamics are truly unaltered, this is a strong and non-obvious claim that requires quantitative support. If the claim is based on a limited metric, it may be misleading.
