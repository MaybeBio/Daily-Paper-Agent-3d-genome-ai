A microexon regulatory code reveals autism-linked genetic variation.

Parada, Guillermo E; Bretschneider, Hannes; Li, Jack Daiyang; Fine, Jacob L; Dupas, Steven J; Ellis, Jonathan D; Braunschweig, Ulrich; Bonnal, Sophie; Dalal, Taykhoom; Engchuan, Worrawat; Zarrei, Mehdi; Wei, Wei; Zafar, Nadia; Hemberg, Martin; Attisano, Liliana; Ellis, James; Irimia, Manuel; Wainberg, Michael; Trost, Brett; Scherer, Stephen W; Morris, Quaid D; Blencowe, Benjamin J
Nature · 2026-10-15 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究神经元微外显子剪接调控及其与自闭症遗传变异的关系，虽用机器学习定义序列代码，但对象为RNA剪接而非染色质三维组织，且无空间架构建模，与本课题关联微弱。

摘要

Neuronal microexons are a highly conserved class of alternative splicing events enriched in genes with critical functions in nervous system biology(1,2). Microexon splicing is often disrupted in the brains of autistic individuals, and mice with microexon deficiencies display autism-like phenotypes(1,3,4). However, the full landscape of microexons is not known, and the impact of genetic variation on these exons has not been systematically investigated. Here, through extensive transcriptome profiling, we detect hundreds of new microexons, including many with neuronal subtype-specific splicing patterns, and others that activate nonsense-mediated mRNA decay. Using machine learning, we define a sequence code predictive of neuronally spliced microexons and use this to identify critical regulatory elements surrounding the expanded microexon repertoire. This code further reveals genomic variants predicted to alter microexon splicing in individuals affected by autism, with rare variants having the strongest estimated impact. These variants are mostly detected in idiopathic individuals and are associated with autism status. They also converge on synaptic and other autism-linked genes, as well as genes not previously linked to autism. Our results collectively define an expanded repertoire of microexons, delineate their regulatory code, and pinpoint variants predicted to affect their splicing, with implications for the disruption of functions central to neuronal biology and brain disorders.

摘要翻译

神经元微外显子（neuronal microexons）是一类高度保守的可变剪接事件，富集于在神经系统生物学中具有关键功能的基因中(1,2)。微外显子剪接在自闭症个体的大脑中常被破坏，且微外显子缺陷的小鼠表现出自闭症样表型(1,3,4)。然而，微外显子的完整图谱尚不明确，遗传变异对这些外显子的影响也未被系统研究。在此，通过广泛的转录组分析，我们检测到数百个新的微外显子，包括许多具有神经元亚型特异性剪接模式的微外显子，以及其他激活无义介导的mRNA降解（nonsense-mediated mRNA decay）的微外显子。利用机器学习，我们定义了一个可预测神经元剪接微外显子的序列代码，并以此识别围绕扩展微外显子库的关键调控元件。该代码进一步揭示了预测会改变自闭症个体中微外显子剪接的基因组变异，其中稀有变异具有最强的估计影响。这些变异主要见于特发性个体，并与自闭症状态相关。它们还汇聚于突触及其他自闭症相关基因，以及先前未与自闭症关联的基因。我们的结果共同定义了扩展的微外显子库，描绘了其调控代码，并精确定位了预测影响其剪接的变异，这对神经元生物学和脑部疾病中核心功能的破坏具有意义。
