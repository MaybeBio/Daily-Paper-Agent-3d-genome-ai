Identification and expression analysis of intergenic long non-coding RNAs (lincRNA) associated with stripe rust resistance in wheat.

Fang, Xiaole; Wang, Xiaoyu; Yang, Tian; Wang, Youning; Ao, Xue; Cao, Peina; Gong, Pan; Ma, Dongfang
Frontiers in plant science · 2026-11-05 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文鉴定小麦条锈病抗性相关lincRNA并预测其与蛋白的相互作用，虽用AlphaFold做结构预测，但对象非染色质三维组织，且无AI/分子模拟建模染色质架构，仅边缘相关。

摘要

INTRODUCTION: Long intergenic non-coding RNAs (lincRNAs) have emerged as important regulators of plant immune responses, yet their functional landscape in wheat (Triticum aestivum) during stripe rust (Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici) infection remains largely unexplored. METHODS: In this study, a genome-wide identification of lincRNAs was performed using strand-specific RNA-seq data from wheat leaves following Pst inoculation. Functional enrichment, co-expression, lincRNA-miRNA-mRNA network prediction, and AlphaFold-based protein-RNA structure prediction were used to characterize candidate lincRNAs. RESULTS: A total of 2,736 expressed lincRNAs were identified, of which 515 were differentially expressed during infection. Functional enrichment of genes located within 100 kb of the differentially expressed lincRNAs revealed significant associations with defense-related pathways, including MAPK signaling and plant-pathogen interaction. Coordinated expression patterns between lincRNAs and adjacent defense-related mRNAs suggested putative cis-regulatory associations. Furthermore, lincRNA-miRNA-mRNA network prediction identified 65 differentially expressed lincRNAs as potential endogenous target mimics for 21 conserved miRNAs, revealing a widespread competing endogenous RNA (ceRNA) regulatory network. Importantly, AlphaFold-based protein-RNA structure prediction demonstrated that the co-expressed lincRNA MSTRG.56980 forms a stable complex with the RGA1-type resistance protein TraesCS6B03G0179100. DISCUSSION: This provides a first computational prediction of a possible physical interaction between a wheat lincRNA and an immune receptor. These integrative findings substantially expand the current understanding of non-coding regulatory networks in wheat immunity and provide a foundational resource for prioritizing candidate lincRNAs as targets for enhancing stripe rust resistance.

摘要翻译

引言：长基因间非编码RNA（lincRNAs）已成为植物免疫反应的重要调控因子，然而其在小麦（Triticum aestivum）应对条锈病（Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici）感染过程中的功能景观仍 largely 未被探索。方法：本研究利用条锈菌（Pst）接种后小麦叶片的链特异性RNA-seq数据，进行了全基因组范围的lincRNAs鉴定。通过功能富集、共表达、lincRNA-miRNA-mRNA网络预测以及基于AlphaFold的蛋白质-RNA结构预测来表征候选lincRNAs。结果：共鉴定出2,736个表达的lincRNAs，其中515个在感染期间差异表达。对差异表达lincRNAs周围100 kb内基因的功能富集分析显示，其与防御相关通路显著关联，包括MAPK信号传导和植物-病原体互作。lincRNAs与邻近防御相关mRNAs之间的协调表达模式提示了潜在的顺式调控关联。此外，lincRNA-miRNA-mRNA网络预测识别出65个差异表达lincRNAs作为21个保守miRNAs的潜在内源靶标模拟物，揭示了一个广泛的竞争性内源RNA（ceRNA）调控网络。重要的是，基于AlphaFold的蛋白质-RNA结构预测表明，共表达的lincRNA MSTRG.56980与RGA1型抗性蛋白TraesCS6B03G0179100形成稳定复合物。讨论：这提供了小麦lincRNA与免疫受体之间可能物理相互作用的首次计算预测。这些整合性发现显著扩展了当前对小麦免疫中非编码调控网络的理解，并为优先选择候选lincRNAs作为增强条锈病抗性的靶标提供了基础资源。
