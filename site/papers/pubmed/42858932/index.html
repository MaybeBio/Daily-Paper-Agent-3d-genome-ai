Transcription factor CTCF activates KIAA1199 expression to inhibit ferroptosis and promote malignant behaviors in colorectal cancer based on bioinformatics and machine learning.

Fan, Xiaoye; Meng, Lei; Zheng, Song; He, Xiaoqing
Gene · 2026-10-11 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究CTCF转录激活KIAA1199调控结直肠癌铁死亡，虽涉及CTCF但仅作为转录因子，无染色质三维组织或空间架构建模，且方法为常规生信与实验，与课题无关。

摘要

OBJECTIVES: Colorectal cancer (CRC) is a common malignancy of the colon and rectum, but its molecular mechanisms remain incompletely understood. METHODS: Gene expression omnibus (GEO) database was used to download CRC-related datasets. Weighted gene co-expression network analysis (WGCNA), least absolute shrinkage and selection operator (LASSO), support vector machine (SVM), and random forest (RF) were used to identify key CRC genes. Cell type identification by estimating relative subsets of rna transcripts (CIBERSORT) was used for immune infiltration analysis. Tumor immune estimation resource (TIMER) database was used to assess gene expression across multiple cancers. Human transcription factor target database (hTFtarget) was used to predict regulatory relationships between genes and transcription factors. Chromatin immunoprecipitation (ChIP), dual-luciferase assay, cell transfection, western blot (WB), and reverse transcription quantitative polymerase chain reaction (RT-qPCR) were used to detect protein-gene binding, transcriptional activity, gene knockdown and overexpression, protein expression, and mRNA expression. JASPAR database was used to identify transcription factor binding sites. Cells were treated with a BODIPY 581/591 C11 kit to assess intracellular reactive oxygen species (ROS) levels. The assay kits were employed to determine the levels of ferrous iron (Fe(2+)), malondialdehyde (MDA), and superoxide dismutase (SOD). Cell counting kit-8 (CCK-8) was performed to measure cell viability. RESULTS: Matrix metallopeptidase 7 (MMP7), KIAA1199, and cadherin 3 (CDH3) were identified as potential key CRC genes. Their expression correlated with altered immune cell infiltration patterns. KIAA1199 showed the highest upregulation in CRC tissues. CCCTC-binding factor (CTCF) transcriptionally activated KIAA1199. Knockdown of CTCF decreased the expression of solute carrier family 7 member 11 (SLC7A11) and glutathione peroxidase 4 (GPX4), while increasing ROS, Fe(2+), and MDA, and decreasing SOD levels. CTCF knockdown combined with KIAA1199 overexpression partially rescued these effects, modulating ferroptosis-related oxidative stress in CRC cells. CONCLUSION: CTCF transcriptionally activates KIAA1199 and regulates ferroptosis-related oxidative stress in CRC cells, providing new insights into the molecular mechanisms of CRC.

摘要翻译

目的：结直肠癌（CRC）是结肠和直肠的常见恶性肿瘤，但其分子机制尚未完全阐明。方法：利用基因表达综合（GEO）数据库下载CRC相关数据集。采用加权基因共表达网络分析（WGCNA）、最小绝对收缩和选择算子（LASSO）、支持向量机（SVM）和随机森林（RF）鉴定CRC关键基因。通过估计RNA转录本的相对子集进行细胞类型鉴定（CIBERSORT）用于免疫浸润分析。肿瘤免疫估计资源（TIMER）数据库用于评估多种癌症中的基因表达。人类转录因子靶标数据库（hTFtarget）用于预测基因与转录因子之间的调控关系。染色质免疫沉淀（ChIP）、双荧光素酶实验、细胞转染、蛋白质印迹（WB）和逆转录定量聚合酶链反应（RT-qPCR）用于检测蛋白质-基因结合、转录活性、基因敲低和过表达、蛋白质表达及mRNA表达。JASPAR数据库用于识别转录因子结合位点。细胞用BODIPY 581/591 C11试剂盒处理以评估细胞内活性氧（ROS）水平。使用检测试剂盒测定亚铁离子（Fe(2+)）、丙二醛（MDA）和超氧化物歧化酶（SOD）的水平。采用细胞计数试剂盒-8（CCK-8）测量细胞活力。结果：基质金属蛋白酶7（MMP7）、KIAA1199和钙黏蛋白3（CDH3）被鉴定为潜在的CRC关键基因。它们的表达与免疫细胞浸润模式的改变相关。KIAA1199在CRC组织中表现出最高的上调。CCCTC结合因子（CTCF）在转录水平上激活KIAA1199。敲低CTCF降低了溶质载体家族7成员11（SLC7A11）和谷胱甘肽过氧化物酶4（GPX4）的表达，同时增加了ROS、Fe(2+)和MDA，并降低了SOD水平。CTCF敲低与KIAA1199过表达联合部分逆转了这些效应，调节了CRC细胞中铁死亡相关的氧化应激。结论：CTCF在转录水平上激活KIAA1199并调节CRC细胞中铁死亡相关的氧化应激，为CRC的分子机制提供了新见解。
