Differential evolution of neuroinflammatory remodeling programs related to scar formation and regeneration in spinal cord injury: A cross-species multi-omics analysis.

Tang, Hao; Cheng, Fei; Xu, Juan; Liao, Huiming; Lu, Shunfei; Fu, Xin; Fang, Sheng
Neural regeneration research · 2026-10-18 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过跨物种多组学分析脊髓损伤后神经炎症与瘢痕重塑程序，虽涉及染色质可及性分析，但核心是炎症与再生机制，非三维基因组/染色质组织建模，且无AI或分子模拟方法贡献。

摘要

Spinal cord injury in mammals often transitions from an acute inflammatory response to persistent neuroinflammatory and scar-associated remodeling, whereas zebrafish attenuate injury-induced inflammatory programs during regenerative repair. The molecular programs underlying these divergent outcomes remain incompletely understood. In this study, we integrated phase-aligned mouse and zebrafish spinal cord injury datasets using single-cell and single-nucleus transcriptomics, spatial transcriptomics, pathway and regulon analyses, expression-based ligand-receptor co-expression analysis, machine-learning validation, chromatin-accessibility profiling, in silico perturbation modeling, and mouse tissue validation. Cross-species analysis revealed a conserved injury-responsive program associated with secreted phosphoprotein 1 (SPP1) and CD44, but with distinct temporal trajectories between species. In mouse spinal cord injury, Spp1 and Cd44 expression remained elevated from acute injury through scar-associated stages and was enriched within lesion-associated myeloid and glial-immune microenvironments. Persistent SPP1-CD44-associated expression was accompanied by inflammatory signaling, reactive gliosis, fibrotic remodeling, and sustained immune-niche remodeling. In zebrafish, spp1 and cd44a were similarly induced during the early injury response but declined during regenerative recovery, consistent with attenuation and reorganization of inflammatory programs rather than absence of early injury activation. Machine-learning analyses supported the cross-dataset reproducibility of SPP1-CD44-related injury-state features across two mouse spinal cord injury datasets. Regulon, perturbation-associated, and chromatin-accessibility analyses further positioned Spp1 within an injury-responsive regulatory context associated with signal transducer and activator of transcription 3 and SMARCA4 (SWI/SNF related, matrix associated, actin dependent regulator of chromatin, subfamily a, member 4) states and identified perturbation-sensitive accessibility changes at the Spp1 promoter. Mouse spinal cord injury tissue validation confirmed increased SPP1 and CD44 protein expression after injury and localized enhanced SPP1 signal to ionized calcium-binding adapter molecule 1-positive lesion-associated myeloid regions. Together, these findings suggest that mammalian spinal cord injury scarring is associated with persistence of a conserved SPP1-CD44-associated neuroinflammatory remodeling program, whereas attenuation of the corresponding program accompanies regenerative spinal cord repair in zebrafish. This cross-species framework provides molecular insights and candidate regulatory programs for future studies aimed at promoting inflammatory resolution and neural repair after spinal cord injury.

摘要翻译

哺乳动物脊髓损伤通常从急性炎症反应过渡到持续的神经炎症和瘢痕相关重塑，而斑马鱼在再生修复过程中会减弱损伤诱导的炎症程序。这些不同结局背后的分子程序仍不完全清楚。在本研究中，我们整合了相位对齐的小鼠和斑马鱼脊髓损伤数据集，使用单细胞和单核转录组学、空间转录组学、通路和调节子分析、基于表达的配体-受体共表达分析、机器学习验证、染色质可及性分析、计算机扰动建模以及小鼠组织验证。跨物种分析揭示了一个与分泌型磷蛋白1（SPP1）和CD44相关的保守损伤响应程序，但不同物种间具有不同的时间轨迹。在小鼠脊髓损伤中，Spp1和Cd44表达从急性损伤到瘢痕相关阶段持续升高，并富集于病变相关的髓系和胶质-免疫微环境中。持续的SPP1-CD44相关表达伴随炎症信号传导、反应性胶质增生、纤维化重塑和持续的免疫微环境重塑。在斑马鱼中，spp1和cd44a在早期损伤响应中同样被诱导，但在再生恢复过程中下降，这与炎症程序的减弱和重组一致，而非缺乏早期损伤激活。机器学习分析支持SPP1-CD44相关损伤状态特征在两个小鼠脊髓损伤数据集中的跨数据集可重复性。调节子、扰动相关和染色质可及性分析进一步将Spp1定位于与信号转导和转录激活因子3及SMARCA4（SWI/SNF相关、基质相关、肌动蛋白依赖性染色质调节因子，亚家族a，成员4）状态相关的损伤响应调控背景中，并识别了Spp1启动子处扰动敏感的染色质可及性变化。小鼠脊髓损伤组织验证确认了损伤后SPP1和CD44蛋白表达增加，并将增强的SPP1信号定位于离子化钙结合适配器分子1阳性的病变相关髓系区域。综合来看，这些发现表明，哺乳动物脊髓损伤瘢痕形成与保守的SPP1-CD44相关神经炎症重塑程序的持续存在相关，而相应程序的减弱伴随斑马鱼脊髓的再生修复。这一跨物种框架为未来旨在促进脊髓损伤后炎症消退和神经修复的研究提供了分子见解和候选调控程序。
