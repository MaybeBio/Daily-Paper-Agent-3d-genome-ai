Deciphering the regulatory code of RNA inosine through enzymatic precision mapping and an explainable deep learning model.

Han, Shaoqing; Li, Jiehua; Yang, Wei; Wei, Qi; Wang, Yafen; Zhou, Xiang; Weng, Xiaocheng
Cell discovery · 2026-11-05 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文开发RNA肌苷检测技术与可解释深度学习模型，聚焦RNA编辑位点预测，与染色质三维组织及空间架构建模无关，方法不可迁移。

摘要

Adenosine-to-inosine (A-to-I) RNA editing is one of the most abundant RNA modifications and is involved in multiple critical biological processes; however, the precise localization of inosine and the mechanisms governing their selective editing remain incompletely understood. Integrating high-accuracy detection technologies with deep learning models can accelerate our understanding of the selective positioning and functional implications of A-to-I editing. To address these challenges, this study simultaneously developed RNA inosine sequencing by endonuclease V (EndoV) and XRN-1 (REX-seq), a highly sensitive and accurate detection method for inosine sites, and Inosine Deep Residual Enhanced Attention Model (InoDREAM), a predictive deep learning framework. REX-seq combines EndoV cleavage specificity with XRN-1 exonuclease activity to enable comprehensive A-to-I editome profiling while maintaining accurate gene expression quantification. Using this method, we identified 590,249 high-confidence inosine sites in HEK293T cells, including 84,645 novel sites. Leveraging the high-confidence A-to-I editing sites identified by REX-seq, we developed InoDREAM, which successfully predicted multiple regulatory proteins that exhibit specific binding affinity to A-to-I edited transcripts while simultaneously identifying disease-associated mutation sites and relevant cis-regulatory elements that are functionally linked to RNA editing events. These findings provide novel mechanistic insights into both the molecular regulation of RNA editing and its pathophysiological implications in disease development.

摘要翻译

腺苷到肌苷（A-to-I）RNA编辑是最丰富的RNA修饰之一，参与多个关键生物过程；然而，肌苷的精确定位及其选择性编辑的调控机制仍未完全阐明。将高精度检测技术与深度学习模型相结合，可加速我们对A-to-I编辑选择性定位及功能意义的理解。为应对这些挑战，本研究同时开发了基于内切酶V（EndoV）和XRN-1的RNA肌苷测序方法（REX-seq），一种高灵敏度和高精度的肌苷位点检测技术，以及肌苷深度残差增强注意力模型（InoDREAM），一种预测性深度学习框架。REX-seq结合了EndoV切割特异性和XRN-1外切酶活性，能够全面分析A-to-I编辑组，同时保持准确的基因表达定量。利用该方法，我们在HEK293T细胞中鉴定了590,249个高置信度肌苷位点，其中包括84,645个新位点。借助REX-seq鉴定的高置信度A-to-I编辑位点，我们开发了InoDREAM，该模型成功预测了多个对A-to-I编辑转录本具有特异性结合亲和力的调控蛋白，同时识别了与RNA编辑事件功能相关的疾病相关突变位点及顺式调控元件。这些发现为RNA编辑的分子调控及其在疾病发展中的病理生理意义提供了新的机制见解。
