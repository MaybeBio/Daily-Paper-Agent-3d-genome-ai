7

ChAI-Net learns a regulatory code for chromatin co-accessibility.

Lee, Jodi; Zhang, Kaite; Shurberg, Ethan; Ramani, Vijay; Pollard, Katherine S
bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology · 2026-11-06 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文用深度学习从DNA序列预测染色质共可及性图谱，解析顺式调控元件内转录因子协同结合的调控语法，虽聚焦可及性而非三维空间折叠，但方法可迁移至染色质空间互作建模。

摘要

The regulation of chromatin accessibility is critical for cell function and identity. Advances in single-molecule long-read footprinting have enabled near-nucleotide-resolution readout of chromatin fiber organization, capturing both coordination of transcription factor (TF) binding and variation in nucleosome positioning previously obscured by bulk methods. Here we combine this technique with deep learning to study how protein-DNA interactions are coordinated within different types of cis -regulatory elements in mouse embryonic stem cells. First, we quantify the frequency with which pairs of positions are simultaneously accessible on individual molecules. Then, we train ChAI-Net, a neural network that predicts these locus-specific chromatin co-accessibility maps from the underlying DNA sequence. Through model interpretation and in silico experiments, we show that ChAI-Net learns unique combinations of sequence motifs underlying co-accessibility within enhancers, promoters, and CTCF-bound sites. Many of these motifs are missed when modeling average accessibility, suggesting a specific role in coordinated binding. ChAI-Net's regulatory grammar also highlights the importance of TF motif orientation, spacing, positioning, and co-occurrence in driving co-accessibility. Thus, our framework for extracting regulatory rules from footprinting data expands understanding of TF cooperativity, while laying the groundwork to elucidate how these rules vary across cellular contexts as single-molecule genomics is deployed at scale.

摘要翻译

染色质可及性的调控对细胞功能和身份至关重要。单分子长读段足迹分析技术的进步使得能够以近核苷酸分辨率读取染色质纤维组织，捕捉到转录因子（TF）结合的协调性以及核小体定位的变异，这些在批量方法中此前被掩盖。在此，我们将该技术与深度学习相结合，研究小鼠胚胎干细胞中不同类型顺式调控元件内蛋白质-DNA相互作用的协调方式。首先，我们量化单个分子上成对位置同时可及的频率。然后，我们训练ChAI-Net，这是一个神经网络，用于从底层DNA序列预测这些位点特异性的染色质共可及性图谱。通过模型解释和计算机模拟实验，我们表明ChAI-Net学习了增强子、启动子和CTCF结合位点内共可及性背后的独特序列基序组合。在建模平均可及性时，许多这些基序被遗漏，表明它们在协调结合中具有特定作用。ChAI-Net的调控语法还强调了TF基序方向、间距、定位和共现对驱动共可及性的重要性。因此，我们从足迹数据中提取调控规则的框架扩展了对TF协同性的理解，同时为随着单分子基因组学大规模部署，阐明这些规则在不同细胞环境中的变化奠定了基础。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何从 DNA 序列预测染色质共可及性（即同一染色质纤维上不同位置同时可及的概率），并解析驱动这种协同可及性的调控编码？
  • 为什么重要：染色质可及性调控是细胞功能和身份的核心。传统 bulk 方法（如 ATAC-seq、DNase-seq）只能提供平均可及性，丢失了单分子层面的协同信息。理解共可及性有助于揭示 TF 协同结合、增强子-启动子互作和染色质高级结构的序列基础。
  • 现有方法为何不足：现有深度学习方法（如 Basenji、Enformer）主要预测平均可及性或表达量，未建模单分子层面的协同可及性；且缺乏对 TF 结合协调性的直接量化。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a neural network learn locus-specific chromatin co-accessibility maps from DNA sequence, and can its interpretation reveal the regulatory grammar (motif combinations, orientation, spacing, positioning) underlying coordinated TF binding within cis-regulatory elements?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法

  • 用单分子长读长足迹数据量化每对位置同时可及的概率（共可及性矩阵）。
  • 训练 ChAI-Net，一个神经网络，以 DNA 序列为输入，预测位点特异的共可及性图谱。
  • 通过模型解释（如 motif 分析、in silico 突变）提取调控语法。

2) 核心洞察

  • 共可及性不是平均可及性的简单函数，而是由序列中 TF 基序的组合、方向、间距和定位共同编码的独立调控层。
  • 深度学习可以从单分子数据中学习到传统平均可及性模型无法捕获的协同结合规则。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]

  • 单分子分辨率的数据 + 可解释深度学习 = 解码染色质调控编码的通用范式。
  • 共可及性可作为连接序列与三维基因组结构的中间表型，其预测模型可能迁移到增强子-启动子互作和 TAD 边界预测。

  • 端到端状态：模型从序列到共可及性图谱的端到端预测，但未验证到三维结构或功能输出。
  • 算力成本：未提供。
  • 历史数据依赖：依赖 mESC 特定表观状态，跨细胞类型泛化未验证。
  • 模型依赖：结果依赖 ChAI-Net 架构和训练策略，不同架构可能得到不同 motif 规则。
  • 最难情形：稀疏覆盖区域、重复序列区域、非经典元件类型。
  • 群体/领域边界：仅限小鼠；人类或其他物种未验证。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选

  1. 共可及性作为中间表型：将单分子足迹数据量化为成对共可及性矩阵，可作为连接序列与三维基因组结构的中间层。可迁移到本课题：用类似方法预测增强子-启动子互作或 TAD 边界。
  2. 可解释深度学习提取调控语法：通过模型解释（motif 分析、in silico 突变）从黑箱预测中提取可检验的生物学规则。可迁移：用于解析 CTCF/cohesin 结合位点的序列编码。
  3. 元件类型特异的调控规则：不同 cis-regulatory element 有独特的 motif 组合和空间规则。可迁移：在三维基因组预测中，按元件类型分别建模可能优于统一模型。
  4. 单分子数据 + 深度学习的范式：单分子分辨率数据为深度学习提供新标签维度。可迁移：将单分子追踪数据（如 DNA 折痕、loop 动态）用于训练三维结构预测模型。
  5. 方向、间距、定位作为特征：TF motif 的空间组织（方向、间距、定位）是协同结合的关键。可迁移：在预测染色质 loop 或 TAD 边界时，显式编码 motif 空间关系可能提升性能。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract describes a potentially valuable integration of single-molecule long-read footprinting with deep learning to infer regulatory grammar governing chromatin co-accessibility. The conceptual framing is clear and the potential biological insights are of interest. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient detail to evaluate the technical soundness of the model, the validity of the interpretation methods, or the robustness of the claimed findings. Several claims are made without visible quantitative support, and the relationship between model performance and biological interpretation is not established from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in regulatory genomics, chromatin biology, and computational biology would be interested. Those working on transcription factor cooperativity, cis-regulatory element function, and single-molecule genomics would find the framework relevant. The potential to extract regulatory rules from footprinting data could also appeal to those developing deep learning approaches for genomic sequence interpretation.
  • Major strengths The abstract addresses a timely and important problem, namely understanding coordinated protein-DNA interactions at single-molecule resolution. The combination of an emerging experimental technique with deep learning is conceptually appealing. The claim that co-accessibility modeling reveals motifs missed by average accessibility approaches suggests a potentially novel biological insight. The focus on regulatory grammar features such as motif orientation, spacing, and positioning is well aligned with current questions in gene regulation.
