JNK acts as a molecular brake of the CDC73 positive feedback loop to modulate osteosarcoma malignant progression via UBR5.

Li, Dingji; Wang, Xinchen; Luo, Huigen; Tu, Hua; Hao, Lili; Lei, IoHong; Hu, Renjie; Zheng, Pengchao; Shi, Yuhan; Sun, Jianbo; Zhu, Minying; Guan, Chengnong; Xu, Baoshan
Oncogene · 2026-09-17 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究JNK-UBR5-CDC73轴调控骨肉瘤进展，虽含ATAC-seq染色质可及性分析，但核心是肿瘤信号通路与蛋白泛素化，无三维基因组/染色质空间架构建模，亦无AI或分子模拟方法贡献。

摘要

CDC73 is a well-characterized tumor suppressor regulated by stress stimuli, governing progression of diverse human malignancies. Although previous studies have shown that E3 ubiquitin ligase UBR5 drives CDC73 ubiquitination and degradation to modulate tumorigenesis, the mechanisms by which stress-responsive pathways regulate UBR5-mediated CDC73 inactivation and transcriptional reprogramming remain elusive. Here, via integrated analyses of public datasets, multi-omics profiling (assay for transposase-accessible chromatin with sequencing [ATAC-seq], cleavage under targets and tagmentation [CUT&Tag], mRNA sequencing [mRNA-seq]), in vitro/in vivo assays, and molecular approaches including co-immunoprecipitation (Co-IP) and molecular docking, we demonstrate that UBR5 depletion profoundly alters chromatin accessibility and genome-wide transcriptional profiles in a CDC73-dependent manner. UBR5 ablation markedly suppresses osteosarcoma malignant phenotypes in cultured cells and xenograft models, with these effects fully rescued by concurrent CDC73 silencing. Mechanistically, we identify the JNK cascade as the critical upstream regulator: JNK activation sustains CDC73 stability by antagonizing UBR5-mediated CDC73 polyubiquitination, and map Lys257 as the key residue for UBR5-dependent CDC73 ubiquitination and degradation. Collectively, our findings define a novel JNK-dependent UBR5-CDC73 axis that acts as a molecular brake of the CDC73 positive feedback loop to orchestrate transcriptional programs, providing new mechanistic insights into CDC73 post-translational regulation in tumorigenesis and promising therapeutic targets for CDC73-dysregulated diseases.

摘要翻译

CDC73是一个特征明确的肿瘤抑制因子，受应激刺激调控，主导多种人类恶性肿瘤的进展。尽管既往研究表明E3泛素连接酶UBR5驱动CDC73的泛素化和降解以调节肿瘤发生，但应激响应通路调控UBR5介导的CDC73失活及转录重编程的机制仍不明确。在此，通过公共数据集的整合分析、多组学分析（转座酶可及染色质测序[ATAC-seq]、靶向切割与标记[CUT&Tag]、mRNA测序[mRNA-seq]）、体外/体内实验，以及包括免疫共沉淀（Co-IP）和分子对接在内的分子方法，我们证明UBR5缺失以CDC73依赖的方式深刻改变染色质可及性和全基因组转录谱。UBR5消融显著抑制培养细胞和异种移植模型中的骨肉瘤恶性表型，而这些效应可被同时沉默CDC73完全逆转。机制上，我们确定JNK级联为关键上游调控因子：JNK激活通过拮抗UBR5介导的CDC73多聚泛素化来维持CDC73稳定性，并定位Lys257为UBR5依赖性CDC73泛素化和降解的关键残基。总体而言，我们的发现定义了一个新型JNK依赖性UBR5-CDC73轴，作为CDC73正反馈环的分子制动器来协调转录程序，为肿瘤发生中CDC73翻译后调控提供新的机制见解，并为CDC73失调疾病提供有前景的治疗靶点。
