Design, Synthesis and Antitumor Activity Evaluation of Novel Dual G9a/EZH2 Inhibitors.

Shi, Yajie; Zhang, Qiuyue; Zhang, Maoying; Zhao, Xiujuan; Zhang, Shuhang; Li, Jirui; Li, Haofeng; Fu, Chenglin; Chen, Guoliang
Molecules (Basel, Switzerland) · 2026-10-16 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文为G9a/EZH2双靶点抑制剂的设计合成与抗肿瘤活性评价，属药物化学研究，与三维基因组/染色质组织建模及AI/分子模拟方法无实质关联。

摘要

Targeting the crosstalk between histone methyltransferases G9a and EZH2 represents a promising strategy for cancer therapy. To improve upon our previously reported dual inhibitor SU08, we rationally designed and synthesized five series (SU, UA, UB, N, and L) of novel G9a/EZH2 dual inhibitors, incorporating diverse pyridone, urea, and thienopyrimidine moieties, yielding a total of 43 compounds. In vitro enzymatic screening revealed that compound SU101 against both EZH2 (IC(50) = 4.08 nM) and G9a (IC(50) = 2.92 nM) was comparable to SU08. Western blot analysis confirmed that SU101 and SU103 effectively suppressed both H3K27me3 and H3K9me2 expression in rhabdomyosarcoma RD cells. In cellular antiproliferative assays, SU101 showed the strongest activity (IC(50) = 15.33 muM). Molecular docking and dynamics simulations verified stable binding interactions between SU101 and both target proteins. Pharmacokinetic studies in rats demonstrated that SU101 possesses excellent oral bioavailability (94.3%), moderate plasma protein binding (84.6%), and an acceptable acute toxicity profile (LD(50) = 31.02 mg/kg in mice). Overall, these results suggest that SU101 may serve as a promising lead compound for further optimization and support the continued exploration of dual G9a/EZH2 inhibitors as a potential therapeutic approach for rhabdomyosarcoma.

摘要翻译

靶向组蛋白甲基转移酶G9a与EZH2之间的相互作用代表了一种有前景的癌症治疗策略。为了改进我们先前报道的双重抑制剂SU08，我们合理设计并合成了五个系列（SU、UA、UB、N和L）的新型G9a/EZH2双重抑制剂，其中引入了多种吡啶酮、脲和噻吩并嘧啶结构单元，共获得43个化合物。体外酶学筛选显示，化合物SU101对EZH2（IC(50) = 4.08 nM）和G9a（IC(50) = 2.92 nM）的抑制活性与SU08相当。Western blot分析证实，SU101和SU103在横纹肌肉瘤RD细胞中有效抑制了H3K27me3和H3K9me2的表达。在细胞抗增殖实验中，SU101表现出最强的活性（IC(50) = 15.33 μM）。分子对接和动力学模拟验证了SU101与两种靶蛋白之间的稳定结合相互作用。大鼠药代动力学研究表明，SU101具有优异的口服生物利用度（94.3%）、适度的血浆蛋白结合率（84.6%）和可接受的急性毒性特征（小鼠LD(50) = 31.02 mg/kg）。总体而言，这些结果表明SU101可能作为进一步优化的有前景的先导化合物，并支持继续探索双重G9a/EZH2抑制剂作为横纹肌肉瘤的潜在治疗策略。
