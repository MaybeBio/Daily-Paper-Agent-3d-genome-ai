CardioChrom Reconstructs the Human Cardiac Virtual Epigenome from Single-Nucleus Transcriptomes.

Huang, Hongbiao; Hui, Xiaodan; Li, Yingjian; Abdelbaset-Ismail, Ahmed; Xu, Zixuan; Yadav, Suchen; Tan, Yi
bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology · 2026-10-01 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文用深度学习从单核转录组重建心脏染色质可及性与组蛋白修饰，虽涉及染色质调控状态预测，但对象非三维基因组/染色质空间组织，且无三维架构建模或分子模拟方法，仅属表观基因组预测，与课题关联有限。

摘要

BACKGROUND: Matched epigenomic profiling remains scarce in human heart failure, limiting regulatory interpretation of single-nucleus ribonucleic acid (RNA) sequencing data. We developed CardioChrom, a cardiac-specialized framework that reconstructs chromatin accessibility, histone H3 lysine 27 acetylation (H3K27ac), and histone H3 lysine 27 trimethylation (H3K27me3) from single-nucleus RNA profiles. METHODS: CardioChrom was developed using same-nucleus human heart datasets jointly measuring RNA with chromatin accessibility by assay for transposase-accessible chromatin (ATAC), H3K27ac, or H3K27me3, with donor-separated development and leave-one-cell-type-out testing. Frozen models were evaluated in 10-donor and 23-sample external human heart cohorts. Histone reconstruction was benchmarked against measured profiles and a Transformer. The models were then applied without refitting to an RNA-only cohort of 43 donors with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF). RESULTS: Across unseen cardiac cell types, CardioChrom increased area under the precision- recall curve by 0.0109 relative to within-cell-type RNA shuffling in 11 of 12 cell types and increased area under the receiver operating characteristic curve by 0.0179 in all 12.CardioChrom exceeded controls in both external cohorts and matched or exceeded the Transformer across 6 prespecified histone metrics. In HFpEF, 3,581 donor-level pathway-layer associations met global false discovery rate, sex-adjustment, and leave-one-donor-out robustness criteria. Endothelial cells showed prominent reconstructed histone-associated changes involving coagulation, inflammation, hypoxia, and profibrotic signaling. Von Willebrand factor expression and its reconstructed regulatory states were consistently reduced, and all 6 linked candidate cis-regulatory elements were classified as repressed. Motif-supported TF-cCRE-gene hypotheses were generated in endothelial cells and fibroblasts. CONCLUSIONS: CardioChrom extends RNA-only human heart failure cohorts toward cell-type-resolved virtual epigenomic and regulatory analysis while distinguishing predictions from direct measurements. CLINICAL PERSPECTIVE: What Is New?: CardioChrom is, to our knowledge, the first cardiac-specialized virtual epigenome framework to integrate same-nucleus human heart RNA-ATAC and RNA-histone reference data to reconstruct chromatin accessibility, H3K27ac, and H3K27me3 states from single-nucleus RNA profiles.The framework was tested beyond its training setting, including unseen donors, leave-one-cell-type-out evaluation, and independent human heart cohorts, and consistently exceeded simple RNA-shuffled or peak-frequency baselines while remaining competitive with or superior to Transformer-based comparators.What Are the Clinical Implications?: Many heart-failure cohorts contain transcriptomic data without matched epigenomic measurements. CardioChrom provides a way to extend these RNA-only datasets toward cell-type-resolved regulatory-state analysis without requiring additional tissue or multimodal assays.In an independent RNA-only HFpEF cohort, CardioChrom identified prominent endothelial regulatory remodeling involving Coagulation, IL6/JAK/STAT3, TNFalpha/NFkappaB, Hypoxia, and TGF-beta/EMT and generated TF-cCRE-gene hypotheses that can be prioritized for experimental validation.

摘要翻译

背景：在人类心力衰竭中，匹配的表观基因组分析仍然稀缺，限制了对单核核糖核酸（RNA）测序数据的调控解读。我们开发了CardioChrom，一种心脏特化框架，可从单核RNA谱中重建染色质可及性、组蛋白H3赖氨酸27乙酰化（H3K27ac）和组蛋白H3赖氨酸27三甲基化（H3K27me3）。方法：CardioChrom利用同核人类心脏数据集开发，这些数据集同时测量RNA与通过转座酶可及染色质测定（ATAC）、H3K27ac或H3K27me3的染色质可及性，并进行供体分离开发和留一细胞类型测试。冻结模型在10供体和23样本的外部人类心脏队列中评估。组蛋白重建与测量谱和Transformer进行基准比较。随后，这些模型未经重新拟合应用于一个仅含RNA的43名射血分数保留型心力衰竭（HFpEF）供体队列。结果：在未见的心脏细胞类型中，CardioChrom在12种细胞类型中的11种中，相对于细胞类型内RNA打乱，将精确率-召回率曲线下面积提高了0.0109，并在全部12种中将受试者工作特征曲线下面积提高了0.0179。CardioChrom在两个外部队列中均超过对照组，并在6项预设组蛋白指标上与Transformer相当或超过。在HFpEF中，3,581个供体水平通路-层关联满足全局错误发现率、性别调整和留一供体稳健性标准。内皮细胞显示出突出的重建组蛋白相关变化，涉及凝血、炎症、缺氧和促纤维化信号。血管性血友病因子表达及其重建调控状态一致降低，所有6个关联候选顺式调控元件被分类为抑制状态。在内皮细胞和成纤维细胞中产生了基序支持的TF-cCRE-基因假设。结论：CardioChrom将仅含RNA的人类心力衰竭队列扩展至细胞类型分辨的虚拟表观基因组和调控分析，同时区分预测与直接测量。临床视角：新内容是什么？：据我们所知，CardioChrom是首个心脏特化的虚拟表观基因组框架，整合同核人类心脏RNA-ATAC和RNA-组蛋白参考数据，从单核RNA谱中重建染色质可及性、H3K27ac和H3K27me3状态。该框架在其训练设置之外进行了测试，包括未见供体、留一细胞类型评估和独立人类心脏队列，并始终超过简单的RNA打乱或峰频率基线，同时与基于Transformer的比较器保持竞争力或优于后者。临床意义是什么？：许多心力衰竭队列包含转录组数据，但缺乏匹配的表观基因组测量。CardioChrom提供了一种方法，将这些仅含RNA的数据集扩展至细胞类型分辨的调控状态分析，而无需额外组织或多模态测定。在一个独立的仅含RNA的HFpEF队列中，CardioChrom识别出涉及凝血、IL6/JAK/STAT3、TNFalpha/NFkappaB、缺氧和TGF-beta/EMT的突出内皮调控重塑，并生成了可优先用于实验验证的TF-cCRE-基因假设。
