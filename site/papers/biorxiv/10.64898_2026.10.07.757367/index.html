Artificial Intelligence Applied to Epigenomic Data in Leukemia: A Systematic Review of Diagnostic, Prognostic and Monitoring Models

Martelli J; Martelli J
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-10-09 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该综述系统评估了AI/ML在白血病表观基因组数据（DNA甲基化、染色质可及性）上的诊断、预后与监测模型，但对象为临床疾病预测而非染色质三维空间组织建模，且无分子模拟或三维架构方法，与本课题关联微弱。

摘要

<h4>Background: </h4> Epigenetic deregulation is a defining feature of leukemia, and DNA methylation and chromatin accessibility profiles encode the cell of origin, lineage and clinical behaviour of the disease. Artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) can extract predictive patterns from these high-dimensional data, but the published models have not been systematically appraised. We systematically reviewed AI models built on epigenomic data in leukemia for diagnosis, prognosis, relapse and treatment-response prediction, and measurable residual disease (MRD) detection. <h4>Methods:</h4> This review followed PRISMA 2020 and the SWiM guideline. PubMed was searched on 7 October 2026 with three structured queries, supplemented by a broader PubMed query, citation searching and web searching. Eligible studies were peer-reviewed primary studies that applied an ML or deep-learning algorithm to DNA methylation, chromatin-accessibility or histone-modification data from human leukemia samples and reported a quantitative diagnostic, prognostic, response or MRD outcome. Risk of bias and applicability were assessed with PROBAST, informed by PROBAST+AI. Owing to heterogeneity, results were synthesized narratively and in structured tables, without meta-analysis. <h4>Results:</h4> Of 68 unique database records and 32 reports from other sources, 30 studies (2010-2026; 22 since 2020) were included: 16 in acute myeloid leukemia, 4 in acute lymphoblastic leukemia, 2 spanning acute leukemia lineages, 3 in juvenile myelomonocytic leukemia and 5 in chronic lymphocytic leukemia; 10 were pediatric. DNA methylation was the input in 28 studies and chromatin accessibility in 2; no eligible study modelled histone marks. Tree ensembles (n = 9) and support vector machines (n = 5) were the most frequent algorithms. Diagnostic classifiers reported accuracy, concordance or F1 scores of 0.87-1.00, and two classifiers were applied to nanopore sequencing data, one returning acute leukemia subtype calls within about 2 hours. Prognostic epigenomic scores were associated with survival (reported hazard ratios 2.36-10.82), but discrimination fell on transport to independent cohorts (C-index 0.677 internally vs 0.529 in one external cohort). Fifteen studies validated models on an external epigenomic cohort, one prospectively. Overall risk of bias was high in 21 studies, unclear in 8 and low in 1, driven by the analysis domain (small samples, absent calibration, possible data leakage). Calibration was reported in 3 studies and code was available for 11. <h4>Conclusions:</h4> Epigenomic AI models classify leukemia subtypes with high reported accuracy and can deliver same-day diagnoses, and methylation-based scores add prognostic information. However, the evidence is dominated by retrospective, high-risk-of-bias development studies with limited external validation and almost no prospective or impact evaluation. Prospective, multi-center validation with calibrated, transparently reported models is required before clinical adoption.

摘要翻译

<h4>背景：</h4> 表观遗传失调是白血病的定义性特征，DNA甲基化和染色质可及性谱编码了疾病的起源细胞、谱系和临床行为。人工智能（AI）和机器学习（ML）可以从这些高维数据中提取预测模式，但已发表的模型尚未得到系统评估。我们系统回顾了基于白血病表观基因组数据构建的AI模型，用于诊断、预后、复发和治疗反应预测，以及可测量残留病（MRD）检测。<h4>方法：</h4> 本综述遵循PRISMA 2020和SWiM指南。于2026年10月7日使用三个结构化查询在PubMed进行检索，并通过更广泛的PubMed查询、引文检索和网络检索进行补充。符合条件的研究为同行评审的原始研究，这些研究将ML或深度学习算法应用于人类白血病样本的DNA甲基化、染色质可及性或组蛋白修饰数据，并报告了定量的诊断、预后、反应或MRD结果。使用PROBAST评估偏倚风险和适用性，并参考PROBAST+AI。由于异质性，结果以叙述性和结构化表格形式综合，未进行荟萃分析。<h4>结果：</h4> 在68条独特数据库记录和32份其他来源报告中，纳入30项研究（2010-2026年；其中22项自2020年以来）：16项针对急性髓系白血病，4项针对急性淋巴细胞白血病，2项涵盖急性白血病谱系，3项针对幼年型粒单核细胞白血病，5项针对慢性淋巴细胞白血病；其中10项为儿科研究。DNA甲基化是28项研究的输入，染色质可及性为2项；没有符合条件的研究对组蛋白标记进行建模。树集成（n = 9）和支持向量机（n = 5）是最常见的算法。诊断分类器报告的准确率、一致性或F1分数为0.87-1.00，两个分类器应用于纳米孔测序数据，其中一个在约2小时内返回急性白血病亚型分类。预后表观基因组评分与生存相关（报告的风险比为2.36-10.82），但在转移到独立队列时区分度下降（内部C指数为0.677，而一个外部队列中为0.529）。15项研究在外部表观基因组队列上验证了模型，其中1项为前瞻性验证。总体偏倚风险在21项研究中为高，8项为不清楚，1项为低，主要由分析领域驱动（样本量小、缺乏校准、可能存在数据泄漏）。3项研究报告了校准，11项提供了代码。<h4>结论：</h4> 表观基因组AI模型以高报告准确率分类白血病亚型，并能提供当日诊断，基于甲基化的评分增加了预后信息。然而，证据主要由回顾性、高偏倚风险的发展研究主导，外部验证有限，几乎没有前瞻性或影响评估。在临床采用之前，需要进行前瞻性、多中心验证，并采用校准良好、透明报告的模型。
