TransBind2: Improving Transcription Factor-DNA Binding Prediction with Multimodal Data and Bidirectional Cross Attention

Basnet S; Cheng J
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-14 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文提出融合DNA序列、染色质可及性与TF结构的双向交叉注意力模型TransBind2，用于预测转录因子-DNA结合，虽非直接建模染色质三维架构，但其整合染色质上下文与蛋白结构的思路对染色质水平调控建模有方法学启发。

摘要

Accurate genome-wide prediction of transcription factor (TF)-DNA binding remains challenging because many models focus mainly on DNA sequence and overlook chromatin context and TF structure. We previously developed TransBind, a protein-aware model that combines TF and DNA representations through cross-attention. Here, we introduce TransBind2, which improves on TransBind in several ways. It incorporates DNase-seq accessibility and genome mappability tracks as additional input, uses a biomodal protein language model (ProstT5) to capture both TF sequence and structure, and applies bidirectional cross-attention so DNA and protein features can refine each other. We also frame prediction as binary classification of individual <DNA bin, TF, cell type> triplets, allowing the model to generalize to new TFs and cell types. Across 690 human ChIP-seq experiments covering 161 TFs and 91 cell types, TransBind2 achieves a macro AUROC of 0.9648 and AUPR of 0.4215, outperforming TransBind and other baselines, with a ≥12.67% relative AUPR gain. The model trained on human data also performs well in cross-species zero-shot prediction on mouse data. Saliency analysis shows that it can identify TF-binding peaks with a median error of 12-38 base pairs (bps) despite being trained on window-level labels. Ablation studies further show that TF structure, chromatin accessibility, and bidirectional attention each improve performance. Overall, these results show that combining TF structure with chromatin context leads to more accurate and generalizable TF-DNA binding predictions.

摘要翻译

准确的基因组范围转录因子（TF）-DNA结合预测仍然具有挑战性，因为许多模型主要关注DNA序列，而忽视了染色质环境和TF结构。我们先前开发了TransBind，一种通过交叉注意力结合TF和DNA表征的蛋白质感知模型。在此，我们介绍TransBind2，它在多个方面对TransBind进行了改进。它整合了DNase-seq可及性和基因组可映射性轨道作为额外输入，使用双峰蛋白质语言模型（ProstT5）来捕获TF序列和结构，并应用双向交叉注意力，使DNA和蛋白质特征能够相互优化。我们还将预测框架化为对单个<DNA片段，TF，细胞类型>三元组的二元分类，使模型能够泛化到新的TF和细胞类型。在覆盖161种TF和91种细胞类型的690个人类ChIP-seq实验中，TransBind2实现了宏AUROC为0.9648和AUPR为0.4215，优于TransBind和其他基线模型，相对AUPR增益≥12.67%。在人类数据上训练的模型在小鼠数据的跨物种零样本预测中也表现良好。显著性分析显示，尽管仅在窗口级标签上训练，它仍能以12-38个碱基对（bps）的中位误差识别TF结合峰。消融研究进一步表明，TF结构、染色质可及性和双向注意力各自都能提升性能。总体而言，这些结果表明，将TF结构与染色质环境相结合，能够实现更准确且更具泛化性的TF-DNA结合预测。

02 一句话总结

该文提出 TransBind2，通过引入 DNase-seq 可及性、基因组 mappability 和 ProstT5 蛋白质语言模型（同时编码 TF 序列与结构），并采用双向交叉注意力机制，在 690 个人类 ChIP-seq 实验上将 TF-DNA 结合预测的宏 AUPR 提升至 0.4215（相对 TransBind 提升 ≥12.67%），且可零样本迁移至小鼠数据。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何利用多模态数据（DNA 序列、染色质可及性、mappability、TF 序列与结构）提高 TF-DNA 结合预测的准确性和泛化能力？
  • 为什么重要：TF-DNA 结合是基因调控的核心环节；仅用 DNA 序列的模型忽略了染色质上下文和 TF 结构信息，导致预测精度有限且难以泛化到新 TF 或细胞类型。
  • 现有方法为何不足：多数模型仅以 DNA 序列为输入，未利用染色质可及性（反映开放染色质区域）和 TF 蛋白结构；部分蛋白感知模型（如 TransBind）仅用单向交叉注意力，未让 DNA 与蛋白特征互相精炼。
  • 精确研究问题：Can incorporating chromatin accessibility, genome mappability, and TF structure via bidirectional cross-attention improve TF-DNA binding prediction accuracy and cross-species generalization?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：将 TF-DNA 结合预测建模为 <DNA bin, TF, cell type> 三元组的二分类问题；输入包括 DNA 序列、DNase-seq 可及性、mappability、TF 序列（经 ProstT5 编码为序列+结构表示）；通过双向交叉注意力融合 DNA 与蛋白特征。

  2. 核心洞察：TF 结合不仅由 DNA 序列决定，还受染色质可及性（DNase-seq）和基因组 mappability 影响；TF 蛋白结构（而非仅序列）对结合特异性有贡献；双向注意力使 DNA 与蛋白特征互相精炼，优于单向信息流。

  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在多模态生物序列预测中，将「上下文信号」（如可及性）与「对象结构」（如蛋白折叠）同时纳入，并允许模态间双向交互，可显著提升泛化能力。此思路可迁移至三维基因组中「序列-结构-功能」联合建模。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：DNA 序列（bin）、DNase-seq 可及性 track、基因组 mappability track、TF 蛋白序列

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：TF-DNA 结合预测，限定于 ChIP-seq 标签定义的结合事件；不涉及三维基因组结构或物理模拟。
  • oracle/真值输入：使用 ChIP-seq 实验标签作为真值；DNase-seq 和 mappability 作为输入特征，非 oracle。
  • 端到端状态：端到端监督学习，从序列+track+蛋白序列到结合概率。
  • 算力成本：未提供。
  • 历史数据依赖：依赖 ChIP-seq 标签质量；DNase-seq 和 mappability 来自参考基因组。
  • 模型依赖：依赖 ProstT5 的蛋白表示质量；未与其他蛋白编码器对比。
  • 最难情形：跨物种零样本（人类→小鼠）表现未量化；新 TF 或未见细胞类型的泛化程度未单独评估。
  • 群体/领域边界：仅人类和小鼠；未涉及其他物种或疾病相关变异。
  • 不确定性：AUPR 0.4215 表明正类预测仍有大量假阳性；定位误差 12-38 bp 为碱基级但非单碱基精度。

有边界复述：TransBind2 在 690 个人类 ChIP-seq 实验上，通过引入染色质可及性、mappability 和 TF 结构信息，将宏 AUPR 提升至 0.4215（相对提升 ≥12.67%），并能在小鼠上零样本预测；但其精度仍有限，且未涉及三维基因组结构。

Agent 提炼的知识候选

  1. 多模态上下文输入：将 DNase-seq 可及性和 mappability 作为辅助输入，可显著提升结合预测精度。→ 可迁移至三维基因组：将 Hi-C 接触频率、CTCF/cohesin 结合等作为辅助输入，预测增强子-启动子互作或 TAD 边界。
  2. 蛋白质语言模型编码结构：ProstT5 同时编码 TF 序列与结构，优于纯序列编码。→ 可迁移至三维基因组：用结构感知的蛋白表示预测染色质相关蛋白（如 CTCF、cohesin）的 DNA 结合特异性。
  3. 双向交叉注意力：DNA 与蛋白特征互相精炼优于单向信息流。→ 可迁移至三维基因组：在「序列-结构-功能」联合建模中，允许序列特征与结构特征双向交互。
  4. 三元组分类框架：将预测建模为 <DNA bin, TF, cell type> 分类，支持新 TF/细胞类型泛化。→ 可迁移至三维基因组：建模 <enhancer, promoter, cell type> 或 <locus, protein, cell type> 三元组。
  5. 窗口级标签+saliency 定位：即使训练标签粗糙，saliency 分析仍可提供碱基级定位。→ 可迁移至三维基因组：用 Hi-C 窗口级标签训练，saliency 分析定位关键接触位点。

  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors present TransBind2, a deep learning model for transcription factor (TF)-DNA binding prediction that integrates DNA sequence, DNase-seq accessibility, genome mappability, and TF sequence and structure via a protein language model (ProstT5) and bidirectional cross-attention. The model is framed as binary classification of triplets. Reported performance includes macro AUROC of 0.9648 and AUPR of 0.4215 across 690 human ChIP-seq experiments (161 TFs, 91 cell types), a ≥12.67% relative AUPR improvement over baselines, cross-species zero-shot transfer to mouse, saliency-based peak identification with median error of 12-38 bp, and ablation support for each added component.

  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full manuscript, methods section, all figures and tables, supplementary information, baseline model specifications, dataset construction details, hyperparameter settings, and code availability

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract describes a technically plausible and potentially valuable extension of a prior model, with a clear motivation rooted in the limitations of sequence-only approaches. The reported performance gains are substantial, and the inclusion of chromatin context and TF structure is well aligned with current directions in regulatory genomics. However, the abstract alone does not permit verification of the core claims. Critical details regarding data partitioning, baseline fairness, class imbalance handling, and statistical robustness are absent. The cross-species and saliency claims are intriguing but cannot be evaluated without methodological transparency. The work is likely of interest to the computational genomics community, but the evidence base provided is insufficient to establish the case at the level expected for a high-impact venue.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational biologists and machine learning researchers working on regulatory genomics, TF binding site prediction, and multimodal integration. The cross-species generalization claim would also interest researchers studying model transfer across organisms. The saliency-based peak identification result may appeal to those focused on interpretability in genomic deep learning.
  • Major strengths The model design is well motivated and integrates multiple biologically relevant data modalities. The framing as triplet classification is a sensible approach to enable generalization to unseen TFs and cell types. The reported performance improvements over prior work are meaningful, particularly the AUPR gain. The inclusion of cross-species zero-shot evaluation and saliency-based localization adds breadth to the evaluation.

  • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract reports aggregate performance metrics but provides no information on how the 690 ChIP-seq experiments were split into training, validation, and test sets. If the test set includes experiments from TFs or cell types seen during training, the generalization claim is weakened.
