Prediction of pH-sensing histidine residues in human cells

Sallah S; Warwicker J
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-20 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文预测人类蛋白质组中pH感应组氨酸残基，虽涉及转录调控和染色质相关复合物（如cohesin），但核心是氨基酸序列与结构分析，无三维基因组/染色质组织建模，也无AI或分子模拟方法，与本课题关联微弱。

摘要

Changes in pH play an important role in human physiology and cell behaviours, evidenced by studies of tissues and gene expression, and the biophysics of individual systems. Bridging between these scales to establish pH-sensing networks has only been partially explored. Here, amino acid conservation and structural context, and benchmarking with known pH-sensors, were used to predict histidine residues within the human proteome that could modulate pH-dependence. Proteins containing these histidines are enriched in many categories, most notably nucleic acid-binding and transcriptional regulation. DNA phosphate backbone interactions are proposed to play a major role in modulating some pH-sensors. Transcription factors with potential pH-sensors and the largest number of documented target genes are highlighted, including those involved in circadian and developmental processes. Certain CDK-cyclin pairs also feature, along with components of SWI/SNF and cohesin complexes. Focussing on histidines, including those that are solvent accessible in AlphaFold protomer models, this study generates benchmarked predictions for possible pH-sensors in the human proteome.

摘要翻译

pH变化在人体生理和细胞行为中扮演重要角色，这一点已通过组织和基因表达研究以及单个系统的生物物理学得到证实。然而，在这些尺度之间建立pH感应网络的桥梁仅被部分探索。本研究利用氨基酸保守性和结构背景，并与已知pH传感器进行基准测试，预测人类蛋白质组中可能调节pH依赖性的组氨酸残基。含有这些组氨酸的蛋白质在多个类别中富集，尤其是核酸结合和转录调控。DNA磷酸骨架相互作用被认为在调节某些pH传感器中发挥重要作用。具有潜在pH传感器且记录靶基因数量最多的转录因子被突出显示，包括参与昼夜节律和发育过程的那些。某些CDK-周期蛋白对也出现在其中，以及SWI/SNF和黏连蛋白复合物的组分。聚焦于组氨酸，包括AlphaFold原聚体模型中溶剂可及的组氨酸，本研究为人类蛋白质组中可能的pH传感器生成了基准预测。
