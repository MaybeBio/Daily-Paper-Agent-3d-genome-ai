DNA-LLM2Vec: Architecture-Aware Task-Conditioned Adaptation of Decoder DNA Foundation Models

Liang Y; Zhai K; Gan Y; Li S; Shao J; Watson GH; Poesio M
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-10-14 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文提出DNA-LLM2Vec，将解码器DNA基础模型转换为双向编码器以增强长程基因组任务（如增强子-启动子互作）的表示，虽涉及染色质水平调控预测但未直接建模三维空间结构，方法上属AI基因组语言模型，对三维基因组调控特征建模有参考价值。

摘要

Decoder DNA foundation models pretrain with dense next-token prediction and scale to long sequences, but their representations are directional. This is a poor fit for genomic tasks that need sequence context both upstream and downstream of a locus. Bidirectional masked-language-model encoders supply this context, but they pretrain more sparsely and scale less easily to long windows. We introduce DNA-LLM2Vec, a staged decoder-to-encoder framework that opens bidirectional context, repairs the resulting mismatch with masked next-token prediction, and applies contrastive views encoding strand and positional invariance. We instantiate it on two architecturally distinct backbones, DNAGPT and HyenaDNA, and evaluate seven biological task families (grouped from GB, NT, GUE, and GUE+EPI) with frozen linear probing. The benefits of conversion are architecture-aware and task-conditioned rather than uniform. Crop-based positional contrastive learning helps most for the extended-context families (enhancer-promoter interaction and Virus-COVID), raising frozen Avg-EPI MCC to 27.5% for DNAGPT and 26.7% for HyenaDNA, whereas for shorter, local families the results depend on backbone and tokenisation. Beyond classification, bidirectional context and MNTP improve causal-eQTL variant-effect prediction specifically at distance, and a base-pair mutagenesis diagnostic shows that adaptation organises motif-aware contact structure while reverse-complement training imprints strand-consistent geometry, yet neither predicts the best downstream view. DNA-LLM2Vec therefore produces frozen bidirectional representations whose most effective adaptation view depends on the task structure and the backbone. Code and checkpoints are available at https://github.com/semi-nothing/dna-llm2vec.

摘要翻译

解码器DNA基础模型通过密集的下一词元预测进行预训练，并能扩展到长序列，但其表示具有方向性。这对于需要基因座上下游序列上下文的基因组任务来说并不适合。双向掩码语言模型编码器提供了这种上下文，但它们的预训练较为稀疏，且难以扩展到长窗口。我们引入了DNA-LLM2Vec，一个分阶段的解码器到编码器框架，它打开双向上下文，通过掩码下一词元预测修复由此产生的不匹配，并应用编码链和位置不变性的对比视图。我们在两个架构不同的骨干模型DNAGPT和HyenaDNA上实例化它，并通过冻结线性探针评估七个生物任务家族（从GB、NT、GUE和GUE+EPI分组）。转换的益处是架构感知和任务条件化的，而非统一的。基于裁剪的位置对比学习对扩展上下文家族（增强子-启动子相互作用和病毒-COVID）帮助最大，将DNAGPT的冻结平均EPI MCC提高到27.5%，HyenaDNA提高到26.7%，而对于较短、局部的家族，结果取决于骨干模型和词元化。除了分类之外，双向上下文和MNTP特别在距离上改善了因果eQTL变异效应预测，而碱基对诱变诊断显示，适应组织出基序感知的接触结构，而反向互补训练印刻出链一致的几何结构，但两者都不能预测最佳的下游视图。因此，DNA-LLM2Vec产生冻结的双向表示，其最有效的适应视图取决于任务结构和骨干模型。代码和检查点可在https://github.com/semi-nothing/dna-llm2vec获取。

02 一句话总结

本文提出 DNA-LLM2Vec，一种将 decoder-only DNA 基础模型分阶段转换为双向 encoder 的适配框架，通过 masked next-token prediction（MNTP）修复方向性失配、并用 crop-based 对比学习注入位置与链（strand）不变性，在七个基因组任务族上以 frozen linear probing 评估，发现转换收益是架构感知且任务条件化的，而非均匀提升。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：Decoder-only DNA foundation models 的表示是方向性的（directional），只依赖上游上下文，这对需要同时利用位点上下游序列信息的基因组任务（如增强子-启动子互作、变异效应预测）是次优的。如何在不重新预训练的前提下，将 decoder 模型转换为具备双向上下文的 encoder 表示？
  • 为什么重要：DNA 序列的功能解读本质上是双向的——调控元件的作用依赖于两侧的序列环境。若基础模型表示方向性过强，下游任务（尤其长程调控任务）将受限于上下文覆盖不全。
  • 现有方法不足：双向 masked-language-model encoders（如 Nucleotide Transformer）虽提供双向上下文，但预训练更稀疏（masked token 比例低），且难以扩展到长序列窗口；decoder 模型虽可长序列预训练，但表示方向性不匹配任务需求。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a staged decoder-to-encoder conversion with masked next-token prediction and contrastive views produce frozen bidirectional representations that consistently outperform the original decoder across diverse genomic task families?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：三阶段转换——(1) 将 decoder 的 causal attention 改为 bidirectional attention；(2) 用 masked next-token prediction（MNTP）在双向上下文中继续训练，修复方向性失配；(3) 用 crop-based 对比学习注入位置与链（strand）不变性。在七个任务族上以 frozen linear probing 评估。

  2. 核心洞察：转换收益不是「越多越好」或「普遍有效」，而是由任务结构（是否需要长程双向上下文）与骨干架构（tokenisation、序列长度处理方式）共同决定。crop-based 对比学习对长程任务（EPI、Virus-COVID）帮助最大，而对短程局部任务则依赖骨干。

  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在基础模型适配中，「架构-任务匹配度」比「模型容量」更重要。对于三维基因组任务（如 Hi-C 环、TAD 边界预测），若任务本质需要双向长程上下文，则 decoder 模型需显式转换；反之，若任务以局部序列特征为主，转换可能无益甚至有害。这提示三维基因组领域的模型选择应基于任务上下文需求而非模型规模。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：DNA 序列（长度因任务族而异，最长覆盖 Virus-COVID 与 EPI 的长窗口）
  • 输出：frozen 双向表示（用于 linear probing 分类或回归）
  • 模块： 1. Bidirectional attention conversion：将 decoder 的 causal mask 替换为 bidirectional mask 2. Masked next-token prediction (MNTP)：在双向上下文中预测被 mask 的 token，修复方向性失配 3. Crop-based contrastive learning：对同一序列的不同 crop 构造正样本对，编码位置不变性；对 reverse-complement 构造链不变性视图
  • 训练：分阶段进行，先 MNTP 再对比学习（staged）
  • 骨干：DNAGPT（GPT 风格）与 HyenaDNA（Hyena 架构，长序列高效）
  • 评估：frozen linear probing，七个任务族（GB、NT、GUE、GUE+EPI 分组）
  • 工具：未提供具体框架（如 PyTorch 版本等）
  • 假设：双向上下文 + 位置/链不变性可提升下游任务泛化；转换收益与任务结构相关
  • 流程：原始 decoder 权重 → 双向 attention 转换 → MNTP 训练 → 对比学习 → frozen 表示 → linear probing 评估

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：本文结论限于 frozen linear probing 评估，不涉及微调（fine-tuning）场景。若下游任务使用微调，转换收益可能不同。
  • oracle/真值输入：EPI 与 eQTL 任务依赖实验验证的互作/效应标签，标签质量决定评估上限。
  • 端到端状态：本文为表示学习 + 线性探测，非端到端任务模型。最终任务性能受分类头容量限制。
  • 算力成本：未提供训练成本数据。MNTP + 对比学习需额外训练阶段，成本高于直接使用原始 decoder。
  • 历史数据依赖：预训练权重（DNAGPT、HyenaDNA）来自各自原始预训练，转换过程依赖这些权重的质量。
  • 模型依赖：结论基于两个骨干（DNAGPT、HyenaDNA），不能外推至所有 decoder DNA 模型（如 Evo、Caduceus 等）。
  • 最难情形：短程局部任务（如启动子二分类）上转换收益不稳定，甚至可能有害。
  • 不确定性：mutagenesis 诊断显示表示结构变化与下游最优视图无稳定对应关系，说明「结构改善」不等于「任务改善」。
  • 群体/领域边界：结论限于基因组序列分类与变异效应预测，不涉及三维基因组结构直接预测（如 Hi-C 接触图）。
  • 有边界的复述：DNA-LLM2Vec 在 frozen 设置下，对需要长程双向上下文的任务族（EPI、Virus-COVID）带来稳定提升，对短程任务则骨干依赖；其表示结构变化（motif 感知、链一致几何）可观测，但不直接预测下游最优视图。

14 Agent 提炼的知识候选

Agent 提炼的知识候选（面向三维基因组/染色质组织 × AI/分子模拟）：

  1. Decoder→Encoder 转换策略可迁移至三维基因组任务：若三维基因组预测模型（如基于序列的 EPI/Hi-C 预测器）使用 decoder 骨干，可借鉴本文三阶段转换（双向 attention + MNTP + 对比学习）获得双向上下文表示。迁移路径：将 EPI 任务替换为 Hi-C 接触预测或 TAD 边界识别。

  2. Crop-based 对比学习适用于长程调控任务：三维基因组中增强子-启动子互作本质是长程（可达 Mb 级），crop 策略可编码「位置不变性」——即互作不依赖绝对基因组坐标。可迁移至：从序列预测 Hi-C 环时，对不同基因组窗口的 crop 构造正样本对。

  3. Reverse-complement 对比学习编码链不变性：三维基因组结构（如环、TAD）不依赖 DNA 链方向，链不变表示可提升跨链泛化。可迁移至：Hi-C 数据增强时利用 reverse-complement 对称性。

  4. 任务条件化评估框架：本文的「按任务族分层评估」设计可迁移至三维基因组领域——不同结构任务（环、TAD、compartment）对上下文需求不同，应分别评估而非聚合。

  5. Frozen linear probing 作为表示质量评估协议：在三维基因组领域，可用 frozen 表示 + 线性头快速评估预训练模型质量，避免微调成本。

  6. Mutagenesis 诊断方法：碱基突变扫描可揭示模型是否学习到 motif 感知结构，可迁移至三维基因组模型的可解释性分析（如 CTCF motif 对环预测的贡献）。

Review setup

  • Input scope Abstract only
  • Assessment boundary Claims and evidence as presented in the abstract; no figures, tables, methods, or supplementary materials were provided
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors propose DNA-LLM2Vec, a staged decoder-to-encoder adaptation framework for DNA foundation models, which introduces bidirectional context, masked next-token prediction (MNTP), and contrastive views encoding strand and positional invariance. They instantiate it on DNAGPT and HyenaDNA, evaluate seven biological task families with frozen linear probing, and report architecture-aware and task-conditioned benefits, including improved enhancer-promoter interaction and Virus-COVID performance, improved causal-eQTL variant-effect prediction at distance, and motif-aware contact structure with strand-consistent geometry.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract text only; no quantitative results beyond two MCC values (27.5% for DNAGPT, 26.7% for HyenaDNA), no baselines, no statistical significance, no method details, no figure or table references
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full manuscript, methods section, all figures and tables, baseline comparisons, hyperparameter details, dataset splits, evaluation protocols, code verification, and any supplementary information

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting and timely contribution to the adaptation of decoder DNA foundation models for bidirectional genomic tasks. The core idea of staged conversion with task-conditioned benefits is plausible and aligns with current trends in genomic representation learning. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient evidence to evaluate the technical soundness, the magnitude of improvements, or the generalizability of the claims. The reported results are sparse, lack baseline context, and do not include statistical validation. The claim that benefits are "architecture-aware and task-conditioned" is intriguing but not substantiated with comparative data. The manuscript may have merit, but the case is not established from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational biologists and machine learning researchers working on genomic foundation models, particularly those interested in adapting large language model architectures for regulatory genomics, variant effect prediction, and sequence-to-function tasks. The work could also interest researchers developing bidirectional encoders for long-context biological sequences and those studying the trade-offs between decoder and encoder pretraining paradigms.
  • Major strengths The conceptual framing is clear and addresses a recognized limitation of decoder-only models for genomic tasks requiring bidirectional context. The staged adaptation approach (opening bidirectional context, repairing with MNTP, then contrastive views) is methodologically coherent. The choice of two architecturally distinct backbones (DNAGPT and HyenaDNA) strengthens the claim of architecture-awareness. The inclusion of causal-eQTL variant-effect prediction and base-pair mutagenesis diagnostics suggests a broader evaluation beyond classification benchmarks.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer The abstract claims that DNA-LLM2Vec "raises frozen Avg-EPI MCC to 27.5% for DNAGPT and 26.7% for HyenaDNA" for extended-context families.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no figure or table reference provided
  • Concern The reported MCC values are presented without any baseline comparison. It is unclear what the MCC was before adaptation, what the performance of the original decoder models was, or what the performance of existing bidirectional encoders is on the same tasks. Without baselines, the magnitude of improvement cannot be assessed.
  • Why it matters The central claim of the paper is that conversion improves performance. Without a baseline, the reader cannot determine whether the reported values represent a meaningful advance or are within the range of existing methods. This undermines the scientific contribution.
  • Resolution test Provide baseline MCC values for the unadapted decoder models, for standard bidirectional encoders (e.g., DNABERT, Nucleotide Transformer), and ideally for a simple linear probe on one-hot encoded sequences, all on identical task splits and evaluation protocols.

  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no method details or results tables provided
  • Concern The abstract does not specify what "crop-based positional contrastive learning" entails, how the contrastive views are constructed, what the loss function is, or how the "best downstream view" is selected.
