Dynamical Regimes in Rejuvenation

Ciarchi M; Rulands S
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-01 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过场论和分子动力学模拟研究表观遗传修饰与染色质三维构象的耦合动力学，揭示重编程中染色质组织介导的表观遗传可逆重塑机制，直接契合染色质空间架构的计算建模与调控机理。

摘要

Biological aging is accompanied by systematic changes in epigenetic modifications and chromatin organization. The reversal of the effects of aging, rejuvenation, is experimentally achieved by the transient induction of factors that modify these marks in cells and organisms. Here, we show that key features of rejuvenation experiments emerge from the biophysical interplay between dynamic epigenetic marks and the three-dimensional conformation of chromatin. Using a minimal field theory and molecular dynamics simulations, we show that the system responds in three distinct temporal regimes. The intermediary regime fulfills necessary conditions for successful rejuvenation. In this regime, the system spends time near a separatrix, allowing for high epigenetic plasticity, while memory retained in the chromatin conformation enables restoration of the original epigenetic correlations. Analysis of sequencing data further supports the predicted coupling between chromatin compaction and epigenetic correlations. Our results provide a physical explanation for how rejuvenation may remodel age-associated epigenetic states without irreversibly erasing cellular identity. We identify a general mechanism by which memory stored in a slow structural variable permits reversible remodeling of a faster internal state.

摘要翻译

生物衰老伴随着表观遗传修饰和染色质组织的系统性变化。通过短暂诱导细胞和生物体中修饰这些标记的因子，可在实验上实现衰老效应的逆转，即年轻化（rejuvenation）。在此，我们展示年轻化实验的关键特征源于动态表观遗传标记与染色质三维构象之间的生物物理相互作用。利用最小场论（minimal field theory）和分子动力学（molecular dynamics）模拟，我们证明系统在三个不同的时间尺度上响应。中间尺度满足成功年轻化的必要条件。在此尺度下，系统在分界线（separatrix）附近停留，从而实现高表观遗传可塑性，同时染色质构象中保留的记忆能够恢复原始表观遗传相关性。测序数据的分析进一步支持了所预测的染色质压缩与表观遗传相关性之间的耦合。我们的结果为年轻化如何重塑与衰老相关的表观遗传状态而不不可逆地抹除细胞身份提供了物理解释。我们识别出一种通用机制，即存储在慢速结构变量中的记忆允许对较快内部状态进行可逆重塑。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：使用最小场论（field theory）描述表观遗传标记（快变量）与染色质三维构象（慢变量）的耦合，并用分子动力学（MD）模拟验证。
  2. 核心洞察：系统存在三个动力学区间——快变量主导（高可塑性但无记忆）、慢变量主导（低可塑性但有记忆）、中间区间（靠近separatrix，高可塑性+记忆保留）。rejuvenation成功的关键是系统在中间区间运行，利用染色质构象作为记忆载体，允许表观遗传状态可逆重置。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：任何由快慢变量耦合的系统（如基因表达与染色质状态、神经网络权重与激活）都可能存在类似动力学区间，其中慢变量作为记忆载体允许快变量可逆重置。这为设计可逆状态转换系统（如细胞重编程、AI模型微调）提供了物理原理。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a conceptually appealing biophysical framework for rejuvenation, linking epigenetic dynamics to chromatin conformation. The idea of a "separatrix" regime that permits plasticity while preserving memory is elegant and potentially unifying. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient detail to evaluate the rigor of the theoretical model, the robustness of the simulation results, or the strength of the experimental validation. The central claim of three distinct regimes and the specific role of the intermediate regime remain largely qualitative at this stage.

  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in aging biology, epigenetics, chromatin biophysics, and systems biology. The work offers a physical mechanism that could explain how transient reprogramming (e.g., OSKM induction) can reverse epigenetic aging without erasing cell identity, which is a major open question in the field. The general principle of memory stored in a slow structural variable may also interest a broader audience studying cellular memory and state transitions.

  • Major strengths

    • Proposes a novel, physically grounded mechanism for rejuvenation that goes beyond descriptive epigenetic clocks.
    • Identifies a specific dynamical regime (intermediate, near separatrix) as the key to successful rejuvenation, which is a testable prediction.
    • Bridges two usually separate fields: epigenetic dynamics and chromatin 3D organization.
    • The general principle (slow structural variable enabling reversible remodeling of a fast state) has potential cross-disciplinary relevance.
